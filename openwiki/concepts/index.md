# Files

- [Cupping Scores & SCA Scoring](cupping-scores.md) - Domain guide to cupping scores, the SCA 100-point specialty scale, what each score range means for quality, and how Kissaten captures, constrains, and surfaces the cupping_score field across its bean, roaster, and search layers.
- [Decaffeination](decaffeination.md) - How Kissaten models decaffeination (the is_decaf boolean), the major decaffeination methods, why decaf matters for accessibility, and how the search filter surfaces it end to end.
- [Origin Geography & the Coffee Belt](origin-geography.md) - Domain guide to coffee origin geography — the coffee belt, the country→region→farm hierarchy, elevation effects, canonical region grouping, geocoding, and the /v1/origins API and frontend origins route.
- [Price Transparency Fields](price-transparency.md) - Domain guide to coffee price transparency — FOB price, farm gate price, price paid to producer, and how the Bean model, API, and FX pipeline surface green-coffee pricing for ethical sourcing.
- [Coffee Processing Methods](processing-methods.md) - Domain guide to coffee processing methods (washed, natural, honey, anaerobic, wet-hulling, etc.), why each matters for flavour, and how Kissaten captures free-text process names, canonicalises them via the ProcessCategorizer AI and processing_methods_mappings.json, and exposes them through the /v1/processes API and frontend route.
- [Roast Levels & Roast Profiles](roast-levels-profiles.md) - Domain guide to roast levels (Extra-Light through Dark) and roast profiles (Espresso, Filter, Omni, Both), what they mean for flavour, and how Kissaten models and filters them.
- [Tasting Note Taxonomy & Flavour Lexicon](tasting-note-taxonomy.md) - How Kissaten models coffee tasting notes as a normalized string array, categorizes them post-hoc into a three-tier SCA-derived flavour hierarchy, and exposes them through AI modules, API endpoints, and interactive frontend charts.
- [Coffee Varietals](varietals.md) - Domain guide to coffee varietals (Bourbon, Gesha, Catuai, Typica, Caturra, etc.), their genetic lineages and flavour characteristics, and how Kissaten captures, canonicalises, and serves them through the variety schema field, WCR reference data, AI varietal categoriser, and /v1/varietals API.
