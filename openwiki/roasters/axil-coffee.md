# Axil Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Axil Coffee was founded by husband-and-wife team Dave Makin and Zoe Delaney, beginning with a single Hawthorn cafe and growing to more than 16 locations across Melbourne plus an interstate location. The company sells blends, single origins, Signature Series and Barista Champion coffees, as well as instant coffee, pods, and drip bags. Axil says its online coffee is freshly roasted and packed at its Hawthorn roastery.

## Address

- 48 Lynch Street, Hawthorn VIC 3122 - Australia

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Axil says its roasting team travels to coffee farms each year to build and maintain relationships, including relationships with some farmers lasting more than 13 years. It describes daily cuppings and checks as part of quality control; no FOB or producer-paid figures are published on the pages checked.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Axil offers free shipping over AUD 75. Orders are dispatched Monday through Friday and leave the roastery or warehouse within two business days; orders placed after 12:00 on Friday are dispatched the following Monday. International orders use DHL International Express, with prices shown at checkout.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Axil's stated mission is to make exceptional coffee accessible to the community. Its distinctive record is a competition focus: the About page says the group is home to two World Barista Championship wins and seven Australian Barista Championship wins.
