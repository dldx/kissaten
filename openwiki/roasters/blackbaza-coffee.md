# Black Baza Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Black Baza Coffee is an Indian specialty coffee company roasting small batches at its Bangalore roastery. Its Shopify shop sells Indian coffees, subscriptions, gear and Field & Forest products, and frames the business around equity, biodiversity and long-term relationships with growers. The site says it received the Specialty Coffee Association's 2025 Sustainability Award.

## Address

- Bangalore, India — full roastery address not published on the consulted site pages.

## Sustainability

- Black Baza says its coffees are grown under native shade trees, without chemicals, in ecosystems that support birds and other wildlife. Its biodiversity-friendly model is community-led: producers define what biodiversity-friendly farming means and monitor it themselves. The company also links this approach to long-term ecological resilience rather than relying only on global certifications.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Black Baza says it works directly with smallholders, cooperatives and farmer associations. It publishes a pricing claim of 1.2–1.4 times the market rate, plus long-term partnerships and guaranteed buy-backs. It also says it provides regenerative pest-and-disease-management training to help improve soil health and farm resilience.

## Roasting & Equipment

- The company says every batch is roasted in small batches at its Bangalore roastery. No roaster brand, model or capacity is published on the consulted pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The “1000 Year Brew” framing connects coffee, livelihoods and biodiversity across a long time horizon. Black Baza describes its aim as enabling producers and consumers to understand the impact of coffee choices and to support farming systems in which people and nature grow together.
