# Nord Kaffe — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Nord Kaffe AS started in 2011 with a small delicatessen and restaurant on Bjølsen in Oslo and now roasts in a dedicated roastery at Tveita. The site brands itself "Norð Kaffe / Nord Kaffe" with the tagline "Special coffee for you, my friend" and positions the range as "Ferskbrent kvalitetskaffe i lys, nordisk brennestil" (freshly roasted quality coffee in a light Nordic roast style). Per their FAQ they "driver med kaffe som historisk kan regnes for å være lysbrent" and add "Vi er ikke glade i brentsmak eller mye bitterhet" (they are not fond of roast flavour or much bitterness). The range is predominantly single-origin farm and washing-station coffees from Brazil, Kenya, Ethiopia, Costa Rica, Honduras, Mexico, Burundi and Rwanda, plus a subscription line and a little brewing equipment, with the coffee shop split into filter and espresso sub-categories.

## Address

- Nord Kaffe AS, Tvetenveien 162, 0671 Oslo, Norway — described by the site as "Hovedkontor og kaffebrenneriet" (head office and the roastery).

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Traceability commitment: "All kaffen vi kjøper er sporbar ned til hva bonden eller den lokale vaskestasjonen har fått betalt for kaffen."
- Sourcing aim: "Målet vårt er å finne skikkelig god kaffe som vi kan importere fra bøndene med færrest mulig mellomledd."
- Per their FAQ, for the last six years they have bought directly from washing stations and farms in Brazil, Kenya and Ethiopia.
- Concrete producer payment figure on the Colombia Condor product page: they secure direct and fair payment "18-20 % over markedsrenten, som tilsvarer $350 000 COP per last, med 50 % betalt direkte til kvinnenes konto" (18–20% over the market rate, i.e. $350,000 COP per shipment, with 50% paid directly into the women's account).

## Roasting & Equipment

- "Vi har et topp moderne kaffebrenneri på Tveita i Oslo hvor vi brener kaffen fersk hver uke" — the coffee is roasted fresh every week at the Tveita roastery.
- No roasting-machine brand or model is published on the site.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Founded 2011, with the first location on Bjølsen.
- Head roaster Alexander Monsen was Norwegian champion at the Barista Championship 2022 ("Vår kaffebrenner Alexander Monsen ble norgesmester i Barista Championship 2022!"); the site also carries a follow-up article "Norgesmester igjen!" and mentions participation in the World Barista Championship and a sponsored trip to Melbourne. The year of the second win is not clearly stated.
- The brand renders as both "Norð Kaffe" and "Nord Kaffe" on the site.

## Scraping Quirks

- Grind names the coffee categories here: `Filter` and `Espresso` are coffee *category* slugs, not grind options, so any slug blocklist that excludes "filter" or "espresso" will silently delete the entire coffee catalogue. Equipment lives under `Utstyr` and subscriptions under `Abonnement` instead.
- Consumer prices include all taxes and surcharges ("Den oppgitte prisen ... inkluderer alle avgifter og tilleggskostnader") while the business terms are exclusive of VAT. Don't apply business pricing rules to consumer prices.
- Subscription products (250 g / 500 g / 1 kg) are a separate line rather than fixed-origin coffees. No tasting-kit or sampler product was present on the site at the time of writing.
