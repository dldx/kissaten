# Ilse — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Ilse is a US specialty coffee roaster founded in 2019 by Rebecca and Lucas,
with its cafe and roastery in North Canaan, Connecticut. The Shopify shop
centers on seasonal single-producer coffees and separates its offering into
Primary, Select and planned Commitment lines.

## Address

- 35 Railroad Street, North Canaan, CT 06018, United States.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Ilse says it builds long-term partnerships with smallholder producers and
  invests in mutual growth. Its Commitment line is described as larger-volume
  lots from growing partners, paid at a premium above market price; the page
  does not publish the premium amount or per-lot prices.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Free domestic US shipping is advertised on orders of $50 or more.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper reads `all-products` through Shopify's products JSON endpoint,
  strips the collection segment from product URLs, and excludes subscriptions,
  gift cards, merchandise and equipment.
