# Original Green Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Original Green Roasters is a specialty coffee importer, roaster and café
business in Santiago, Chile. Its Spanish-language shop lists coffees by origin,
including Colombia, Sumatra, Ethiopia, Kenya and Rwanda, alongside chocolate,
accessories and café equipment. The storefront is powered by Jumpseller.

## Address

- Santiago, Chile — full roastery address not published on the consulted site
  pages. The site separately mentions a Parque Bustamante café at Rancagua 042,
  Providencia; that café address is not treated as the roastery address.

## Sustainability

- OGR says its operation uses solar energy and has Sello Sol energy-traceability
  certification. It also says its roasting equipment uses technology certified
  to European standards for reducing gas emissions.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- OGR says it buys green coffee from different origins with traceability as a
  guiding criterion. Its public pages do not state producer-paid prices,
  volumes or a cost breakdown.

## Schedules & Shipping

- OGR ships throughout Chile. Delivery timing depends on the external provider
  selected (Chilexpress, Correos de Chile or Starken, subject to availability).
  No free-delivery minimum or published destination-specific rate was found on
  the consulted shipping page.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper visits two paginated `/cafe-tostado` URLs and only follows
  `a.product-image` links. It skips cards whose text says “No Disponible”; the
  current shop also exposes non-coffee items, while the scraper's exclusion list
  is intentionally empty, so future catalogue changes need review.
