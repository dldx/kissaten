# Heart Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Heart Coffee Roasters opened in Portland, Oregon, in 2009 and is owned and
operated by Rebekah and Wille Yli-Luoma. It has two retail cafes and a roasting
facility, and its Shopify shop focuses on seasonal single-origin coffee,
subscriptions and brewing equipment. Heart says its roast profiles aim for
sweetness, fruit tones and a juicy finish rather than a light/medium/dark label.

## Address

- Portland, Oregon, United States — full roastery address not published on the
  consulted site pages.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Heart says it visits farming partners during and after harvest and maintains
  direct relationships in Kenya, Ethiopia, Colombia, Honduras, Mexico, Brazil
  and Guatemala. Its 2022 transparency report says it negotiates most coffees
  directly with producers, has obtained FOB pricing for all coffees through
  selected importers and exporters, and introduced farm-gate prices for some
  producers in 2022. It says the amount paid to producers increases year over
  year; detailed figures are supplied as a downloadable PDF.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Heart ships freshly roasted coffee Monday through Friday. Orders by 12pm PST
  on weekdays ship the next day Monday through Thursday; orders from Thursday
  after 12pm PST through Sunday 12pm PST ship Monday. It does not ship on US
  federally recognised holidays. The site banner states free shipping after
  spending £75.00; no destination-specific rates are published on the consulted
  shipping page.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Every bag is tagged with a roast date and batch number. Heart says batches that
  fail its quality standards are donated to the local food bank, and that it
  does not compromise quality by over-roasting, under-roasting or baking coffee.

## Scraping Quirks

- Heart's Shopify collection can be geo/market detected as the UK by requests;
  the scraper pins `country=US` to obtain the intended US catalogue and USD
  prices, then canonicalizes collection product URLs to `/products/<handle>`.
