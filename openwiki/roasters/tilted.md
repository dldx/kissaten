# Tilted Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Tilted Coffee (tiltedcoffee.com) is an independent specialty coffee roaster
based in Nantwich, Cheshire, running a Squarespace storefront. It focuses
primarily on single-origin coffees, many developed with filter brewing in
mind but versatile enough for espresso. What began as curiosity tasting
bright coffees roasted in Denmark and Norway grew into a small-batch roastery
shaped by Scandinavian coffee culture. The catalogue holds 6 saved coffees
plus one tasting kit (the Cultivares Tasting Pack).

## Address

- Nantwich, Cheshire — United Kingdom (full street address not published on
  the site).

## Roasting & Equipment

- Coffee is roasted on a ROEST P3000, a precision, data-driven roaster used
  for clarity and consistency, with a gentle-development Nordic-influenced
  profile.

## Scraping Quirks

- Squarespace store (no products.json) — the scaffold for the profile follows
  the shared Squarespace scraper.
- The Cultivares Tasting Pack is a tasting-kit product: it must flow through
  the review queue (`is_tasting_kit` / `requires_review`), never excluded.
