# Rose Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Rose Coffee Roasters is a Zurich, Switzerland roaster founded by Matt Winton after
his 2021 World Brewers Cup win. Rose says it began roasting in Zurich in 2022 and
became independent in 2025. Its Shopify storefront divides the range into Daily
everyday coffees, Origins seasonal single origins and Obsessions rare lots and
Geishas, with coffee roasted and explained for home brewers and wholesale customers.

## Address

- Zurich, Switzerland — full roastery address not published on the reviewed site pages.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Rose says it personally tastes and selects coffees through relationships with
  trusted producers, exporters and importers. No FOB or farm-gate prices were found
  on the reviewed pages.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Rose roasts on Stronghold Roasters using renewable electricity. Matt Winton cups
  the coffees, works on the roasts and writes the recipes. The site does not publish
  the Stronghold model, capacity or a roasting software system.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Rose roasts every Monday. Orders placed by 23:59 Sunday (Zurich time) are included
  in the next roast and usually leave on Wednesday, occasionally Thursday. Swiss
  orders normally arrive 1-2 business days after dispatch; Swiss shipping is free
  over CHF 50 and for coffee subscriptions. International FedEx service normally
  takes around 5 business days, while postal services can take up to 20 business
  days; duties, taxes and handling fees are paid by the recipient.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Rose says it is roasting a little lighter for clear flavour and sweetness, while
  retaining enough development for everyday enjoyment. It presents easy espresso
  and rare competition lots in the same range.
