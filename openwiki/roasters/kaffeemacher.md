# kaffeemacher — Roaster Profile

## Overview

kaffeemacher is a Swiss coffee company with its café, academy, roastery, test centre and offices in Basel, plus a German site in Dinslaken. The German Shopify storefront sells filter, espresso, decaf and fully automatic coffees alongside equipment and courses. The company says it has been co-owner of Finca Santa Rita in Nicaragua since 2017.

## Address

- Otto-Lilienthal-Straße 13, 46539 Dinslaken, Germany — the German coffee-makers site identifies this as the location from which online coffee and accessories are dispatched; the Basel roastery street address was not published on the consulted page.

## Sustainability

- kaffeemacher describes long-term farm partnerships that address challenges, develop solutions and invest in infrastructure and quality. Its Toca project in Mexico is described as building resilient producers through long-term cooperation, infrastructure and quality improvement; its Apas partnership creates incentives and a growing market for organic coffee production in Serra do Mantiqueira, Brazil.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The company says its partnerships are developed over many years and that it is a co-owner of Finca Santa Rita. Its current site also publishes an article arguing that “Direct Trade” has no seal, definition or inspection body and says the company does not use the term as an assurance. No current per-lot price table was visible on the consulted pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Free shipping in Germany is advertised from €75. Dinslaken pickup is offered Tuesdays 10:00–12:00 and Fridays 10:00–12:00, except holidays. The consulted page did not publish a wider dispatch cadence or destination-specific rates.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The site’s guiding invitation is “Kaffee vom Anbau bis in die Tasse” (“coffee from cultivation to the cup”), spanning farming, roasting, education, testing and cafés rather than roasting alone.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper reads three Shopify collection feeds, canonicalizes collection URLs to `/en/products/<handle>`, excludes equipment, gift sets, subscriptions and German `-abo`/`geschenkset` handles, and always translates German product content to English. Product-page scraping is disabled and cached feed data is used instead.
