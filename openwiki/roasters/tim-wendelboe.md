# Tim Wendelboe — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Tim Wendelboe is the archetypal Nordic light-roast operation in Oslo, founded
by a world-champion barista. It runs an espresso bar and has roasted to order
every week since 2007, publishing a transparency report every year since 2010.
It sells filter and espresso coffees on a Shopify storefront at timwendelboe.no
priced in NOK, organised into `filter-coffee` and `espresso-coffee`
collections.

## Address

- Espresso bar at Grünersgate 1, Grünerløkka, Oslo — Norway.
- Roastery at Tøyengata 29c, Oslo — Norway.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Publishes an annual Transparency Report since 2010 (15th edition, 2024).
- Average FOB paid was 6.37 USD/lb in 2024 (6.07 in 2023); 2024 C-market
  average was 2.43 — above the Specialty Coffee Transaction Guide high-tier
  benchmark (~3.50 average).
- Example (per their site): $5.75/lb FOB for Finca Tamana, with the farmer
  receiving $4.75/lb net.
- Named partners: Belco Trading (Ethiopia), Fairfield Trading (Colombia),
  Boncafe & San Vicente (Honduras), C. Dormans (Kenya).
- Signed The Pledge (transparency.coffee, 2019). Reports all purchases.

## Schedules & Shipping

- "Roasting to order every week since 2007"; "we always send coffee within a
  week after it was roasted" (per their site).
- Coffee ships in nitrogen-flushed vacuum bags.
- Subscriptions dispatch on the first Wednesday of each month.
- Norway via Posten; international via DHL Express (with a country exclusion
  list); international shipping includes DDP/customs.
- No published free-delivery threshold; shipping is calculated from
  weight/volume/destination.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Long-term direct relationships: Caballero since 2009, Nacimiento since 2008,
  Los Pirineos since 2010, Finca Tamana since 2012.
- Owns Finca El Suelo (Huila, Colombia) since 2019.
- TW Biological Project (Nov 2023, with Biofilia Mexico): goal that by 2034 all
  purchased coffee comes from regenerative farming.
- Norwegian Barista Champion 2001/2002/2004; WBC runner-up 2001/2002; World
  Barista Champion 2004; World Cup Tasters Champion 2005.
- The bar serves espresso-based drinks.

## Scraping Quirks

- Shopify storefront scraped from two collections: `filter-coffee` and
  `espresso-coffee`.
- The `test-roast-1kg` and `coffee-berry-fizz` products are excluded from the
  catalogue.
