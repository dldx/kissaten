# Chipp Coffee Co — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Chipp Coffee Co (chippcoffee.co.uk) is a Leeds coffee roaster on a Shopify
storefront in GBP. Its catalogue runs from core espresso and filter offerings
to limited-edition drops and micro-lots, "all selected and roasted in Leeds by
our head roaster" per its homepage. The store also sells personalised coffee
gifts.

## Address

- 37 Crossland Court, Czar St, Holbeck, Leeds, LS11 9PR — United Kingdom
  (published in the store footer).

## Scraping Quirks

- Domain correction: the checklist "CHIPP" row listed chippcoffeeroasters.co.uk;
  the live shop is chippcoffee.co.uk.
- The personalised-coffee gift bundle is dropped from the catalogue (not a
  whole-bean coffee product).
