# Coffee Architects — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Coffee Architects is a Swiss coffee company whose Squarespace site offers
espresso, filter and decaffeinated coffees. It describes a three-person team
and a one-stop service spanning coffee production, service, bar architecture
and packaging, with coffee available through its own site and partner cafés.
The legal notice identifies the company as Coffee Architects AG.

## Address

- Thalwil, Switzerland — the legal notice gives Coffee Architects AG's domicile
  as Schützenstrasse 5, 8800 Thalwil, but the site does not separately identify
  that domicile as a roasting address; no other roastery address is published.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The terms say orders are handed to Swiss Post or a carrier no later than five
  working days after receipt. Swiss delivery is free above CHF 100 and normally
  arrives within 2–5 days; express or courier delivery is available at postage
  cost. The terms restrict delivery to Switzerland and publish no other
  destination-specific shipping rates.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Coffee Architects presents coffee as part of a wider design and hospitality
  practice: it says its team brings together production, service, bar and
  interior design, and packaging expertise. Its public offer is organised by
  brew style and includes named coffees such as Amizade, Bélico, Fusão, Joya,
  Faya and Muraho.

## Scraping Quirks

- This is not a conventional product catalogue: the scraper finds the
  Squarespace “Angebot” section, turns each bean image into a stable hash-like
  pseudo-URL, downloads the image, and asks the AI extractor to read the label.
  The pseudo-URL is stored as the bean URL and the image URL as `image_url`; a
  name containing “Coffee Architects” is discarded.
