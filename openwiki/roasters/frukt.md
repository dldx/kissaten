# Frukt Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Frukt Coffee Roasters is a contemporary coffee roasting company established in 2018 in Turku, Finland. Its Shopify storefront sells seasonal and rare coffees, accessories and subscriptions; the site describes its coffees as suitable for both filter and espresso (an omni-roast). The footer identifies the business as Frukt Coffee Roasters Oy.

## Address

- Graniittilinnankatu 2 k, 20100 Turku, Finland.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The monthly subscription is sent out in the last week of each month; the site asks customers to subscribe by the 21st, with renewal on the 22nd and processing the following week. It names Posti for Finland, GLS for the EU and DHL Express for overseas delivery. A delivery-time promise or general free-shipping minimum was not found on the pages checked.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Frukt presents its core coffee range as seasonal and rare releases and explicitly positions the regular range as omni-roast. Subscription boxes contain two featured coffees, normally in 250 g bags, though special releases may be 125 g.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses the `/pages/store` page and only the first three `div.site-box-container` sections. It excludes URLs containing `gift-card`, `accessories` or `merch`, and skips listing links whose text contains “Sold out”.
