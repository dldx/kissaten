# Smuggler's Drop — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Smuggler's Drop (smugglersdrop.co.uk) is a small-batch speciality coffee
roastery based in Hastings, East Sussex (per their site), serving coffee
"small-batch roasted, ethically sourced, and shipped fresh across the UK".
The whole-bean range is playing-card themed — Ace of Clubs, King of Clubs,
Queen of Hearts (decaf), Jack of Clubs and The Joker — sold from a Squarespace
Commerce storefront in GBP.

## Address

- Full street address not published on site — Hastings, East Sussex, United
  Kingdom.

## Scraping Quirks

- Squarespace; products live at `/shop/p/...` and are discovered from the
  `/shop` listing, skipping sold-out cards.
- The bare `smugglersdrop.co.uk` domain 301-redirects to the `www` canonical
  host, which is what stored product URLs are based on.
- Card-named catalogue; the Ace of Spades t-shirt, gift cards and monthly gift
  subscriptions are excluded. No tasting kits in the line-up, so no reviewing
  needed.
