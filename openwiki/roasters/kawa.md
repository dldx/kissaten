# Kawa — Roaster Profile

## Overview

The checklist names Kawa and links to `shop.kawa.coffee`; that address currently
returns the Tanat Coffee storefront. Tanat describes the business as a Paris
specialty coffee roaster focused on direct relationships with farms, producers
and cooperatives, and lists Kawa-branded professional links in its footer. The
current storefront is a WordPress/WooCommerce site with coffee, equipment and
training content.

## Address

- 189 Rue d'Aubervilliers, 75018 Paris, France.

## Sustainability

- Tanat says it reduces impact through sustainable agricultural practices,
  lighter packaging, reconsidered transport and restrained consumption. It
  also says it will not produce capsules.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The site says coffees are selected directly at source with farms, producers
  and cooperatives, often over relationships lasting several years, with lots
  traceable end to end. No price-paid-to-producer or cost-breakdown figures
  were published on the reviewed pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders are prepared and dispatched within 1–3 working days, Monday–Friday
  excluding public holidays. The site says it makes its best effort to roast
  to order and lists destination-specific shipping zones, but the reviewed
  page says it is being updated and directs customers to checkout for final
  shipping charges.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Tanat says it has focused on roasting since opening and tunes each profile
  by tasting, adjusting and refining it to reveal the terroir rather than mask
  it.

## Scraping Quirks

- No `@register_scraper` declaration for Kawa was found in the current
  `src/kissaten/scrapers/` tree. The separate `tanat_coffee.py` module is
  registered as `tanat-coffee`, not Kawa; this page therefore records the
  checklist/storefront identity without claiming a Kawa registry key.
