# Roasticious — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Roasticious describes itself as a specialty coffee nano-roaster. Its WooCommerce shop lists small-lot coffees from Kenya, Ethiopia, Ecuador, Colombia and other origins, alongside equipment and green coffee. The shop uses CHF pricing and distinguishes especially high-scoring “TOP” coffees and espresso-friendly offerings.

## Address

- Switzerland — full roastery address, including town, was not published on the consulted site pages.

## Sustainability

- Roasticious says its coffee bags, including the valve, are made from **100% LDPE-4** and are fully recyclable in Switzerland. Customers may bring or send bags back for reuse or recycling.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The FAQ names Covoya, Falcon and El Placer as trusted sourcing partners and says it selects coffees from Ethiopia, Kenya, Rwanda, Tanzania, Yemen, Brazil and Colombia. It states that it prioritises transparency and sustainable, ethical relationships with farmers and cooperatives; no price-paid-to-producer figures are published on the consulted page.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Switzerland orders use Swiss Post Priority PostPac or Economy PostPac, with a stated maximum merchandise limit of **9,000 g**. EU and international orders use Swiss Post Economy International with tracking, with a maximum merchandise limit of **1,800 g**. Pickup is free by appointment. Customs duties and taxes are not included for EU and international parcels. No roast-day cadence or free-shipping threshold is published in the consulted FAQ.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Roasticious says coffee is at its best within three months of roasting, while remaining safe to drink for up to nine months. Its catalogue uses visible “TOP” and “ESP” markers to distinguish top lots and espresso-friendly coffees.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper reads the homepage, where all products are listed, and keeps only in-stock WooCommerce items carrying the `product_cat-coffee` class. It narrows product detail HTML to two Divi rows and records a product as **Both** when its roast selector offers both filter and espresso. It excludes equipment, green coffee, kits, taster products, drip bags and other non-bean slugs.
