# Crosby — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Crosby Coffee was founded in Liverpool in 2014 by Jack Foster — named as a
tribute to his hometown — who began roasting on American home roasters and
selling at local markets; Mark Slinger joined as co-owner in 2016. From the
Bridge Road Industrial Estate it has grown into the **Ten Streets Roastery**
with a shop, plus cafés at Waterloo, Lark Lane and Hightown (per their site).
All beans score above 80 on the speciality scale, and the roastery has picked
up awards including the SME Business Elite Award 2025 for Best Independent
Coffee Roasters Liverpool. The site is a Shopify storefront.

## Address

- Crosby Coffee Roastery (Ten Streets), Ground and 1st Floor, 13 Glegg Street, Liverpool L3 7DX — United Kingdom

## Sustainability

- Roasts on a **Loring roaster**, whose closed-air, single-burner design uses
  up to **80% less energy** than traditional machines and needs no separate
  afterburner (per their sustainability page).
- Donates waste coffee chaff and grounds to local gardening groups for
  composting, and educates wholesale partners on eco-friendly practices.
- Café packaging and disposables are eco-friendly: biodegradable napkins,
  corn-starch cutlery, paper straws, kraft bags, recyclable and reusable cups.
- Direct-trade relationships with producers, emphasising transparency through
  the supply chain.

## Roasting & Equipment

- All beans roasted in-house at the Ten Streets Roastery. The roaster was
  upgraded to a **Loring S35 Kestrel** in January 2025 (per co-owner Mark
  Slinger's LinkedIn post); the site confirms the Loring brand and its
  energy-saving closed-air design.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Most orders dispatch the **same working day** if placed before 11am
  (Mon–Fri); after that or over a weekend, the next working day.
- Rates (per their delivery & returns page): **standard tracked delivery
  £4.00** (up to 2kg, 2–3 working days), **£7.00** for 2–20kg; **tracked next
  working day** £7.00 (up to 2kg) / £10.00 (2–20kg) when ordered before 11am
  Mon–Fri.
- **Subscriptions ship free** on every order.
- Wholesale orders get **free delivery over £100**.
- Click & Collect from the roastery (ready within 24 hours) or from any shop
  (coffee delivered to shops every Friday, so up to 7 days).

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Founding belief: "great coffee should be enjoyed by everyone" — accessibility
  is the stated mission, not exclusivity.
- Named for Jack Foster's hometown of Crosby, on Liverpool's northern coast.
- Long award list: Business Elite Award 2025 (Best Independent Coffee Roasters
  Liverpool), Liverpool City Region Tourism Awards finalist, North West
  Business of the Year shortlist, Liverpool Hospitality Barista of the Year
  won and runner-up at Lark Lane, MIB Retailer of the Year shortlist two years
  running, and a twice-awarded Good Food Award Gold Seal.
- Sells **Thirsty Planet** water bottles in support of clean-water projects.
