# Ditta Artigianale — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Ditta Artigianale is an Italian specialty coffee roaster. Its registered shop
offers multilingual Shopify collections for single origins, decaf, blends and
special editions; the reviewed official pages did not confirm a roastery city
or street address.

## Address

- Roastery: Italy — full address not published on the reviewed official pages.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper crawls separate availability-filtered collections for single
  origins, decaf, blends and special editions, including a second page of the
  single-origin collection.
- It removes non-coffee URLs such as learning variety boxes, chocolate, bundles,
  gift cards, accessories, drip bags, tea and `selection` products. Sold-out
  cards are skipped before AI extraction, and extracted content is translated to
  English.
