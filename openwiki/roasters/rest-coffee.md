# Rest Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Rest is a specialty coffee shop, roastery and art space in northern Cyprus. Its Wix storefront sells coffee, drip bags and equipment and offers SCA education and wholesale. The company’s Turkish-language about page describes a goal of making competition-level quality and coffee knowledge part of everyday coffee culture.

## Address

- Direniş Caddesi, No. 2, Zeytinlik Köy Meydanı, Girne, Cyprus.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Founder Narden Sonel is described by the site as Cyprus’s only CQI-licensed Q Grader and as a World Coffee Championships Brewers Cup judge. Rest presents coffee through a scientific, analytical and educational lens, not only as a beverage.
