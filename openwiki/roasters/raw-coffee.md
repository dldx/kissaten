# RAW Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

RAW Coffee Roasters (rawcoffeeroasters.co.uk) is a specialty coffee and
micro-roastery in Leeds (per their site), with a coffee shop and menu at its
Mill Hill site. The storefront is a non-standard Shopify build — its
`products.json` endpoint is disabled — so the scraper discovers the
catalogue via the sitemap instead (9 products saved).
