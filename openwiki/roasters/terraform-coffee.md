# Terraform Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Terraform Coffee Roasters says it was founded in Shanghai in 2019 and grew from the same roots as Blacksheep Espresso, established in 2017. Its Shopify storefront sells espresso, filter and limited-selection coffees, alongside subscriptions and merchandise. The brand says it operates offline through Blacksheep Espresso stores in Shanghai and Tokyo.

## Address

- Minhang District, Shanghai, China — the roastery district is published, but a street address is not published on the consulted site pages.

## Sustainability

- Terraform says it uses biodegradable, easily recyclable packaging and reusable exhibition materials, and describes employee well-being and long-term development as part of its sustainability work. It identifies itself as the 85th B Corp Certified business in mainland China.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The roaster says its team visits coffee-growing regions, builds direct relationships with estates and farmers, develops custom micro-lots with estates, and rigorously cups coffees at the roastery. No FOB, farm-gate or price-paid-to-producer figures are published on the consulted pages.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Terraform’s Minhang facility uses a Loring S15 hot-air roaster for single-origin pour-over coffees, a Loring S70 for espresso beans, and a UG22 for the Blacksheep Espresso Blacksheep Series. The facility includes a public second-floor space from which visitors can overlook the production workshop; the packaging area uses FUSO automated packaging machines and automatic drip-bag packers.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The shipping page says orders are sent through the roaster’s preferred carrier and that a shipping confirmation is issued when a parcel is on its way. Import duties and taxes are the customer’s responsibility. No roast cadence, dispatch days, free-delivery minimum or destination-specific rate is published on the consulted page.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Terraform describes its role as a link between growers and consumers and says it focuses on the clean elegance of washed coffee. Its seasonal Blacksheep Series launches four signature beans around distinct themes, while its Omni blend is positioned as an approachable everyday coffee.
