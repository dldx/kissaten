# 44 North Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

44 North Coffee is a women-owned small-batch coffee roaster based in Deer Isle,
Maine, United States (per the registered roaster declaration). Its online
store is Shopify-based, prices in USD and exposes a `fair-trade-organic-beans`
collection. The site was rate-limited during direct fetching, so additional
claims about current products, sourcing numbers, hardware and fulfilment are
not made here.

## Address

- Deer Isle, Maine, United States — full roastery street address not published on site.

## Scraping Quirks

- This scraper uses Shopify `products.json` for the fair-trade-organic-beans
  collection and does not scrape individual product pages.
- It excludes subscriptions, drip bags, bundles, canned coffee, cascara,
  tea/matcha, tea ware, food, chocolate, drippers, latte mix and other named
  non-bean handles.
