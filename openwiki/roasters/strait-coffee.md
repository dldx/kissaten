# Strait Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Strait Coffee Roasters is a specialty roaster in San Jose, California, United
States, focused on ultra-premium single-origin coffees. The registered site
separates current coffee from a coffee archive, preserving historical lots as
well as current offerings.

## Address

## Roasting & Equipment

- The roaster's registered description identifies a clean-air electric
  fluid-bed roaster. No brand, model or capacity is published in the reviewed
  pages.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper intentionally crawls both `/coffee` and `/coffee-archive`; the
  latter contains sold-out historical beans and is needed for the back
  catalogue.
- Product cards are lazy-rendered. The scraper dismisses Cookiebot, waits for
  product groups and auto-scrolls up to 60 times before collecting HTML.
