# Artisan Roast — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Artisan Roast is a Scottish specialty coffee roaster based in Edinburgh, **roasting
since 2007**. Its café on **Broughton Street** is widely credited with helping shape
Scotland's coffee scene, and it has since grown to four Edinburgh cafés plus a
roastery. In **2012** it won the **Glenfiddich Spirit of Scotland Award**. The shop is
a Shopify storefront at [artisanroast.co.uk](https://artisanroast.co.uk) with a
curated coffee collection holding the house blends **Janszoon**,
**Trigonometry** and **Cobblestone**, single origins (Colombia — La Guaca;
Ethiopia — Banko Gotiti; Brazil; Peru — El Palto) and a decaf — plus a
**Rare Club** subscription and a **Taster Pack** (flagged for review).

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Roasting since **2007** with a **Glenfiddich Spirit of Scotland Award (2012)** —
  named recognition for the role the Broughton Street café played in establishing
  specialty coffee in Edinburgh.
- The house identity rests on three signature blends: **Janszoon**,
  **Trigonometry** and **Cobblestone**, with single origins rotating through the
  roastery (Colombia — La Guaca; Ethiopia — Banko Gotiti; Brazil; Peru — El
  Palto) plus a decaf.
- The **Rare Club** is the shop's recurring coffee subscription programme,
  sold alongside single bags on the storefront.

## Scraping Quirks

- The curated `coffee` collection already contains the house blends and the
  **Taster Pack** — the Taster Pack is a tasting kit and is **deliberately not
  excluded**: it flows through the shop and is flagged `is_tasting_kit` /
  `requires_review` by the base class, landing in the admin review queue.
