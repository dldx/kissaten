# Imbibe Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Imbibe Coffee Roasters is an Irish roasting business based in Dublin 8, trading
on a Shopify storefront in EUR. Its tagline — "Dublin 8 Roaster. We give up 4%
of all you spend before we make a cent. We roast coffee very well." — sums up
a founder-led, ethically-run operation selling single origins, blends and
decaf, and one of Ireland's few certified-organic roasteries (Organic Trust).

## Address

- 9 Dolphin's Barn, Dublin 8, Dublin, D08 WD35, Republic of Ireland.

## Sustainability

- ~90% of wholesale coffee is packed in zero-waste cans that are collected,
  cleaned and reused; retail packaging is recyclable from a carbon-neutral
  supplier.
- Deliveries use electric vehicles with An Post as courier; the roaster has an
  afterburner fitted to break down volatile organic compounds from roasting.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Pays above commodity and fair-trade pricing with social, quality and organic
  premiums (this year $8,500 between the Colombia ASOPEP and Peru Aprysa
  co-ops); direct-trade relationships with producers including La Huella,
  Ardent, Mikava/El Placer and 90 Plus Estates; WCR Checkoff participant.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Free shipping on all orders of €35 or above in Ireland (per the site footer).

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Gives 4% of all revenue to good causes before taking a cent (over 30% of
  profits each year), with a minimum 1% of annual revenue to Women's Aid;
  funded four bridges for the Colombia ASOP co-op and donates coffee weekly
  to Dublin hospitals.
- Staff policy: paid above industry rates, living-wage part-timers, pensions,
  full sick pay, and 1% of annual revenue shared with staff.

## Scraping Quirks

- The curated `all-coffees` collection is read via Shopify's JSON products
  feed (JSON-only): 16 whole-bean coffees plus a rotating "Two To Go Bundle"
  sampler flagged `is_tasting_kit` / `requires_review` into the review queue.
