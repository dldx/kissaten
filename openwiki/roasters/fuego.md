# Fuego Tostadores — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Fuego Tostadores is an Argentinian specialty coffee roaster. Its Tiendanube storefront sells single-origin coffees, blends, subscriptions, equipment and barista courses; product pages publish origin, region, producer, variety and process for many coffees. The catalogue includes coffees from Colombia, Brazil, Bolivia and Peru as well as house blends.

## Address

- Argentina — full roastery address not published on site. The storefront references deliveries throughout AMBA; the CABA address shown for a barista course is a course venue, not a verified roastery address.

## Sustainability

- The shop offers selected 250 g coffees in a compostable “recarga” format and warns that the refill bag is not intended for storage: customers should transfer the coffee to another Fuego bag or airtight container.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Product pages give concrete lot information including named producers or producer groups, region, variety and process. For example, the current listings identify Augusto Borges for Brazil Arara, Mauricio Osorio for Colombia Guayacán, and Wilkim Huelgos and Didier Quintero for Finca Los Girasoles. No price-paid-to-producer or cost-breakdown figures were found.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The storefront advertises free delivery throughout AMBA for purchases over ARS 70,000. It exposes postcode-based shipping calculation at checkout; no fixed roast or dispatch cadence was found on the pages checked.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper visits two paginated coffee-category URLs and uses AI extraction. Before sending a page to the extractor it reduces the HTML to the product container, selected metadata, JSON-LD and the `LS.variants`/`LS.currency` script. Product URLs are filtered using the `/productos/` path and pages are translated to English.
