# Subtext — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Subtext is a Toronto specialty coffee roaster with a Shopify storefront offering
filter, espresso, reserve and rested coffees, subscriptions, classes and
events. Its philosophy rejects blends, renamed coffees and dark roasting in
favour of producer names, seasonality and micro-regionality. The same west-end
space houses the roastery, cafe and education programme.

## Address

- 130 Cawthra Avenue, Unit 104, Toronto, Ontario M6N 3C2, Canada.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Subtext says each coffee carries the producer's name and that product pages
  identify the producer, location down to the town and processing. It combines
  some direct sourcing with importers it considers transparent and values-led.
- The site publishes **2022, 2023 and 2024 Transparency Reports**. No per-coffee
  price figure is summarized on the profile pages; the reports are the relevant
  source for the detailed purchasing information.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Subtext calibrates roasts to the second and degree, evaluates profiles blind,
  measures green-coffee moisture and density, and uses thermocouple readings,
  hundreds of roasted-coffee colour readings and solubility testing. It also
  uses a **ColorTrack** for roast-development consistency. The roaster brand,
  model and capacity are not stated on the pages checked.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Roast days are **Tuesday and Thursday**; orders ship Wednesday and Friday.
  Cutoffs are Sunday at **11:59pm Eastern** for Tuesday roasting and Tuesday at
  **11:59pm Eastern** for Thursday roasting. Canadian subscriptions ship the
  first Wednesday of each month.
- Coffee-only Canadian orders of **$60 CAD or more** ship free. US orders of
  **$75 USD or more** ship free, excluding PO boxes and rural areas. UK orders
  of **£60 or more** qualify for **£11 flat-rate** DHL Worldwide Express;
  European orders of **€65 or more** qualify for **€13 flat-rate** DHL delivery
  to most countries in Europe.
