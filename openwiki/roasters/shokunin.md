# Shokunin Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Shokunin is a coffee roastery in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. It presents coffees through a WooCommerce shop and an accompanying learning portal, with omniroast coffees, subscriptions and producer stories. The company says transparency and stability are the basis of sustainable partnerships across the coffee chain.

## Address

- Rotterdam, Netherlands — full roastery street address was not published on the consulted site pages; the roastery is not open to visitors.

## Sustainability

- Shokunin describes its producer relationships as sustainable partnerships and says its work can support projects for growth across the coffee chain. It reports building drying beds, fermentation tanks and depulping tanks with Colombian producers Juan Pablo and Efrain Argote.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Shokunin describes direct trade with producers including Ana Restrepo, whom it says it self-imports, and the Argote family. Its product pages carry details of its transparent cooperation with importers and farmers. Producer stories also cover the Tocto family's organic practices, the Hopong cooperative and work supporting Gilbert Makembele's rebuilding and peace-building cause.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Shokunin roasts to order every Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday, sends orders every day when coffees are available, and says an order will never take longer than a week to send. Coffee is no more than 10 days from roasting when dispatched. Free shipping is published for the Netherlands above **€70** and the EU above **€200**, with the EU threshold varying by country; worldwide shipping is available on request.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Shokunin says its omniroast coffees are suitable for filter and espresso and need only one day of rest after roasting. Its name and programme centre on learning: the shop links recipes, a jargon library and producer stories alongside coffee.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper targets `/shop/` and selects only links matching `/product/` with the `a.pushed` selector. It explicitly excludes mug, pin, green-coffee, cups and surprise URLs so those shop items do not enter the bean catalogue.
