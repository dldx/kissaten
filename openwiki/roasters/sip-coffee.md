# SIP Collective — Roaster Profile

## Overview

SIP Collective (sipcoffee.co) is a UK specialty coffee roaster billing itself
"Home of the 2026 UK Coffee Roasting Champion | 8th Place at the World Coffee
Roasting Championship" (per their site). Founded by Alexandru-Catalin Marin,
the 2026 UK Coffee Roasting Champion (per the company's own LinkedIn, linked
from the site), it sources and roasts rare, traceable microlots from
producers worldwide, with seasonal coffees sold from a Shopify storefront in
GBP and free UK delivery over £30.

## Address

- London, United Kingdom (per the company's LinkedIn profile, linked from
  their homepage); full street address not published on site.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Free UK delivery on all orders over £30 (per the site's announcement bar).

- Shopify — scraped JSON-only from the curated `coffee` collection; brewing
