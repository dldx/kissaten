# Style Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Style Coffee is a Japanese specialty coffee business based in Kyoto. Its BASE
storefront presents beans, gifts and subscriptions, with coffees such as
Ethiopia Anasora, Honduras lots, Kenya Gititu, Brazil Monte Alegre and an
Ethiopian decaf. The site describes a sensory lab used for coffee study,
cupping workshops and wholesale training.

## Address

- Kyoto, Japan — full roastery address not published on site; the access page
  gives 1F 360-1 Masuya-cho, Kamigyo-ku, Kyoto 602-0877 for its sensory-lab
  premises.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Style Coffee says it uses light roasting to preserve bean character, aiming for
  fruity sweetness, complex acidity and a clean texture. No machine brand,
  model or capacity is published on the pages checked.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The storefront states **free shipping on purchases of ¥6,000 or more**. No
  dispatch day or delivery-time promise was found on the accessible storefront
  pages.
