# Fathers — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Father's Coffee Roastery is a Czech specialty roastery founded in Ostrava in
2018. It sells whole-bean single-origin coffee for filter and espresso through
a SvelteKit storefront, alongside subscriptions and wholesale. The company
also has a coffee store in Prague.

## Address

- Mitrovicka 128/66, 724 00 Ostrava 24, Czechia.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Fathers says it sources exclusively from fresh harvests and builds long-term
  relationships. Since 2023 it has funded a colour-sorting station and quality-
  control lab in eastern Uganda with The Coffee Gardens, backed by the Czech
  Development Agency. It also works with Apunu/Siruma in Colombia on quality-
  control technology and solar dryers. The site says these projects help farmers
  earn higher prices and improve production sustainability.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Fathers roasts on an American Loring S35 hot-air roaster. After roasting,
  coffee passes through an optical sorter to remove quakers and other defects;
  every batch is tracked with eight critical data points and multiple quality
  checks.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Coffee is roasted four times a week, Monday through Thursday, and is usually
  handed to the carrier the same day. Orders by Monday midnight ship Tuesday;
  Tuesday orders ship Wednesday; Wednesday orders ship Thursday; Thursday-
  Sunday orders ship Monday. Czech delivery is usually 1 working day from
  dispatch and Slovakia 1-2 working days. Father's Family members receive free
  Czech shipping from one coffee package of 250 g or more.

## Scraping Quirks

- The English storefront exposes filter and espresso categories at
  `/en/coffee/filter` and `/en/coffee/espresso`. The scraper excludes drip bags,
  capsules and courses, and narrows product pages to the CZK value in embedded
  multi-currency data before AI extraction.
