# Caretta Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Caretta Coffee Roasters is a young UK speciality roaster founded in **October
2024** by lifelong friends and coffee professionals **Geo Patrik** and
**Cristian Vergara**. Based in Cornwall (the house espresso **COASTLINE** is
named after "our home here in Cornwall — a place shaped by the sea"), they
roast light, fruit-forward coffees sourced from dedicated producers, with
focus on clarity, sweetness and expression. The Shopify storefront is at
[carettacoffee.com](https://carettacoffee.com), which also sells brewing
equipment (Fellow, Origami, Barista Hustle) alongside the coffee range.

## Address

- Caretta Coffee Roasters, Sithney Common Hill, Helston, TR13 0RH — United
  Kingdom

## Roasting & Equipment

- **Roasted to order**: "we roast and ship on the same day or within 48
  hours of order", with the exact roast date printed on the box.
- **Light roast philosophy** — they recommend going much finer on espresso
  and resting light roasts 2–3 weeks from roast date to open up the flavour.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Coffee is roasted and shipped the same day or within 48 hours of ordering.
- Shipping is calculated at checkout based on delivery location and order
  weight — no published flat rates or free-delivery threshold.
- 14-day return window for unused equipment and merchandise in original
  packaging; roasted coffee is not returned or exchanged (perishable).

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Purpose statement: "**Crafting Coffee, Elevating Moments**" — coffee as
  "a story, an experience, and an invitation to savor life's most meaningful
  moments".
- Runs its own brewing competition, the **Caretta Brewers Cup** — the 2026
  edition at Moonstruck Cafe, Lambeth, London billed as "16 competitors. 15
  rounds. One ultimate brewing event!", with competitor tickets at £15
  (including a box of coffee to practise with) and free spectator tickets.

## Scraping Quirks

- The storefront is equipment-heavy (Fellow brewers, Origami drippers,
  Autocomb, apparel) and sells event tickets, so the scraper applies a long
  slug-based exclusion list — including `ticket` (the Brewers Cup competitor/
  spectator tickets), `gift`/`gift-card`, `equipment`/`brewing`, and an
  assortment of gear slugs (`grinder`, `kettle`, `scale`, `acaia`, …).
- The `-pack-` exclusion pattern filters multi-pack merchandise products.
