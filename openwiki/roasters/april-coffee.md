# April Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

April Coffee is a Denmark-rooted roastery founded in 2016 by Patrik Rolf. The company says it roasts at its R&D headquarters just outside Copenhagen in Solrod Strand, while its Copenhagen presence includes an April Coffee Store and Showroom and SP Coffee Shop. Its shop separates filter, espresso, limited, organic, drip-pack/capsule, and equipment lines.

## Address

- Solrod Byvej 15, 2680 Solrod Strand - Denmark
- April Coffee Store & Showroom, Pilestraede 39, 1112 Copenhagen - Denmark
- SP Coffee Shop, Pilestraede 37, 1112 Copenhagen - Denmark

## Sustainability

- April has an organic-coffee project and says it is exploring organic production despite certification differences between origins. The project page names Kayon Mountain in Ethiopia and Milton Fernandez in Peru; it does not claim that every April coffee is organic.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- April says its partner farmers decide the price of their coffee and that the company is seeking better quality rather than cheaper coffee. It describes direct partnerships and an aim to grow together with farmers, but publishes no per-kg FOB or farm-gate figures on the pages checked.

## Roasting & Equipment

- April reports that its first roaster was a Loring S15 Falcon purchased in 2022 and upgraded to a Loring S35 in autumn 2024. It also operates a lab for quality control and research, monitoring green lots before arrival and throughout the season.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders are roasted, packed, and shipped on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. The published deadline for same-week coffee shipping is 12:00 Copenhagen time on Monday; the stated arrival range is 1 to 21 days from roast depending on shipping option. No free-shipping minimum is published on the policy page checked.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- April's stated aim is to reclaim coffee flavour through direct partnerships, clean traditional processing, and attention to variety and terroir. The founder also frames national coffee competitions as a way to keep learning rather than as the company's identity.
