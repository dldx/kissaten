# Artefact Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Artefact Coffee (artefact.coffee) is a UK specialty roastery in the
Farnham/Surrey area, on a Shopify storefront in GBP. It is the rebrand of Kota
Coffee — the former kotacoffee.com domain now serves the Artefact store. The
roastery positions itself on technical engineering, using fluid-bed technology
for what its site calls "zero combustion and absolute precision", with free
tracked delivery over £25.

## Address

- Farnham, Surrey — United Kingdom (full street address not published on site;
  the site's "Locations" pages list café partners such as Higher Ground Kaffe,
  Farnham rather than a roastery address).

## Roasting & Equipment

- Fluid-bed (hot-air) roasting technology; the site describes a "fluid-bed
  technology for zero combustion and absolute precision" approach.
