# Gardelli Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Gardelli Coffee is an Italian specialty coffee company whose PrestaShop storefront offers single-origin coffees, rare micro-lots and the Cignobianco signature blend, alongside equipment and education. The site identifies Rubens Gardelli as World Coffee Roasting Champion and presents coffees for both espresso and filter. Its flagship Caffeteca is in Forlì.

## Address

- Forlì, Italy — full roastery address not published on the site.

## Sustainability

- Gardelli says its bags use certified compostable NatureFlex film made from FSC-certified cellulose and recommends industrial composting. It also reports photovoltaic panels on the roastery roof, biodegradable cleaning products and recycled paper in operations.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Gardelli says every coffee is fully traceable, sourced from fresh in-season crops and selected through a strict blind-tasting protocol. Its pricing explanation combines cup-quality score with a separate rarity score; no FOB or producer price figures were found.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Each lot is tested with multiple profiles and cupped before the profile is selected. Gardelli says it uses a fully customised roasting machine with controlled heat transfer and airflow, plus an optical sorter; no brand, model or capacity is published. The company uses one roast for both espresso and filter.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Gardelli says coffees are roasted three times a week and packaged and shipped the same day. During the current site notice it lists the next roast as 31 August; no general destination-specific shipping table was found.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The site explicitly separates “quality” from “rarity”: a higher quality score does not necessarily mean a higher price because scarcity is priced separately.
