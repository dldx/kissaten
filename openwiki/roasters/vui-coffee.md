# Vui Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Vui Coffee Roasters is an Indian coffee farm-and-roastery business selling blends and single-origin coffees through a Shopify storefront. The shop organises coffee by roast level, equipment and preference, and also offers subscriptions, brew guides, events, B2B coffee solutions and a Vui café. Current product pages show roast labels such as Light–Medium and Medium–Dark and prices in Indian rupees.

## Address

- India — the consulted site pages do not publish a full roastery address.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Vui’s site uses the line “Every Bean Has a Soulmate” for its coffee-matching quiz and describes the café as a place where beans are roasted, brewed and served without pretence. Its subscription offer promises 10% off and a chosen delivery frequency.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses the Shopify all-products collection but extracts product links with generic `/products/` selectors. It excludes URL matches for `biochar` and `rdx`, which are non-coffee items on the live catalogue; those exclusions should be revisited if the product taxonomy changes.
