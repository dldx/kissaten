# Little Fin Coffee Roastery — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Little Fin Coffee Roastery (www.littlefinroastery.com) is a Leigh-on-Sea
(Essex) coffee roaster running a shop at 114 Broadway that is open seven days a
week — "beans, bakes, brews and ground coffee", per the site — with an online
Shopify storefront in GBP. The catalogue holds 11 whole-bean coffees plus two
tasting selection packs: the "Triple Shot" and "'The Five'" selection packs.

## Address

- 114 Broadway, Leigh-on-Sea, Essex, SS9 1FS — United Kingdom (published shop
  address; roasting takes place in the same broadway premises).
