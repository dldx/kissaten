# Coaltown Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Coaltown Coffee is a B Corp-certified specialty roaster in Ammanford
(_Rhydaman_), a former anthracite-mining town in South West Wales. The
founder Scott James set out to bring "a new kind of Black Gold" to the town
after its last colliery closed in 2003, and the machines "began to hum once
more" in the roastery in 2018. The Shopify storefront plays the heritage
theme to the hilt — house blends are named Candle Light, Anthracite, Black
Gold, The Union and Jenkin Jones. Coaltown operates a roastery espresso bar
in Ammanford and a second espresso bar in Pontarddulais.

## Address

- The Roastery, Foundry Rd, Ammanford SA18 2LS, Carmarthenshire, Wales —
  United Kingdom

## Sustainability

- **B Corp certified** — per their site, they began the certification journey
  in 2019 and were "the first specialty coffee roaster in the UK to obtain
  certification".
- **Living Wage Foundation employer**, paying above the Real Living Wage in a
  post-industrial town.
- States a commitment to reducing direct environmental impact (waste and
  emissions evaluation) under its B Corp policies.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Direct-trade sourcing: "we prefer to deconstruct traditional supply chains
  and develop direct trade value chains", with stated accountability and
  transparency across the supply chain (per their site).
- Named relationships include Kisinga (Uganda, raised drying beds) and
  Kapchwora (Uganda); the team documented a 2026 trip to Volcafe partner
  farms in Brazil.

## Roasting & Equipment

- **GG75 "Geoffrey"** — a custom-restored drum roaster with 75kg capacity,
  used for house blends and popular single origins.
- A **12kg Probat** for smaller-batch single origins.

## Schedules & Shipping

- **Roasted Monday/Friday weekly**; same-day despatch cut-off is 12pm.
- Orders shipped within 48 hours Monday to Friday; free UK shipping over
  **£30** (Tracked, 2–3 days).
- Royal Mail Tracked 48 (up to 200g £3.00 / up to 5kg £3.50), Royal Mail
  Tracked 24 (£4.00 / £4.50), DPD next working day from £6.50 (UK mainland).
- Click-and-collect available at the Ammanford roastery.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- "Black Gold means three things": Fair, Different, Delicious — with coffee
  as the town's new Black Gold after coal.
- Founder Scott James's great-grandfather **Ben Addis** worked the Ammanford
  collieries from age 14; a 1956 photograph of him anchors the brand story.
- Winner of the **2026 SCA Coffee Design Awards (Branding)** at World of
  Coffee Brussels for the Black Gold rebrand.
- Sells Nespresso-compatible capsule ranges, brewing gear and a Stanley
  thermal-bottle collaboration alongside the coffee.
- Runs **Coaltown Academy** workshop courses at the roastery (which include a
  small roastery tour; no standalone tours).
