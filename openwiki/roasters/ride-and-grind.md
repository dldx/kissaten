# Ride and Grind — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Ride and Grind (www.ridegrind.com) is an East Lothian (Scotland) coffee
roastery operating from Unit 1E Wallyford Industrial Estate, on a WooCommerce
storefront in GBP. It blends a motorsport-flavoured brand ("Ride, Brew, Refuel
& Repeat", blends named Motor oil, Gasolina and Rocket fuel) with a site-side
café — "Collect from Cafe: orders ready after 24 hours, open 8–2 Monday to
Saturday" — and coffee subscriptions. Its catalogue holds 25 whole-bean coffees
plus a Favourites Tasting Pack.

## Address

- Unit 1E, Wallyford Industrial Estate, Wallyford, East Lothian, Scotland —
  United Kingdom (published on the storefront; postcode not stated on site).

## Scraping Quirks

- Successor/domain correction: the live storefront is www.ridegrind.com; the
  former rideandgrind.co.uk is parked. The checklist "Ride and Grind" row maps
  to here.
- Free UK delivery on orders over £25.
- The "Favourites Tasting Pack" (3×250g refill bags) is a tasting-kit product:
  it must flow through the review queue (`is_tasting_kit` / `requires_review`),
  never excluded.
