# Crankhouse Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Crankhouse Coffee was launched in 2014 in Exeter, Devon (its founder's blog
traces the beginnings back to a single-car garage in 2013), and styles itself a
"**speciality micro-roaster**" — "buying interesting coffees, roasting them
well and giving them the voice they deserve". It has moved four times: from a
single-car garage, to a converted barn on the edge of Dartmoor, to central
Exeter (Fore Street), and now to a small industrial unit near Exeter airport.
The site is a Shopify storefront, and the roaster also sells brewing equipment
and runs brewing/barista classes on request.

## Address

- Unit 11, Skyways Business Park, Fair Oak Close, Clyst Honiton, Exeter EX5 2UL — United Kingdom

## Sustainability

- Contributing member of **One Percent for the Planet**, plus the Specialty
  Coffee Association, World Coffee Research and the Alliance for Coffee
  Excellence (per their about page).
- Coffee pouches are single-layer plastic (rating 4) and recyclable in
  household recycling (per their FAQ).

## Roasting & Equipment

- Roasts on a **Loring S15 Falcon** Smart Roast — nicknamed "Loretta" — which
  arrived in June 2022, replacing a long-serving **Petroncini** drum roaster
  that had followed the roastery from garage to Longdown to Exeter.
- Signature "**omni roast**" style: every coffee is roasted to a middle
  profile so it works for both filter and espresso (espresso shots may need
  longer run times, and filter brews are best after 5–7 days rest, per their
  FAQ).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Roast days: generally Mondays, Tuesdays and Fridays (can vary).
- Posts every weekday; orders placed before 1pm are sent the same day where
  possible, via Royal Mail tracked with email/SMS tracking.
- Subscriptions ship in the first and third week of each month.
- Roastery collection available Monday–Friday, 8am–4pm.
- No free-delivery minimum or per-region rates are published on the site.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The journey is documented in witty blog posts — "Moving house" (the fourth
  home), "7 days" (dismantling the Petroncini for 17 Grams in Brighton), and
  the "2022 wrap up" announcing Loretta the Loring.
- Founder trained at the London School of Coffee's "how to start a coffee
  roastery" course and roast profiles sit deliberately in the middle of the
  filter/espresso spectrum.
- Fresh-release banner on the shop highlights current lots (e.g. Colombia
  Monkaaba washed Sidra, Ethiopia Rumudamo Natural).
- Offers brewing/barista classes on request (no fixed schedule).
