# 20grams Coffee Roastery — Roaster Profile

## Overview

20grams Coffee Roastery is based in Singapore and describes its work as craft-roasting coffee in a Scandinavian approach intended to preserve origin character. Its current menu is organised by origin and by espresso or filter use, with examples including Colombia Finca Bet-el, Panama Finca Momoto, El Salvador La Pacaya, and rotating Roaster's Choice coffees. The store also sells brewing equipment and masterclasses alongside coffee.

## Address

- 14 Arumugam Road, #01-05, LTC Building C, Singapore 409959 - Singapore

## Sustainability

- The roastery says its vision is to roast rare, sustainable coffee and describes sustainability as dependent on paying for quality rather than seeking lower prices.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Per their sourcing and quality pages, 20grams works closely with partner farmers and says its partner farmers decide the price of their coffee; no FOB or producer-paid figures are published on the pages checked.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Domestic Singapore delivery is free for purchases over SGD 100.00. The shipping page does not publish a roast or dispatch cadence; it lists Standard Post and FedEx options and places carrier support with the carrier after dispatch.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The site presents the coffee as a terroir-led, Scandinavian-style roasting project and maintains individual farm pages, including Finca Bet-el, El Cerro, El Socorro, and other producer lots.

## Scraping Quirks

- The Shopify scraper uses the all-products `products.json` endpoint, while the storefront separates coffee into many origin and espresso/filter collections. Many non-coffee products are mislabeled as `Coffee Beans - Roasted`, so the scraper relies on slug exclusions for masterclasses, water kits, equipment, apparel, and test roasts rather than product type alone.
