# Snakebite — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Snakebite Coffee Co. (snakebitecoffee.co) is "an independent coffee roastery
and speciality coffee shop based in Beverley, East Yorkshire, built on
community, sustainability, ethics" (per their About page). The whole-bean
line-up is snake-themed — Anaconda, Anti-Venom, Black Mamba, Boa, King Cobra
and Python — sold from a Shopify storefront in GBP.

## Address

- Unit D2 Grovehill Rd, Beverley, East Yorkshire, HU17 0JJ, United Kingdom
  (per the footer contact block; "speciality coffee shop" on the same page).
