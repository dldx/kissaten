# Snakebite — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Snakebite Coffee Co. (snakebitecoffee.co) is an independent speciality coffee
roastery and coffee shop in Beverley, East Yorkshire, built on community,
sustainability, ethics and an informal, self-deprecating personality (per
their About page). No founding year, founders, owners or named roasting team
are published on the site. The storefront is Shopify, sold in GBP, with a
snake-themed whole-bean line-up — Anaconda, Anti-Venom, Black Mamba, King
Cobra and Python — plus sampler, mystery and cause-driven coffees. The
roastery is open to the public as a shop (Tuesday–Sunday, 09:00–15:00).

## Address

- Unit D2 Low Works, Grovehill Road, Beverley, HU17 0JJ, United Kingdom (full
  roastery address on the Contact page; preferred over the shortened footer

- Contact: support@snakebitecoffee.co, 01482 373425.
- Opening hours: Tuesday–Sunday, 09:00–15:00.

## Sustainability

- The About page says the roastery works towards zero waste and diverts its
  coffee waste for garden compost, beekeeping and natural exfoliants.
- A dedicated page says one tree is planted through One Tree Planted for every
  bag sold; no current running total is published.
- The About page briefly lists supported causes (e.g. Project Waterfall's
  clean-water work in coffee-growing communities, plus local and emergency
  relief appeals) without detail.
- The white-label page says packaging is sustainable, but materials are not
  specified — nothing more should be claimed.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Snakebite says it uses direct-trade partnerships and does not use Fairtrade
  certification; this is a company claim — no prices paid, contracts,
  importer/exporter records or a full sourcing ledger were found to back it.
- Product pages do carry origin/process/producer detail; representative
  examples (not exhaustive):
  - **Anaconda** — Brazil / Minas Gerais, Santo Antonio II farm, producer
    Alexander Menezes Alckmin, Red Catuai, 1,200 masl, natural.
  - **Black Mamba** — Uganda / Rwenzori, floated natural, varieties
    Nyasaland/Bugisu/SL28/SL34, 1,600–1,800 masl.
  - **Python** — Kenya, washed & patio dried, SL28/SL34/Bourbon, published
    altitude 3,500–6,000 feet (kept as published).
  - **King Cobra** — Brazil/Mexico blend from Fazenda Picada (Marcelo Miguel)
    and small-scale Indigenous Oaxaca producers.
  - **Anti-Venom** — Colombia / Villa Maria, Castillo, 1,800 masl, natural /
    EA sugarcane decaf.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Small-batch, hand-crafted / hand-roasted in Yorkshire, with bespoke roast
  profiles; the range spans light / fast-light roasts (e.g. Black Mamba) up
  to medium (e.g. Anaconda "medium-roasted").
- No roasting machine or model, batch size, fuel, roasting software or
  detailed roast workflow is disclosed on the site.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Shipping policy: orders are processed within 1–2 business days; allow 3–5
  business days from order for delivery; a tracking number is emailed on
  dispatch; contact them if an order hasn't arrived within 14 days of the
  shipping confirmation. No dispatch-day / roast-day schedule is published.
- Rates (flat, per order): UK standard £4.20, UK tracked £5.20, worldwide
  standard £12.00. No free-delivery threshold is stated.
- Refund policy: 30-day returns with conditions (unused, original packaging,
  receipt; non-returnable sale items and gift cards); treatment of opened
  coffee bags is not specified.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Mission is "more than coffee": an independent, community-and-ethics-first
  roastery that "gives a shit", makes "stupid videos", plays loud music and
  takes itself less seriously than most coffee companies would recommend.
- Snake-themed product naming (Anaconda, Anti-Venom, Black Mamba, King Cobra,
  Python) and a Survival Kit (3×17g = 51g of random whole-bean samples) and
  Explorers Delight (50g mystery coffee).
- Profit for Palestine Coffee is listed in the All Coffee collection and says
  100% of profits go to Gaza aid.
- Core coffees sell as 250g / 1kg with grind options (wholebean, filter,
  aeropress, cafetiere, espresso).
- Subscriptions offer 250g and 1kg with grind choices and weekly /
  fortnightly / monthly frequencies and terms; displayed prices can conflict
  (£9 vs "from £8" for 250g on the collection), so pricing is best avoided or
  qualified.
