# Wide Awake Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Wide Awake Coffee is a contemporary roastery in Brussels, Belgium. Its Shopify storefront sells espresso and filter coffees, brew bags, subscriptions, equipment and other accessories. The site says its coffees are released in 12 series and shows current coffees from producers and farms in Brazil, Colombia, Ethiopia and Peru; it also operates the St Catherine and Flagey café locations.

## Address

- Brussels, Belgium — the consulted site pages identify the roastery’s city but do not publish a separate roastery street address. The café addresses are intentionally not listed here.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Wide Awake says it sources through long-standing partnerships and names partner farms and producers on its shop pages, including Fazenda Nunes, Santa Hedwirges, Finca Bethel, Aricha Adorsi Station and Jhon Silva. No FOB, farm-gate or price-paid-to-producer figures are published on the consulted page.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The brand describes its approach as exploring contemporary coffee and the variety of specialty coffee. Its brew bags are single-origin coffees from favourite producers, dosed and ground into ready-to-brew bags.

## Scraping Quirks

- Several distinct coffees can share one Shopify product handle and are differentiated by product images. The scraper therefore emits one URL per Shopify image variant using the CDN `?v=` value (or image ID), maps stock to the image’s variants, and sends the product-page screenshot to AI extraction. It excludes discovery boxes, subscriptions, 6 kg packs, steep bags, cascara, matcha, hojicha and verbena.
