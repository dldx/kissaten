# Sweven Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Sweven Coffee is a specialty roaster in Bristol, United Kingdom, founded in
2019 by Jimmy & Aga Dimitrov after "a journey in coffee" that began in 2010.
The storefront is Shopify (`sweven-coffee-bristol.myshopify.com`), and the
focus is rare, exclusive and microlot coffees — recent featured releases
include Los Rodriguez Geisha Apex, La Florida Typica Mejorado and El Diviso
Ají, sold alongside a subscription. Sweven also runs a café at 12 North
Street, Bristol BS3 1HT (open daily), which serves a rotating menu of rare
and exclusive selections plus "a limited frozen offering that showcases the
pinnacle of each producer's craft".

## Address

- Bristol, United Kingdom — full roastery address not published on site;
  the site footer lists only the café at 12 North Street, Bristol BS3 1HT

## Roasting & Equipment

- Roasts on a **Loring S15 Falcon** (per their FAQ).
- Roast style is **"Light Nordic"** — sweetness and brightness with "nuanced
  floral acidity and a harmonious balance".
- Separate **filter** and **espresso** roast profiles: filter aims to reveal
  the coffee's "innate character and vibrant acidity"; espresso refines the
  profile for solubility and balance.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Wholesale roasting runs **Monday to Wednesday**, with orders shipped
  **Tuesday to Thursday**; wholesale minimum is **6 kg**, shipping costs
  calculated at checkout.
- Retail orders dispatch **Tuesday to Thursday**; UK retail ships via
  **Royal Mail Tracked 48** (2–3 working days from dispatch), wholesale via
  **UPS** (1–2 working days UK, 3–5 worldwide).
- Ships worldwide; postage costs are shown at checkout — no free-delivery
  minimum is published.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The name is Sweven's own shorthand for purpose: coffee as "community,
  conversation, and a legacy built on excellence" rather than just flavour.
- Unusually precise **resting recommendations**: at least **2 weeks rest for
  filter**, **3 weeks for espresso**, and 4–6 weeks for intricate varietals
  and processing methods (per their FAQ).
- Publishing detailed brew specs down to water ppm (85 ppm / 95 °C for
  filter, 115 ppm / 93 °C for espresso) and TDS ranges on the FAQ page.
- Sourcing trips are announced in advance because orders may dispatch a
  little earlier or later while the founders visit producer partners.
