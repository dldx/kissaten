# Archetype Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Archetype Coffee is a small-batch roaster based in Omaha, Nebraska, with coffee, subscriptions, gear, and three Omaha cafe locations. The current coffee menu includes rotating single-origin lots such as Burundi Ngozi, Kenya Kiambu, and Colombia Lucy Fernanda Galindez Pink Bourbon. The company also highlights Isaiah Sheese as 2023 US Barista Champion and World Barista finalist.

## Address

- Blackstone, 3926 Farnam Street, Omaha, Nebraska - United States
- Little Bohemia, 1419 South 13th Street, Omaha, Nebraska - United States
- Ashton at Millwork Commons, 1229 Millwork Avenue, Suite 101, Omaha, Nebraska - United States

## Schedules & Shipping

- The FAQ says orders enter a roasting queue and ship within 1-2 business days, Monday through Friday. It also says the coffee is roasted and shipped the same day and recommends resting it for a few days to a week. No free-shipping minimum is published in the FAQ checked.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Archetype describes coffee as having a serious, demanding side and a fun, ridiculous human side. Its shorthand is: "We take coffee seriously. Not ourselves." The stated purpose is to keep specialty coffee intentional and caring without becoming pretentious or cold.

## Scraping Quirks

- The Shopify scraper uses the curated `archetype-coffee` collection and fetches product pages because the products.json payload does not provide all bean detail. Subscription, drip-bag, bundle, cascara, and gift-card slugs are excluded from the bean catalogue.
