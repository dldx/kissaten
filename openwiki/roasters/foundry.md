# Foundry Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Foundry is a Sheffield specialty roaster on a Shopify storefront at foundrycoffeeroasters.com. The `coffee-beans` collection carries 9 coffees — Villa Pastora Colombia £15.50, Imbalu Uganda £13.50, Kii AA Kenya £16.50, El Indio Colombia, Etiopia Gesha El Salvador £18.50, Yulieth Mora Colombia £19.50, Coproca Rwanda, No. 9 Roast, Popayan Decaf — adventurous single origins plus a house espresso blend, in GBP.

## Address

- Sheffield, England — United Kingdom (full street address not published on site)

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Adventurous single-origin focus, organised into "Adventure"/"Comfort" groupings plus a house espresso blend (No. 9 Roast).
- Also sells green coffee in a separate collection.

## Scraping Quirks

- The curated `coffee-beans` collection (9, all `product_type` "Coffee Beans") avoids green-coffee/gear that would appear in `/collections/all`.
- Canonical product URLs are `/products/<handle>` (collection path stripped); JSON-only; currency pinned to GBP.
