# Artisan Roast Chile — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Artisan Roast Chile is a Chilean specialty coffee roaster whose Shopify shop is
hosted at shop.artisanroast.cl. Its registered store range is split between
origin coffees and blends. The accessible official pages did not expose a
verifiable roastery street address, equipment details or fulfilment schedule.

## Address

- Chile — full roastery address not published on the accessible site pages.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper merges the Shopify `origenes` and `blends` collections. Because
  the shop is Spanish-language, its AI extraction override always translates
  the extracted content to English.
