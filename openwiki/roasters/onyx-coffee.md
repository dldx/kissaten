# Onyx Coffee Lab EU — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Onyx Coffee Lab EU is the European storefront registered in Kissaten for Onyx
Coffee Lab. The scraper targets the EU Coffee collection on
`onyxcoffeelab.eu`; the registered roaster location is the Netherlands. The
consulted site was rate-limited during this research, so no founding, facility
or equipment claim is added here.

## Address

- Netherlands — full roastery address not published on the consulted site pages.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses the EU collection and Shopify product-link selectors. It
  excludes subscriptions, gifts, wholesale, equipment, accessories,
  merchandise, test roasts, advent calendars and sipping chocolate; products
  remaining in the coffee collection are sent to AI extraction.
