# Anonymous Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Anonymous Coffee Co. is an independent, family-owned and operated specialty
coffee roaster based in South Oxfordshire. "Phil and the team" source, roast
and deliver responsibly produced specialty coffee across the UK from a
Shopify storefront at [anonymouscoffee.co.uk](https://anonymouscoffee.co.uk).
They are a **Living Wage employer**, roast blends and single origins
(including the **No Name Signature Blend** and **Fernwood Espresso Blend**),
and run a roastery coffee bar; their coffee is also served at Roost on the
14th floor of Thames Tower, Reading (a tenant-only café, not the roastery)
and by partners around the south east and London.

## Address

- Unit 16, Manor Farm, Peppard Common, Henley-on-Thames, RG9 5LA — United
  Kingdom (roastery and coffee bar, open 8am–3pm Mon–Fri, 9am–12pm Saturday)

## Sustainability

- **Greenspark partnership**: every retail purchase contributes to global
  reforestation and plastic waste removal; the site banner notes donations
  "for every bean purchased".
- Works with **Eumelia** to offset its carbon footprint; has **begun the
  journey towards B Corp certification**.
- Nothing wasted: spent coffee grounds and roasting chaff are collected and
  reused by local growers and agriculturists, keeping them out of landfill.
- Packaging: plant-based certified compostable cups at the roastery and
  events, CO₂-compensated coffee bags, recyclable cardboard boxes and
  sugarcane-based retail mailing bags.
- Ships via Royal Mail, chosen for the UK's lowest average parcel carbon
  footprint.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- All coffees are traceable back to the producer or purchased by direct
  trade; they pay well above market price and are "working to provide a
  detailed breakdown of every penny (or cent) spent on each coffee" (per
  their site — the breakdown is not yet published).
- Named sustainability-forward producers: Daterra (Brazil's first
  Rainforest Alliance certified farm), Coope Dota (Costa Rica; the world's
  first carbon-neutral coffee processor in 2011), Racafé's CRECER programme
  (Colombia), Finca Liquidámbar (Honduras), Kinini (Rwanda), Kongo Coffee
  (Papua New Guinea), Fedecocagua (Guatemala) and Swiss Water decaf.
- Community giving: pledged **10% of all online orders to Project Waterfall**
  during UK Coffee Week (13–19 Oct 2025); commissioned and equipped an
  espresso bar for the Launchpad charity's training centre in Reading and
  run barista training sessions there.

## Schedules & Shipping

- **Free local delivery** to the RG1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 9, 30 and 31 postcodes,
  made weekly on Wednesday or Thursday; orders placed by 5pm Monday are
  fulfilled the same week, later orders the following week.
- Postal orders received before 12pm are shipped the next day
  (Monday–Friday only). No national free-delivery threshold is published.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- "To make great quality, great tasting coffee easy to enjoy; and to do some
  good in the process" — the homepage hero is literally poetry ("poetry to
  your taste buds" — "Breakfast in a cup, / Or a post lunch pick me up…").
- Only fresh-crop coffees, "rigorously selected" into a deliberately concise
  cross-section of origins.
- The flagship blend is literally called **No Name**.
- Runs pop-ups and events, brewing at local markets; wholesale arm covers
  equipment, training and support.

## Scraping Quirks

- The Shopify catalogue mixes beans with merch and brew gear (trucker cap,
  logo tee, Chemex/AeroPress/Hario brewers and filter papers). The scraper
  keeps an explicit exclusion list of those slugs (`trucker-cap`,
  `logo-tee`, `chemex`, `filter-papers`, `aeropress`, `hario`, `brewer`, …)
  so only beans land in the Kissaten catalogue.
