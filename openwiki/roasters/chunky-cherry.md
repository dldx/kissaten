# Chunky Cherry Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Chunky Cherry Coffee was a Dutch specialty coffee roaster. The registered
scraper description says the roaster is no longer active and the official site
was unavailable during this research, so current products, operating details
and company history could not be verified. The scraper declaration describes a
sustainability and alternative-variety focus, but that is registry metadata,
not a currently accessible official-site claim.

## Address

- Netherlands — full roastery address and city not published in the consulted
  registry or accessible site pages.

## Scraping Quirks

- The unavailable scraper targets three collection URLs for Arabica, dark roast
  and fine Robusta. It expects product cards with the `cc-product-card` class,
  drops cards containing “Sold out”, and excludes URL patterns for equipment,
  subscriptions, gifts, merchandise and capsules. It uses AI extraction rather
  than a structured commerce endpoint.
