# Jae's RoaStory — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Jae's RoaStory (jaesroastory.co.uk) is a UK specialty coffee roaster on a
Shopify storefront, rebranded from "Jae's Roaster". The live catalogue is a
compact literature-themed range: three signature espresso blends — The SciFi
Blend (Light), The Fiction Blend (Medium Light) and The Classics Blend
(Medium Dark) — plus the True Crime Blend decaf. Each bean ships as 250g /
500g / 1kg whole-bean variants in GBP, priced at £12 for 250g.

## Address

- Full street address not published on site — West Yorkshire, United Kingdom
  ("Roasted locally in West Yorkshire" per home-page copy). Jae's RoaStory is a
  trading name of Prototypist Keyboards Limited, Company No 12292970, per the
  site footer.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Carbon-neutral positioning: the company calculates its carbon output and
  has implemented offsets; it partners with farmers who prioritise organic
  and sustainable farming practices (per the About Us page).
