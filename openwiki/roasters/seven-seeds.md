# Seven Seeds — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Seven Seeds is a Melbourne specialty coffee company established in 2007. It
sources, roasts, wholesales, brews and retails coffee through a Shopify storefront
offering single origins, year-round house blends, decaf, subscriptions and brewing
products. The business also operates cafes and a Fairfield bakery; the roastery and
head office are listed separately at 7 Montefiore Street.

## Address

- 7 Montefiore St, Fairfield VIC 3078, Australia.

## Sustainability

- Seven Seeds frames its sourcing as a long-term effort to keep producing coffee
  viable and publishes an annual impact report covering sourcing, relationships and
  sustainability. The FY25 report records the above prosperity-pricing payment as
  a producer-support initiative; no broader unquantified sustainability claims are
  included here.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Seven Seeds says it began publishing the price paid directly to producers
  (farmgate) in 2018, for both single origins and blends. Its FY25 Impact Report
  reports 107,035 kg sourced from 12 origins and 34 producer relationships, and
  provides coffee-price detail in the linked PDF, including a weighted-average FOB
  graph in USD/lb.
- The FY25 report describes buying most of Willyan Zambrano and Maria's Pink
  Bourbon harvest, plus an additional 15% payment generated from sales of the fifth
  Pink Bourbon release, paid directly to them through Caravela.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Standard shipping Australia-wide is free over AUD 60. Subscriptions receive free
  standard shipping. Orders are usually completed in 24-48 hours for local pickup
  from Fairfield Monday-Friday, 08:00-15:00; no roast-day cadence or international
  shipping schedule was found on the reviewed pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Seven Seeds says its name refers to the seven coffee seeds Baba Budan carried from
  Yemen to India. The company describes its culture as transparent and open to
  everyone, and keeps both single origins and house blends in its core range.
