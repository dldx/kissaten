# Exemplar — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Exemplar (www.exemplarcoffee.com) is a UK specialty coffee company that styles
itself "Speciality Coffee Rooted In Research", pairing its coffee with a
printed and digital quarterly magazine (the "Exemplar Quarterly"). It sells a
small whole-bean range on a Squarespace storefront in GBP — currently the Daily
and Chronicle blends, the Shyira (Rwanda) and Unión San Pedro (Mexico) single
origins — plus the magazine, back issues and quarterly subscriptions. Town of
operation is not stated on the site.

## Address

- United Kingdom — full street address not published on site (town not stated).

## Scraping Quirks

- Domain correction: the canonical site is the .com (www.exemplarcoffee.com);
  the .co.uk variant is NXDOMAIN.
- The catalogue is filtered to the four whole-bean coffees; magazine issues and
  subscriptions are excluded.
- Free shipping on all UK orders over £30 (per the site's announcement bar).
