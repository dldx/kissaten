# BOB Coffee Lab — Roaster Profile

## Overview

BOB Coffee Lab is a specialty coffee roaster in Bucharest, Romania. Its site
describes a business that began in 2017 and combines its own cafés with a
roastery; the English shop is a catalogue of coffee, gear and merchandise. BOB
describes coffees selected from Panama to Indonesia and an emphasis on constant
recipe and product quality.

## Address

- Bucharest, Romania — full roastery address not published on the consulted
  roastery page; the site's footer gives the business address as Charles de
  Gaulle 3, 011857, Bucharest, but does not label it as the roasting site.

## Roasting & Equipment

- BOB says the Head Roaster listens for first crack and develops coffee through
  repeated tests, cuppings, profiling and experiments. No roaster brand, model
  or capacity is published on the consulted pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The roastery page says the main roasting days are Tuesday and Thursday. No
  dispatch timetable, destination-specific shipping rates or free-delivery
  minimum was found on the consulted pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The site's description of the roastery treats listening for first crack and
  iterative cupping/profile work as the basis of its roasting philosophy rather
  than presenting a fixed roast-level system.
