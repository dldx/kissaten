# Rosso Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Rosso Coffee Roasters is a Calgary, Alberta specialty roaster founded in 2007 and
roasting in Calgary since 2012. Its Shopify storefront offers a year-round Core
Lineup, seasonal single origins, Gold Series rare lots, instant coffee and
subscriptions, alongside Rosso cafes. Rosso describes its work as considered
sourcing, meticulous roasting and partnership, and says a second roastery in
Houston is forthcoming.

## Address

- Calgary, Alberta, Canada — full roastery address not published on the reviewed site pages.

## Sustainability

- Rosso identifies itself as B Corp certified and says the certification reflects
  sustainable practices, responsible sourcing and efforts to reduce environmental
  impact. Its Supplier Code of Conduct requires environmentally responsible
  practices, waste reduction, resource conservation and control of emissions and
  wastewater.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Rosso describes its coffee as sustainably sourced and its site highlights coffee
  partnerships. Its Supplier Code of Conduct sets expectations for labour and human
  rights, safe conditions, ethical conduct, environmental protection and community
  engagement across suppliers. No lot-level purchase prices were found on the
  reviewed pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Rosso processes and ships orders within 1-3 days and roasts daily. Delivery is
  free within Calgary city limits over CAD 20, and across Canada and the USA over
  CAD 75. International customers choose among rates at checkout; customs and
  import duties are paid by the recipient.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Rosso's public positioning is to make specialty coffee approachable rather than
  precious. Its coffee range includes everyday Core coffees as well as small-format
  Gold Series lots and fermentation-focused releases.
