# Stables Coffee Co. — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Stables Coffee Co. (stablescoffee.co.uk) is a UK specialty coffee roaster with
cafés in Arborfield and Buckler's Park, Berkshire (per their site). The
Arborfield café — "where it all began" — is also home to the roastery. The
online catalogue is a compact range of four coffees — Bondi Blend, Didgeri
Decaf, Great Ocean Roast and Uluru — sold from a Shopify storefront in GBP.

## Address

- Unit 1, Bridle Road, Arborfield Green, Reading, Berkshire RG2 9QT, United
  Kingdom — the Arborfield café, which is also home to the roastery (published
  on the site's locations page).
