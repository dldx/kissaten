# The Barn — Roaster Profile

## Overview

The Barn is a specialty coffee roaster founded in Berlin in 2010. Its online shop is a Shopify storefront with filter, espresso, decaf, ready-to-brew and coffee-bundle collections, alongside cafés in Berlin, Munich, London and elsewhere. The roaster presents its coffees as light-roasted, single-origin lots, including the permanent Elemental and Genesis coffees and the newer Atlas core coffee.

## Address

- Lübarser Str. 40-46/Haus 3, 13435 Berlin, Germany.

## Sustainability

- The Barn says its climate project with Daterra, Brazil, will plant **40,000 native trees** over five years on **25 hectares**, with Daterra managing and protecting them for at least ten years.
- At origin it describes support for water-filtration systems, eco-pulpers, agronomy education and community projects. Examples named on its sustainability page include wastewater treatment in Brazil, the AMACA women-farmer project in Colombia, drying facilities in Ethiopia, processing upgrades in Kenya and a late-harvest auction in Honduras.
- The Barn also describes social-impact work in Berlin, including fundraising for the Kältebus and free barista training for people with refugee status.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The Barn says it works with local partners and builds long-term producer relationships, giving each coffee “a face” in the market. It says it often pays multiple times the Fair Trade price for quality coffees, but the pages checked do not publish per-lot FOB or farm-gate figures.
- Its stated sourcing approach is single-origin rather than blending coffees, so the farm and producer identity remain visible to customers.

## Roasting & Equipment

- The Barn says all coffees are roasted manually on a **tailormade Probat drum roaster**. Its method applies heat gently to develop the inside while keeping the exterior light and clean.
- Baristas evaluate each roast on the company's coffee bars as part of quality control; the roaster also says it hosts weekly cuppings and workshops.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders are packed and shipped within **48 hours after roasting**. The contact page says orders are usually prepared and picked up on the next working day.
- The published shipping policy gives these examples: Germany **€2.00** by DHL Kleinpaket (0-0.6 kg), **€4.50** by UPS Standard (0-5 kg), or **€4.90** by DHL Paket (0-5 kg), with free shipping from **€45.00**; the stated delivery time is **1-3 working days**.
- Other published destination tiers include the United Kingdom at **€10.50** DHL Kleinpaket, **€11.00** UPS (0-1 kg), **€14.50** UPS (1-5 kg), or **€12.50** FedEx (1-5 kg), normally **2-4 working days**; the policy does not state a UK free-shipping threshold. Australia and New Zealand are listed at **€48.00** by FedEx (0-5 kg), with delivery in **4-6 working days**.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The Barn's stated aim is to preserve the identity of each lot through light roasting, precision and long-term relationships rather than trends or hype. It says it does not blend its coffees in order to honour farm work and make terroir legible in the cup.
