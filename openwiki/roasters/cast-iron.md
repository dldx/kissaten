# Cast Iron — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Cast Iron Coffee Roasters is a family business in Chichester, West Sussex,
founded in 2014 by husband-and-wife team Tilly and Guy Spurr (founding year
per third-party directories; the site itself says the family business was
"started with a vision of a family business focused squarely on producing
the best quality speciality coffee possible"). The Shopify storefront at
[castironroasters.com](https://castironroasters.com) sells seasonal single
origins plus espresso and filter blends, alongside a **Black Label
Collection** of rare, competition-level lots and a La Marzocco machine
partnership. The roasting workshop at The Mill doubles as an open café where
you can watch the whole roasting operation, barista training and the QC lab.
Head roaster Guy is assisted by David Leeper (Head of Wholesale & QC), a
licensed AST who placed in the Top 10 of both the UK Barista Championships
and Brewers Cup.

## Address

- Units 11 & 12 The Mill, Stane Street, Maudlin, Chichester, West Sussex
  PO18 0FF — United Kingdom (roasting workshop, café, training and dispatch;
  Companies House records the same address as the registered office).

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Long-running direct-trade relationship with **Cafe Lumbus** in Antioquia,
  Colombia, source of their Finca La Colombia coffee; the homepage calls it
  "our stunning new direct trade relationship coffee".
- The site's stated stance: knowing coffee's origin and that it is
  responsibly sourced "is of vital importance — it often costs more this way
  but this ultimately means a sustainable source of production AND
  better-tasting coffee". No price figures are published.

## Roasting & Equipment

- All coffee is **hand roasted to order**, roast date stamped on the pack.
- The roastery page shows a Giesen lineup — **W15, W6 and WPG1** — in the
  open-plan workshop (per their site; third-party directories describe
  earlier twin 15kg Toper drum roasters).
- Dual roast profiles: the same coffee is offered as a **light "filter
  roast"** and a **medium "espresso roast"**, deliberately not one profile
  "covering all bases".

## Schedules & Shipping

- Roasting Monday through Thursday.
- Hand roasted to order with the aim of delivery **within 3 working days**;
  per their FAQ, UK standard is Royal Mail Tracked 48 (~2 working days),
  Tracked 24 at extra cost, and orders over 3kg ship with DPD. Overseas
  orders: allow 7–10 days.
- Subscription orders ship in the third week of every month.
- Rest guidance: filter coffees 5–7 days off roast, espresso 7–10 days;
  optimum at days 7–30, fine for up to 3 months.
- No free-delivery threshold is published.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Motto: "It's just coffee, but then it is so much more than that…." — and
  the sign-off "nobody ever argues with deliciousness!"
- "Consistency of quality is the aim", achieved by roasting "both
  scientifically and sympathetically".
- Founder quirks: Guy has raced the Nürburgring 24hr three times ("his
  greatest achievement is 2nd place at the Rackham Fete cheese straw
  competition"); Tilly's sister has a coffee farm in Panama producing honey;
  David has kept a sourdough starter alive.
- Beyond coffee they supply La Marzocco machines, Mork hot chocolate and a
  Storm Tea range, and run barista courses.

## Scraping Quirks

- **Deliberate double listings**: because each coffee is released in both a
  filter roast and an espresso roast, the catalogue contains near-duplicate
  products per origin ("some coffees appear to be listed twice" — their
  roast-profiles page) — worth remembering when matching or deduping beans.
- The scraper keeps a long hand-maintained exclusion list so the Storm Tea
  range, Mork hot chocolate, equipment (La Marzocco, Wilfa, V60 gear), merch
  and the Roaster's Choice subscription never enter the catalogue.
