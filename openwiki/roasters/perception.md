# Perception Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Perception Coffee Roasters (perceptioncoffee.co.uk) is a small Manchester
specialty roaster on a Shopify storefront in GBP. The catalogue is tiny —
four whole-bean coffees — freshly roasted in small batches with a mix of
filter and espresso options, delivered in 24–48 hours. The business also
sells consultancy alongside its bean range.

## Address

- Full street address not published on site — Manchester, England, United
  Kingdom.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Freshly roasted in small batches.
- Items dispatched in 1–2 working days.
- Free shipping offered (homepage banner).
