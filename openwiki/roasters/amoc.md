# AMOC Coffee / A Matter of Concrete — Roaster Profile

## Overview

A.M.O.C. (A Matter of Concrete) is a specialty coffee roastery based in the
Van Nelle Factory in Rotterdam, Netherlands, founded in 2021 by Rob. Its
WooCommerce storefront combines coffee with brewing equipment, objects and
collaborations. Coffee is organised into classic, uncommon and mono/rare
series; the company says its coffees are fully traceable to farm and that it
works directly with many farmers or processing plants.

## Address

- Van Nelle Factory, Rotterdam — Netherlands; the roastery page identifies the building but does not publish a street/postcode for the roasting premises.

## Sustainability

- AMOC says its energy supplier has Gold Standard certification, with CO₂-neutral
  gas and electricity from renewable sources. Its reusable PET AMOC jars are
  designed to be refilled; refills use a vacuum-packed food-grade PP bag that
  the site says is 40% lighter than coffee bags and easier to recycle.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Selection is based on blind cuppings, and the company says it maintains strong
  supplier relationships and direct work with many farms or processing plants.
  No per-kg FOB or farm-gate prices were published on the pages checked.

## Roasting & Equipment

- AMOC identifies its roaster as a **Loring S35 Kestrel**. The roastery also has
  a Sovda colour sorter, a quality-control space and a tasting room.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The site advertises free shipping at **€50+ in NL/BE**, **€100+ in the EU/UK**
  and **€1500+ in the US/Canada/Asia**. No roast or dispatch day was published
  on the accessible pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Its stated approach is “concrete decisions based on vision and hard data,”
  with classic coffees alongside “uncommon” lots selected for unusual variety,
  origin, processing or exclusivity.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses Playwright because the WooCommerce coffee archive is
  JavaScript-rendered, applies custom product-link selectors, and excludes
  `brew-bag` and `filter-bundle` products.
