# Bjorn — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Bjorn Speciality Coffee is a speciality coffee roaster and café in Holmfirth
(West Yorkshire), at 5A Hollowgate Yard. It rebranded in October 2025 from
"Yellow B Roasters" after the shop was taken over by Danelaw Coffee, with
roasting moved to their roastery in Meltham Mills. "Bjorn" is Old Norse for
"bear" — a nod to the Danelaw — and the brand previously used bear-themed blend
names such as Br Necessities and Brown Br.

## Address

- 5A Hollowgate Yard, Holmfirth HD9 2DG, West Yorkshire — United Kingdom.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The rename to Bjorn avoided clashing with the Derby brunch chain "Bear",
  so diners wouldn't mistake one coffee for the other.
- Blends retain a Bear lineage: house espresso **Bjorn Out Of Necessity**
  (ex Br Necessities), the **Brown Ben** gentle dark roast (ex Brown Br), and
  Red Panda unchanged.
- House blends plus single origins (Banko Gotiti Ethiopia, a strawberry
  co-ferment honey "Strawberry Beret", etc.).
- Aurora decaf.

## Scraping Quirks
