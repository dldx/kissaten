# West Berkshire Roastery — Roaster Profile

## Overview

West Berkshire Roastery (wbroast.co.uk) is a Berkshire coffee roaster at a
Lower Way Farm unit in Thatcham, on a Shopify storefront in GBP. It sells
roastery blends, single origins, new arrivals, decaf and brew bags, plus
wholesale and sample-roasting services, and also trades as Berkshire Coffee
Co. The catalogue holds 12 saved coffees plus one tasting kit (the Ultimate
Taster Pack).

## Address

- Unit 1, Lower Way Farm, Lower Way, Thatcham, Berkshire, RG19 3TL — United
  Kingdom.

## Scraping Quirks

- Domain correction: the long domain westberkshireroastery.co.uk is dead; the
  recovered live shop is wbroast.co.uk.
- Also brands as Berkshire Coffee Co. (same roastery, different brand name).
- The Ultimate Taster Pack is a tasting-kit product: it must flow through the
  review queue (`is_tasting_kit` / `requires_review`), never excluded.
