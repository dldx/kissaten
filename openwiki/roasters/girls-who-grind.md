# Girls Who Grind Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Girls Who Grind Coffee is a UK specialty coffee roaster with an explicit
"Grown by Women" positioning, on a Shopify storefront at
girlswhogrindcoffee.com. Its curated `coffee` collection carries 9 products
priced in GBP, e.g. the Girl Crush house blend, Oh My Goth, DREAMY DECAF,
One Eye Open Half Caff, Café Femenino Peru, Back To The Filter, the Nafeesa
Esmail Specialty Robusta and Rise & Grind.

## Address

- United Kingdom (city not stated on the site's contact or about pages).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Free UK shipping on all orders over £30 ("FREE SHIPPING!" on the homepage).

## Philosophy & Quirks

- "Grown by Women" positioning — "Ridiculously good specialty coffee, grown
  by women. ALWAYS."
- Café Femenino Peru (women-grown coffee certification programme).
- A Specialty Robusta line (Nafeesa Esmail Specialty Robusta / Canephora) and
  a half-caff option (One Eye Open Half Caff).

## Scraping Quirks

- The curated `coffee` collection holds 10 raw products; the
  "Test Roast - Specialty Robusta / Canephora" product is dropped by the base
  `test-roast` exclusion, leaving 9 in the catalogue.
- Canonical product URLs are `/products/<handle>` (the collection segment is
  stripped from the products.json URLs).
- Currency is pinned to GBP.
