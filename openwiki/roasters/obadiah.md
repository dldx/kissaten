# Obadiah Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Obadiah is a modern specialty roastery "rooted in Edinburgh since 2017",
founded by Sam and Alice Young. Sam learned his trade in his native Perth,
Australia (including time at Five Senses Coffee, per interviews) before moving
to Scotland. The offering is resolutely seasonal and single-origin-led, with
curated packs, a "Solasta" themed subscription and steeped-coffee bags alongside
the core beans. Storefront is Shopify at
[obadiahcoffee.com](https://obadiahcoffee.com).

## Address

- Unit 4-5 The Arches, Abbeyhill, Edinburgh, EH8 8EE, Scotland, United Kingdom
  (roastery sales counter open weekdays; card only).

## Sustainability

- Packaging is fully recyclable paper and plastic (per their FAQ, which still
  encourages checking local recycling acceptance).

## Roasting & Equipment

- "Gentle touch" roasting: parameters adjusted per lot to highlight its natural
  character, with each coffee roasted for versatility across filter and
  espresso; a dedicated QC cupping room backs the sourcing decisions.
- The roastery operates under working railway arches — trains rattle overhead
  (per their contact page and local press).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders dispatched within 1–2 working days of purchase; orders can be changed
  or cancelled before 9am on dispatch days.
- **Free UK shipping on orders over £35** (sitewide announcement bar).
- EU prices include import taxes; for the rest of the world, duties and taxes
  are payable by the recipient. Coffee itself is zero-rated for EU duty/VAT.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Tagline: "**Clean and expressive**".
- They recommend resting sealed bags for **a minimum of 21 days** after roast
  before brewing — an unusually long and prominently advised rest window.
- Subscriptions are fully curated by the team around a monthly theme or featured
  producer; individual coffees cannot be swapped or subscribed to, and coffee
  is only ever dispatched whole bean.
- One of the last coffees to sell out is often the story: when a lot sells out,
  they aim to buy from the same partner producers at the following harvest.
