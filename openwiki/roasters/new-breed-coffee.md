# New Breed Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

New Breed Coffee is a London-based specialty roaster redefining what
Vietnamese coffee can be — "the Robusta rebrand: from commodity to
speciality". Founded by **Chris Chung**, a British-Vietnamese entrepreneur
whose parents fled Vietnam by boat and rebuilt their lives in the UK
(Companies House lists incorporation as New Breed Coffee Ltd in November
2023), it partners with pioneering smallholder farmers across Vietnam to
produce expressive Arabicas and bold Robustas, roasted in the UK in small
batches. The Shopify storefront is at
[newbreed.coffee](https://newbreed.coffee).

## Address

- New Breed Coffee Ltd, 3rd Floor, 86-90 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NE,
  United Kingdom (published company address; coffee is "roasted in the UK"
  per their site, with no separate roastery address published).

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Direct partnerships with named Vietnamese producers, profiled on the Learn
  page: **Mr Toi** (Central Highlands, leading a small collective elevating
  Robusta through modern fermentation), **Will Nguyen** (second-generation
  producer experimenting with selective harvesting and controlled
  fermentation) and **Lim and Marian** (highland Arabica lots blending Lim's
  K'Ho heritage with Marian's technical expertise).
- Emphasis on regenerative agriculture and experimental processing (honey,
  anaerobic, "supernatural" open fermentation, washed, semi-washed).

## Roasting & Equipment

- Every batch is profiled on a **Giesen W15A** roaster, using "measured
  control and sensory intuition" — lighter roasts for floral clarity and
  fruit, medium/darker profiles for depth and body.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Small-batch roasting; orders are usually dispatched the **next working
  day** (weekend orders ship the following Monday).
- UK only, via **Royal Mail Tracked 48** (2–4 working days from dispatch);
  international shipping not yet offered.
- **£3.55 flat rate under £30; free UK delivery on orders £30 and over.**

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Founder story is central: "New Breed is my way of honouring that journey —
  a bridge between cultures, crafted through coffee" — belonging, identity
  and reshaping how Vietnamese coffee is seen globally.
- Leads "the Robusta rebrand": selective picking, precision fermentation and
  small-batch drying to show Robusta's depth, sweetness and complexity.
- Range is split into Classic (Core / Hero) collections; coffees include an
  espresso-friendly tier (Explorer, Voyager) and expressive filter lots.

## Scraping Quirks

- **Sold-out filtering at listing time**: product links carrying the
  `product__sold-out` class are skipped, so only in-stock coffees are
  discovered.
- **Bundle exclusion**: URLs containing `bundle` or `ultimate-collection`
  are filtered out (multi-bag gift sets rather than single beans).
- Product pages are narrowed to the `section.single-product` block before
