# Potterbeans — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Potterbeans (potterbeans.coffee, not .co.uk) is a Cornwall, UK roastery that
pairs wood-roasted coffee with hand-thrown pottery, selling through a Shopify
storefront in GBP. The whole-bean catalogue (`all-coffee`) carries 41 coffees —
single origins, blends and decaf plus a Speciality Mixed Box — with curated
subscription options. Reviews note beans are roasted freshly after ordering.

## Address

- Full street address not published on site — Cornwall, England, United
  Kingdom.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Roasts, hand-packs and ships all orders from the Cornwall roastery.
- Standard UK delivery is £4 and usually takes 2–4 working days (Royal Mail).
- Free UK shipping on orders over £50.
- Wood-fired roasts are shipped on a Thursday.

## Scraping Quirks

- Canonical domain is potterbeans.**coffee** (the checklist's `.co.uk` is
  stale — a domain correction).
- 41 products land from the `all-coffee` collection; 4 are tasting-kit /
  sampler products flagged `is_tasting_kit` / `requires_review` for the admin
