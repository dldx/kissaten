# Onibus — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Onibus is a Tokyo, Japan specialty coffee company whose Shopify storefront sells
single origins, the Original Blend, subscriptions, gifts and brewing products. It
also supports wholesale cafe operations and has venues in Japan and overseas. Its
stated vision is "Connecting people through coffee" and its broader aim is to enrich
cities and everyday life through coffee.

## Address

- Tokyo, Japan — full roastery address not published on the reviewed site pages.

## Sustainability

- Onibus describes circularity as its sustainability approach and publishes work
  that includes the Soil Project in Rwanda, which composts coffee-processing
  by-products to help regenerate soil and biodiversity. It also says it measures
  business CO2 emissions through a carbon audit. In July 2026, Onibus announced
  that it had obtained B Corp certification.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Onibus says it visits producing countries and, where possible, buys through direct
  relationships. Its sourcing criteria include producers supporting education for
  workers' children, fair working conditions, community livelihoods and organic
  fertiliser use. The site publishes transparency reports covering its purchases,
  including reports for 2023-2025.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The storefront states that domestic shipping in Japan is free for orders of
  JPY 5,500 or more. No roast cadence, dispatch days, delivery times or
  international shipping tiers were found on the reviewed pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Onibus combines retail coffee with wholesale beans, equipment, cafe-opening
  support and consulting, while framing coffee as a connection between producers,
  servers and drinkers.

## Scraping Quirks

- Registry key is `onibus`. The Shopify JSON content is authored mainly in Japanese,
  so the scraper always requests English translation and rewrites collection URLs
  to `/en/products/<handle>`. Its defensive exclude list currently omits
  subscriptions, cold-brew packs, tasting sets, drip bags and gifts from the coffee
  collection.
