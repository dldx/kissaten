# Barista Culture — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Barista Culture Coffee (Barista Culture LTD) is a Milton Keynes specialty coffee
roaster, coffee shop and cocktail bar, partnered with the Blackened Sun Brewing
Company micro-brewery — the two share the bar, roastery and taproom at Unit 3,
Heathfield Industrial Park in Stacey Bushes. The storefront is Shopify at
[baristaculture.co.uk](https://www.baristaculture.co.uk). Per Companies House
(via third-party listings) the company was incorporated on 6 January 2025;
co-founder Tom Cozens — per their own Martini Club page, a lifelong beverage
professional with a background in global beverage innovation — hosts the site's
distinctive "Martini Club" events. The range is single-origin heavy and process-led
(Ethiopian anaerobic naturals, thermal-shock Sidamo, a Diego Samuel Bermudez
Castillo), with a rotating "Coffee of the Month".

## Schedules & Shipping

- Free delivery on orders over £20 (site-wide checkout banner); beans are
  "posted through your letterbox".
- A "Subscribe & Save" plan is offered; no roast cadence or per-region rates
  are published.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Product names are music and pop-culture riffs: **Shreddy Krueger**,
  **Without Me**, **Lost Ones**, **November Has Come**, **Funk Freakers**,
  **jöy**, **Happiness for dogs**, and a Coffee of the Month currently named
  **Straight Outta Milton Keynes** (El Salvador Los Angeles Pacamara,
  hydro washed) — the handle is `coffee-of-the-month-straight-outta-stacey-bushes`.
- **The Martini Club**: immersive, intimate tasting-menu evenings with Martini
  pairings, co-hosted by chef James Carr ("cooked over coal") at £165 a seat —
  volumes have been held at Barista Culture itself and at Andes Project Coffee.
- Wholesale goes beyond coffee: Barista Culture supplies Blackened Sun beer,
  runs barista training and equipment supply, and works with Lupo Ceramics on
  custom functional coffee ceramics. They also offer beverage-consultancy
  services across "coffee, non-coffee and alcoholic beverages".
