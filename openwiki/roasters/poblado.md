# Poblado Coffi — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Poblado Coffi (pobladocoffi.co.uk) is a North Wales specialty coffee roaster in
the Nantlle Valley / Eryri (Snowdonia) area. Its story page describes the
journey "from the 'Cwt Coffi' at the bottom of the garden to the old quarryman's
barracks in the small village of Nantlle", where quality of produce and
relationships with customers, suppliers and producers go together. It sells a
whole-bean catalogue from a Shopify storefront in GBP.
