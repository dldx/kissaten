# Catalyst Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Catalyst Coffee is a UK speciality coffee roaster and café on a Shopify
storefront at catalyst.coffee (trading as "Catalyst Cafe & Coffee Roasters").
Its "Coffee" collection carries 10 products — blends, single origins, one
decaf — priced in GBP, e.g. AFTERMATH £12, MAVERICK £12.80, LUCID TRANCE decaf
£12.80, COCO JAMBO £22, CLOUD NINE £16.80 and WHIPLASH £18. The current range
is named after music-inspired themes (e.g. COCO JAMBO, CLOUD NINE).
The company describes itself as a blending-first roaster that has been
"hustling for more than ten years", with every offering supporting two
different farmers.

## Address

- United Kingdom (city not stated on the site's contact or about pages).

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Explicitly blend-driven: single origins "get the hype", but Catalyst blends
  origins, processes and varieties to build flavour profiles, and says every
  offering supports two different farmers ("twice the collaboration, twice the
  impact").
- Music-inspired product naming (e.g. COCO JAMBO, CLOUD NINE, WHIPLASH).

## Scraping Quirks

- The `coffee` collection is the master beans set (10 products, all typed
  "Retail Coffee") — it already contains the decaf (LUCID TRANCE) and blend
  (e.g. COCO JAMBO) items.
- Canonical product URLs are `/products/<handle>` (no collection segment).
- Currency is pinned to GBP because Shopify Markets can geo-locate the
  datacenter IP to a non-GBP market and convert the prices.
