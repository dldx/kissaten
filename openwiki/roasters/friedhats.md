# Friedhats — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Friedhats is a micro-roastery based in Amsterdam. Its official story says it
sells consistently high-quality coffee sourced directly from producers and
trusted Europe-wide importers, and its shop is a Shopify storefront in EUR.
The business also operates Fuku Café in Amsterdam; the café is not the
roastery address below.

## Address

- Amsterdam, Netherlands — full roastery street address not published on site.

## Sustainability

- Friedhats says sustainability is an ongoing process and that it wants its
  packaging to be reused, refilled or at least recycled. The site does not
  claim that the packaging already meets all three goals.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The company says it buys directly from producers and from trusted importers
  across Europe, with the aim of improving the lives of coffee growers and
  producers. No FOB, farm-gate or price-paid-to-producer figures were
  published on the fetched pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Roasting takes place Monday, Tuesday and Thursday. Orders are shipped within
  three working days, Monday–Friday; filter coffee is shipped up to seven days
  after roasting and espresso up to ten days after roasting, allowing 12–24
  hours of degassing.
- Netherlands: DHL Parcel costs €3.95; orders over €25 ship free. Amsterdam
  local delivery by bicycle is also offered.
- Eurozone: orders over €75 ship free. The policy separately lists Switzerland
  free over CHF 150 and Norway free over €150, with country rates (for example
  Germany €8, Belgium €7.90, France €14 and Sweden €20) when below the
  threshold. The UK rate is €16 with a maximum order value of £135.
- Outside Europe, rates are calculated at checkout by weight; the policy lists
  the countries served and says US orders resumed 27 February 2026.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Friedhats describes its method as sourcing for flavour and roasting to
  accentuate natural characteristics. Its stated goal is coffee that tastes
  great consistently, with transparency and honesty.
