# Kaffa Roastery — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Kaffa Roastery is a Helsinki, Finland specialty roaster with a Shopify storefront offering coffee, cascara, equipment, gifts, workshops and subscriptions. Its homepage frames the company around Finland’s high coffee consumption and the ambition that Finnish drinkers should also have access to excellent coffee. The range includes recurring blends such as Aina and Herra Korppi alongside espresso and decaffeinated coffees.

## Address

- Helsinki, Finland — full roastery address not published on the consulted homepage.

## Sustainability

- Kaffa’s homepage says its coffee is ethically and sustainably grown and roasted and states the principle that good coffee should be good for the drinker, its makers and the planet. The consulted page did not expose further measurable programme details.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Free delivery is advertised for orders over €30. The consulted homepage did not publish roast days, dispatch cadence or destination-specific shipping rates.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The shop includes a limited-edition Fermentation Project set made with James Hoffmann and presents coffees with Finnish names as well as globally sourced single origins.
