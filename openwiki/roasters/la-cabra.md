# La Cabra — Roaster Profile

## Overview

La Cabra is a modern specialty coffee company established in Aarhus, Denmark,
in 2012; it began sourcing and roasting its own coffee in 2013. It has since
moved its main roastery to Copenhagen and expanded with locations in New York,
Bangkok and Oman. The online shop is Shopify, with seasonal coffee, single
serve bags, subscriptions, bakery and brewing equipment.

## Address

- Copenhagen, Denmark — full roastery address not published on site.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- La Cabra travels to origin, works directly with producers and with a small
  number of specialist importing companies, and says it visits origin
  countries several times a year. Its sourcing pages document producer and
  community stories in Colombia, Rwanda and Ethiopia; no FOB or
  price-paid-to-producer figures were published on the reviewed pages.

## Roasting & Equipment

- La Cabra makes roasting decisions at the cupping table and develops one
  profile intended to reveal each coffee's character rather than separating
  profiles into filter and espresso. The site does not identify the current
  roaster model or capacity.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders ship within 1–3 business days from the Copenhagen roastery. La Cabra
  ships coffee no more than 14 days from roast, and says subscription delivery
  is free to the EU and selected countries outside the EU.
- Shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination and are shown at
  checkout. Import duties, taxes and brokerage fees are not included.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The company emphasizes clean, bright cups, seasonal availability and
  respecting the producer's raw material through a transparent expression of
  its origin.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper reads Shopify's `coffee` collection, strips collection segments
  from product URLs and removes a broad equipment, apparel, chocolate,
  brewing-accessory and bundle list. It keeps coffee single-serve products
