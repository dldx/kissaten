# Silver Oak Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Silver Oak Coffee is an independent roastery in the Cambridgeshire Fens,
started by Bruce as a garage-roasting hobby in 2013; after training at the
London School of Coffee and a year of practice roasting, the business began
trading in 2014, built up through farmers' markets across the East of England
(Ely, Cambridge, Stamford, Newmarket, St Ives, Bury St Edmunds). Bruce and
Susanne are the owners; the shop at [silveroakcoffee.co.uk](https://silveroakcoffee.co.uk)
is a Shopify storefront. They also run two cafés in Ely — **The Yard** on
Newnham St (opened March 2023, an outdoor site of five shipping containers,
awarded Cambridgeshire's Best Independent Café 2024 and a Tripadvisor
Travellers' Choice winner 2024) and **The Rig**, their two-storey market
trailer on Ely Market Square where customers can sit on the roof overlooking
Ely Cathedral.

## Address

- Silver Oak Coffee Ltd, Unit 1a, Saxon Business Park, Littleport, Cambs,
  CB6 1XX — United Kingdom (working roastery in the Fens; the roastery page
  notes it is "very much a working unit not a retail site").

## Sourcing & Transparency

- All beans are sourced through **Mercanta – The Coffee Hunters**, whose
  buyers visit farms to verify sustainable and ethical practices.
- Sources seasonally from ethically and sustainably run small independent
  farms and co-operatives; current offer spans Kenya, El Salvador, organic
  Peru, Brazil and Costa Rica (e.g. the Aquiares house espresso).

## Roasting & Equipment

- Three roasters: a large production roaster for wholesale volumes, a small
  roaster taking 6 kg at a time for microlots and filter roasts, and a
  100 g sample roaster for test roasts.
- Three team members are trained to roast under Bruce's lead; quality is
  checked with cuppings every week.
- Roasts to order and ships each Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Roast days are **Monday, Wednesday and Friday** — orders placed by 11am on
  a roast day are dispatched the same day.
- Per their shipping policy: stock items within 10 days of ordering, though
  usually dispatched within 48 hours, and fresh coffee within 24 hours of
  the next roasting day; delivery prices are quoted at checkout and
  calculated on weight (no free-delivery minimum is published on site).

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Founder Bruce's perfectionism is the house standard: "'That's still
  drinkable' won't cut it here!"
- The Rig began as a market trailer and lets customers sit on its roof
  overlooking Ely Cathedral; the Coffee Bus still tours shows and events.
- Bruce and Susanne leapt at the chance to trade seven days a week on Ely
  Market before opening The Yard — the market-trader DNA is part of the
  brand.
