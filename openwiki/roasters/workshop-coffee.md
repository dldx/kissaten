# Workshop Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Workshop Coffee is a London specialty coffee company that opened in **April 2011**. Its Shopify storefront offers single origins, house blends, decaf and half-caff, capsules, instant coffee and filter drip packs; the company also operates the Workshop Cafe & Academy in Belgravia. Workshop describes an integrated approach spanning origin visits, in-house roasting, cafés, wholesale and education.

## Address

- Hatfield, England, United Kingdom — the site identifies Hatfield as the roasting and packing location but does not publish the full street address.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Workshop says it works with producers, exporters and co-operatives through long-term relationships, visits farms and mills, and maintains an open dialogue about production costs so prices paid are fair and equitable. It says it pays premiums for quality and does not pursue exclusivity where that could limit a producer's ability to sell other volume.
- Its sourcing policy says it checks for no child or slave labour, no deforestation in its supply chain, responsible wastewater treatment, and support for organic growing, biodiversity and shade-grown coffee. It also describes supplier programmes involving agronomical support, pre-financing, financial support and community initiatives.
- No per-kg FOB, farm-gate or price-paid-to-producer figures were published on the pages checked.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Workshop roasts and packs coffee **Monday to Thursday**, excluding Bank Holidays. Orders are packed and dispatched **Monday to Friday**, excluding Bank Holidays; weekday orders placed before **9:00am** are usually dispatched the same day.
- UK Royal Mail 48 Tracked costs **£3.95** and is stated as **2-5 working days**, or free on one-time orders over **£25**. Royal Mail 24 Tracked costs **£4.50** and is stated as **1-2 working days**. UK subscriptions are sent free by Royal Mail 48 Tracked.
- For international orders, the site says Royal Mail delivery typically takes **5-10 working days for Europe**, while Rest of World deliveries may take longer. Orders over **2kg** are dispatched by FedEx International with costs calculated by weight and destination; no fixed international rates are published.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Workshop says its goal is “the best coffee possible”: clean, sweet, fresh coffees that express place, season and producer. Its stated quality cycle links better pay for producers with reinvestment and training at farm or co-operative level.
