# PLOT Roasting — Roaster Profile

## Overview

PLOT Roasting is a London roastery founded in 2019 by Matthew (co-founder of a
Brighton café micro-chain, later a roaster/trainer across London and the South
East) "to source and roast unique and interesting coffees, building an
equitable supply chain based on long-term relationships". Despite launching
just before the pandemic, they now supply some of the country's most
quality-focused coffee shops and run a coffee school with SCA courses. The Shopify
storefront leans experimental: the numbered **ICON Series** (a limited-release
subscription, 69 releases and counting, from Yemen Ja'adi naturals to
four-strain-yeast India lots) and collaborations like the **Fermentation
Project (James Hoffmann x Lucia Solis)**.

## Address

- London — United Kingdom (the site's Location page shows only a Google Maps
  pin in East London; full street address not published)

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Product pages and homepage features name producers farm-by-farm — Wilder
  Lazo and Las Etíopes (Peru), Thiriku Farmers Cooperative (Kenya), Mooleh
  Manay (India), Familia Hartmann (Panama), the Haraz community (Yemen) — with
  harvest/processing detail for each.
- Founder doubles as "Green Buyer" and holds Q-Instructor and SCA AST
  credentials (green coffee, sensory, roasting, brewing).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Dispatch Monday to Friday; **order by 11:00 for same-day dispatch**, and
  coffee is typically roasted and dispatched within 1–2 working days of
  ordering (roasted to order rather than to a fixed weekly schedule).
- UK mainland: Royal Mail Tracked 48 **free over £45**; Tracked 24 £3.95
  (1–2 days); DHL next working day £5.95.
- ICON Series subscription ships on the **first working day of each month**.
- International shipping is notably transparent: EU VAT collected at checkout
  under the IOSS scheme (orders under €150), Norway via VOEC, Switzerland
  pre-paid, US orders shipped Delivered Duty Paid — with per-destination
  delivery estimates published on the shipping policy page.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Three guiding principles: **Honesty, Consistency, Continuous improvement**.
- Motto on the site: "Roast, taste, repeat", with a "Worldwide Coffee Supply"
  limited-edition tee to match.
- Serious about education: the team includes the **UK Cup Tasters Champion
  2024** (Alexa, Head of Training) and the roastery teaches SCA courses.
- The ICON Series list spans 69 numbered releases — first time featuring an
  Indian coffee at release 69 (Mooleh Manay four-strain yeast lot).

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper filters the `all-coffee` collection by slug to drop non-bean
  products: `subscription`, `gift-card`, `wholesale`, `equipment`,
  `accessory`, `merchandise` and — notably — `icon-limited-release-series`,
  so the ICON subscription product itself never appears as a bean.
