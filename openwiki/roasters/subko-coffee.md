# Subko Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Subko Coffee is an Indian specialty coffee roaster and part of a wider
Subko operation that also includes fine cacao and a craft bakehouse. The site
says its in-house Arabica Q Graders oversee coffee from Karnataka, Tamil Nadu,
Andhra Pradesh, Meghalaya, Assam, Nagaland, Mizoram and Tripura, as well as
Nepal and Sri Lanka. It deliberately works across Arabica, Robusta, Liberica
and Excelsa.

## Address

- India — full roastery address not published on the reviewed site.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Subko publishes origin and producer context on coffee pages and describes a
  subcontinental sourcing approach. No price-paid-to-producer, FOB-per-kg or
  cost-breakdown figures were found on the reviewed pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Its “From the Subcontinent, For All” positioning extends beyond coffee into
  fine cacao and micro-batch craft baking; the bakehouse team is described as
  beginning work at 5 am.
