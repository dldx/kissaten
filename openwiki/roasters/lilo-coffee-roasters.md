# Lilo Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Lilo Coffee Roasters is an Osaka specialty coffee company founded in 2014 by
former high-school classmates Hotta and Keita, later joined by designer Yoshi
Nakatani. It operates the original LiLo Coffee Roasters shop, LiLo Coffee Kissa
and the LiLo Coffee Factory, and sells seasonal, innovative, competition and
producer-focused coffees through a Shopify storefront.

## Address

- LiLo Coffee Factory, 3-3-17 Tamagawa, Fukushima Ward, Osaka, Japan.

## Sustainability

- LiLo's SDGs page describes a Myanmar farm project in which the company works
  as a "co-producer" with the farm. It also describes Fair Trade Timor-Leste
  coffee obtained through a Japanese NGO, refillable coffee packaging,
  reusable shipping boxes for trade customers and outlet-bean sales to avoid
  waste.
- The company says its Loring roaster can burn chaff as fuel, with potential
  energy-cost savings of up to 80%.

## Roasting & Equipment

- LiLo Coffee Factory opened in 2020 with a Loring roaster and a fully
  automatic drip machine using NASA technology. The site does not publish the
  Loring model or capacity.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The company profile says orders are usually shipped within two business days
  from the day after payment confirmation. The Coffee Factory is open Monday
  and Thursday, 12:00–18:00, and closed Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday
  and Sunday.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- LiLo identifies enjoyment, diversity and connection between people as values
  unchanged since its founding, and frames its mission as making life more fun
  and interesting through coffee.
