# Caravan Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Caravan Coffee is the in-house roasting arm of **Caravan**, the all-day
dining group founded in early 2010 on Exmouth Market by three New Zealand
friends — Laura Harper-Hinton, Miles Kirby and Chris Ammerman. The group now
runs seven London restaurants (Exmouth Market, King's Cross, Bankside, City,
Canary Wharf, Fitzrovia, Covent Garden) plus Vardo in Chelsea and a
Manchester site, all serving Caravan's own freshly roasted coffee. The
roastery and online shop are branded Caravan Coffee Roasters; the Shopify
storefront lives at [caravanandco.com](https://caravanandco.com) with a
separate wholesale site at caravancoffeeroasters.co.uk. Signature range
includes the DAILY, Roaster's Rotation (subscription-only) and the Myanmar
special lot "The Lady".

## Address

- Lambworks, North Road, London, N7 9DP — United Kingdom (the roastery, also
  used for click-and-collect)

## Sustainability

- **B Corp certified in 2024** — "business as a force for good" is one of
  the four published sourcing principles.
- **Energy-efficient roasting**: all coffee is roasted on a machine that
  recirculates hot air to recapture energy, using up to 80% less gas than a
  direct gas flame.
- Recyclable bags; home-compostable pods.
- Soil Association certified since 2021 — the flagship Market Blend is fully
  organic.
- Won the **Sustainability Award at the 2023 Retailers' Retailer Awards**.
- Roastery sustainability tours run through the Caravan Coffee School.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Four published sourcing principles: traceability & long-term
  relationships, ecological sustainability, the Female Producer Programme,
  and B Corp certification.
- Works with sustainability-focused importers such as B Corp certified
  **DR Wakefield**.
- **Female Producer Programme**: aim to purchase 50% of coffee from women
  producers (up from 41% in 2022), paying an additional price premium plus
  donating an extra 10p per kilo to projects advancing women in
  coffee-growing communities.
- Where official organic certification is unaffordable for farmers, they
  work with producers practising regenerative agriculture regardless of
  certification status.

## Roasting & Equipment

- A **Loring S70** hot-air roaster — nicknamed **"Lucy"** per their site —
  which recirculates hot air to recapture energy (up to 80% less gas than a
  direct gas flame).
- Coffee is "roasted down the road" for the restaurants; the roastery runs
  public tours via the Caravan Coffee School.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders are processed and shipped **2–3 business days** after ordering;
  coffee is typically dispatched within days of roasting (they recommend
  resting 3–7 days from roast date).
- Shipping rates (per their site): Royal Mail Tracked 48 **£3.50**, Royal
  Mail Tracked 24 **£5.00**, DHL next-day tracked **£9.00**; international
  from **£12.95** (allow up to 30 days).
- Free delivery: free Royal Mail Tracked 48 on orders **£25–£59.99**; free
  DHL next-day on orders **£60+**; subscriptions qualify for free Royal Mail
  48 (untracked) up to £59.99.
- Click-and-collect from the roastery (order by 2pm Mon–Fri for same-day
  collection until 4pm).

## Philosophy & Quirks

- "Well-travelled food for all day dining + well-travelled coffee for all
  day drinking" — no borders on the menu, everyone welcome.
- The roaster has a name: **Lucy**.
- **Roaster's Rotation** is a subscription-only rotating coffee — it never
  appears as a one-off purchase.
- Physical gift cards are made from **waste coffee** and sold in the
  restaurants.
