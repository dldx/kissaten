# Fire and Flow Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Fire and Flow Coffee is a UK coffee roaster on a Shopify storefront at www.fireandflowcoffee.co.uk. The curated `beans` collection carries 10 coffee products in GBP — AURORA £10.50, IGNITE, COTSWOLD BLEND £9.95, Colombia Hulia Supremo, Decaf Natural Mountain Water, Colombia Jairo Arcila £15.95 and Uganda Rwenzori £17.50, among others.

## Address

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Lineup mixes signature blends (AURORA, IGNITE, COTSWOLD BLEND) with single origins and a Mountain Water decaf.

## Scraping Quirks

- Canonical host is www.fireandflowcoffee.co.uk (fireandflow.co.uk 301-redirects to it).
- The curated `beans` collection (10) is a clean subset of `our-coffee` (14, which includes a COFFEE PODS item).
- Canonical product URLs are `/products/<handle>` (collection segment stripped). Currency is pinned to GBP.
