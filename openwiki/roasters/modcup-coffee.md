# Modcup Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Modcup Coffee is a United States specialty coffee roaster. The registered shop is the Modcup Shopify storefront, with an all-coffee collection at `/collections/all-coffee`; the registry describes the programme as offering modern and traditional expressions and direct-trade relationships. The storefront returned a rate-limit response during this research pass, so no roastery address, equipment, published sustainability detail, or shipping threshold is asserted.
