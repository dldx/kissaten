# One Half Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

One Half Coffee is a specialty roastery in Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia.
Its site says it focuses on seasonality, harmony and accentuating origin
character, with filter coffees, espresso and seasonal lots in the shop. The
store is Shopify-backed, with a separate store page for the coffee catalogue.

## Address

- One Half Roastery, 19, Jalan Bukit 11/2, Seksyen 11, 46200 Petaling Jaya,
  Selangor, Malaysia.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- One Half says it selects coffees for terroir, variety and purposeful
  processing, and aims to preserve origin character rather than mask it with
  roast flavours. The consulted pages do not publish producer-paid prices or a
  cost breakdown.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders are shipped from Malaysia within 1–2 working days after verification,
  excluding weekends and public holidays. The site gives a typical arrival time
  of 2–5 working days and says it ships worldwide, but publishes no fixed
  free-delivery minimum or destination-specific rates on the consulted policy.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The roastery frames its work as celebrating coffee by unlocking its potential
  through respect for seasons, cultivation and craft from farm to cup. Its site
  also operates the deliberately separate One Half x ilaika and One Half
  @steppes locations; those are cafés/retail sites, not the roastery address.

## Scraping Quirks

- Store/collection pages are fetched with Playwright and auto-scrolled (up to
  100 attempts, with support for “Load more” buttons) because products are
  loaded progressively. The scraper excludes equipment, accessories, brewing
  water, drip bags, gifts, bundles and sets, but the underlying site also sells
  these alongside coffee.
