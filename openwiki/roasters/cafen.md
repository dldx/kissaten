# cafēn — Roaster Profile

## Overview

cafēn is an Edinburgh "modern roasting company" roasting intentionally light to
honour "the vibrancy, complexity, terroir, and individuality of each lot". It
began as a home-brewing fascination that became the **cafēn brew bar** at
44 Dundas Street in Edinburgh's New Town (open 8am–6pm daily), with the roastery
hand-roasting lots "just a few miles away" — the idea crystallised in April 2024
per a third-party Dayglow profile. The storefront is Shopify at
[cafen.co.uk](https://cafen.co.uk), styled throughout in lowercase, and the
company behind it is Cafen Speciality Roasters Ltd (registered office
60 Constitution Street, Edinburgh EH6 6RR, per Companies House). Retail coffees
are seasonal single estates and separated lots — Kenya, Colombia, Costa Rica,
Ecuador, Rwanda, Ethiopia — sold at 125g/200g and 1kg/3kg sizes.

## Address

- Edinburgh, Scotland, United Kingdom — roastery address not published on the
  site (described only as "locally … just a few miles away" from the brew bar).
  The flagship **brew bar** (a café, not the roastery) is at 44 Dundas Street,
  Edinburgh EH3 6JN, and Companies House lists Cafen Speciality Roasters Ltd at
  60 Constitution Street, Edinburgh EH6 6RR.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- **cafēn Kenya Sourcing Project**: a direct-sourcing initiative for
  "single-estate, single-tree and separated-varietal" Kenyan lots, developed
  with producer partner **Peter Mbature of Kamavindi**, who helps connect and
  guide processing (e.g. the Ndwiga Family's Nginda Estate lot in Embu).
- Every product page carries full provenance — country, region, producer,
  farm, varietal, altitude, harvest year, process and roast style — with
  lengthy producer and process write-ups (no FOB/farm-gate prices published).
- Wholesale lots are described as "each with full transparency – origin,
  processing, roast approach, and tasting notes".

## Schedules & Shipping

- **Roasted to order: roasted fresh every Tuesday and dispatched on Wednesday
  afternoon** (per their "roasting & shipping policy" page); cancellations must
  be requested at least 48 hours before the Tuesday roast.
- Coffees are meant to rest: they recommend **at least 14 days after roast
  date**, with dense, high-elevation lots peaking "between 3 to 6 weeks".
- No free-delivery threshold or per-region shipping rates are published.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- "Minimalist yet intentional": balance "between science and intuition,
  innovation and purity", with "long-lasting, meaningful and reciprocal
  relationships with producers".
- The site's about copy credits "a quiet home ritual" for starting it all, and
  the brand deliberately never fell into reach-of-luxury territory: coffees
  should be "thoughtful, refined, conscious, yet never out of reach".
- Occasionally drops celebration lots, e.g. the **Jubilee Garden 3rd
  Anniversary** advanced-natural SL28 from La Quebraditas, Huila.
