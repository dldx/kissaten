# Volcano Coffee Works — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Volcano Coffee Works (volcanocoffeeworks.com) is a South London speciality
coffee roaster on a Shopify storefront. Its roastery sits metres from its own
West Norwood café, and it sells whole beans/ground coffee, compostable pods,
cold-brew cans and subscriptions alongside home-brewing equipment and barista
courses. The catalogue holds 16 saved coffees plus one tasting kit (the
Speciality Coffee Starter Box).

## Address

- West Norwood, South London (SE21) — United Kingdom (full street address not
  published on the site; the roastery is located metres from its own café at
  West Norwood SE21 8EN).

## Schedules & Shipping

- A "Taste Guarantee" backs the first order, with 20% off your first coffee
  purchase on the storefront.

## Scraping Quirks

- The Speciality Coffee Starter Box is a tasting-kit product: it must flow
  through the review queue (`is_tasting_kit` / `requires_review`), never
  excluded.
