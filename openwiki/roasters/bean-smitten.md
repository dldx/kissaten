# Bean Smitten — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Bean Smitten is an independent specialty coffee roaster in a rural spot on the
**Kent/East Sussex border at Flimwell**, about 20 minutes from Royal Tunbridge
Wells. Former finance executive **Darren Tickner** quit "the rat race" in
**2014**, hired a small workshop near Flimwell and set up the roastery. The
storefront is Wix, selling four signature blends (Classic, Revive, Equinox and
a sugarcane-decaf Colombia) plus rotating single origins from Brazil, Burundi,
Colombia, DR Congo and Peru. Their Classic and Revive blends are **Great Taste
award winners** (3-star badge shown on site), with a Muddy Stilettos rosette
and "Produced in Kent" membership. They are also an official distributor of
Sanremo espresso machines and serve wholesale customers across Kent, Sussex
and Surrey.

## Address

- Bean Smitten Coffee Roasters, Cedar Gables, Hastings Road, Flimwell, East
  Sussex, TN5 7QA — United Kingdom

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Ethically sourced through reputable specialty merchants — notably **Falcon
  Specialty** — with the site stating this "ensures total transparency about
  provenance and peace of mind that farmers have received a fair price". No
  FOB/farm-gate figures are published.
- Member of **Produced in Kent** (local produce association).

## Roasting & Equipment

- Hand-roasted in **small batches several times a week**, roasting just enough
  to meet demand for freshness.
- Uses "the latest roasting technology" with careful data tracking, but
  explicitly "relies on trained human senses" — each batch is looked after by
  a person (no machine brand/model published).
- They can grind to order for customers without grinders, and give
  coffee-specific brewing advice.
- The roastery is open to the public for take-away coffees and bean sales,
  Monday–Friday 9am–4pm.

## Schedules & Shipping

- **Same weekday dispatch** on orders placed before 10am (subject to roasted
  stock availability); standard delivery is **Royal Mail 48 Tracked**, with
  premium options at extra cost.
- **Free tracked shipping on UK orders over £30**; UK-only shipping.
- Click & Collect available 24/7 from the collections box at Cedar Gables
  Campsite next to the roastery.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Mission statement: to talk about coffee "in a straightforward and
  unpretentious way" — "specialty coffee, in our opinion, should be accessible
  to everyone".
- Founder story: the major chains got Darren hooked on coffee, then "copious
  cups of underwhelming coffee" led him to question what makes a good cup.
- A Muddy Stilettos rosette sits alongside the Great Taste awards on the site.
