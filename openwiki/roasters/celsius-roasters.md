# Celsius Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Celsius Roasters is a specialty roaster founded in Lyon in 2022 by Nicolas and
Lucas. Its Shopify storefront divides coffees into blends, Pure Origins and
Explorations, alongside equipment, workshops and advice. The brand describes
its work as a balance between progressive specialty-coffee guidance and
exceptional coffees for experienced and occasional drinkers.

## Address

- Lyon, France — full roastery address not published on the consulted pages.
  The site identifies 12 rue des Capucins as its shop/café address, which is
  not used here as a roastery address.

## Sustainability

- Celsius says it limits carbon emissions and waste through eco-designed
  packaging, bicycle transport and sharing of technical resources. The site
  also labels its packaging 100% recyclable.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Celsius says it pays a fair price for quality coffee, wants producers to be
  fairly remunerated, and seeks long-term relationships through the supply
  chain. It presents transparency about product information and decisions as a
  core value, but publishes no concrete price-paid-to-producer figures on the
  consulted pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The shipping policy says coffee is sent by post once a week. The shop states
  free delivery to a relay point for purchases over €39 and separately gives
  free delivery from €39 in mainland France. No destination-specific rates or
  general dispatch weekday was published on the consulted pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- “Precision” is Celsius's original promise: precision down to the degree in
  roasting, green-coffee selection and extraction. The brand frames its other
  two values as responsibility and sincerity, explicitly rejecting a fixed
  dogma about how specialty coffee must taste.
