# Philocoffea — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Philocoffea is the specialty coffee brand founded by Tetsu Kasuya, the 2016 World
Brewers Cup Champion and first Asian winner of the competition. Based in Tokyo,
Japan, it sells coffee, gift sets, brewing equipment and its signature dip-style
coffee through a Shopify storefront, alongside retail cafes in Tokyo and Chiba.
Kasuya selects and buys green coffee, creates roasting profiles and controls quality
for the brand.

## Address

- Tokyo, Japan — full roastery address not published on the reviewed site pages.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Philocoffea says it looks for traceable specialty coffee defined by location,
  cultivation, hand-picking and sustainability. Kasuya sometimes buys directly
  from farms after visiting producers and experiencing their working conditions and
  local culture. No per-lot purchase price or farm-gate figures were found on the
  reviewed pages.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Philocoffea's methodology page says its roasting station is equipped with Loring
  machines. Kasuya creates and manages the profiles, repeatedly testing new green
  coffees and adjusting the roast to the lot. The site does not publish a Loring
  model or capacity.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Philocoffea says orders paid by credit card ship within seven days of payment and
  uses DHL and EMS. No free-shipping threshold or destination-specific rate was
  published on the reviewed FAQ.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The name combines "philosophy" and "coffea"; its motto is "Delivering coffee
  everywhere." Philocoffea offers light through dark roasts, while describing
  cleanliness and a pleasant, persistent aftertaste as its quality ideals.

## Scraping Quirks

- Registry key is `philocoffea`. The scraper is JSON-only on the curated coffee
  collection, excludes books, filters, equipment and merchandise, rewrites URLs to
  canonical `/products/<handle>` paths, and pins Shopify requests to JPY because
  automatic localization otherwise returns converted currencies.
