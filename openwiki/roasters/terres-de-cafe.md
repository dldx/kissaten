# Terres De Café — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Terres De Café is an independent French specialty coffee company founded by
Christophe Servell in **2009**. Its PrestaShop storefront offers scored single
origins, blends, organic coffees, capsules, subscriptions and brewing equipment,
and the company operates a network of shops. The site describes its coffee as
roasted in Île-de-France and presents traceability from producer plot.

## Address

- Île-de-France, France — the site identifies its roasting workshop by region but
  does not publish the workshop's full street address.

## Sustainability

- Terres De Café reports an AFNOR CSR label based on ISO 26000, **79%** of green
  coffee from agroforestry systems, **21%** forest coffees, **47%** certified
  organic green coffees and **100%** traceable coffees.
- Since early 2023, partner Chaff has collected coffee grounds from two shops;
  the company says more than **600 kg** were converted into heating granules and
  green biogas. Its Forest Fund is financed by **€1 per kilogram** of The Forest
  coffee and helped finance a washing station at Ethiopia's Tatmara farm.
- Terres De Café says its first sailing-coffee cargo with TOWT left Colombia in
  **August 2024**, and that Belco plans to transport 100% of its coffees by sail
  by 2030.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The company says it works with **18 partner farms**, producer cooperatives and
  a network in Latin America and Africa, with clear information on terroir,
  variety, producer and fermentation. No FOB or farm-gate price figures are
  published in the pages checked.

## Roasting & Equipment

- All coffees are roasted in Île-de-France on **Loring** roasters using a
  low-carbon process. Profiles are adapted to the coffee and extraction method:
  espresso, mild or uniroast. A machine model or capacity is not published.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders are prepared within **24-48 hours, Monday-Friday**; weekend orders are
  prepared Monday. The site says regular roasting can extend this lead time.
- In mainland France and Corsica, point-relay shipping is **€4.99** under €50
  and free at €50 or more. Colissimo home delivery without signature is **€6.99**
  under €50 and **€4.99** over €50; with signature it is **€7.99** under €50
  and **€5.99** over €50. The delivery page lists separate rate bands for EU
  zones, overseas territories, North America/Asia/Oceania and Africa/South and
  Central America.
