# Flat&White — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Flat&White is an Argentine specialty coffee brand founded in 2016 by Martina
Vigo and Gabriel Gazdik. Its official site describes five Buenos Aires cafés,
an online shop shipping throughout Argentina, and coffee roasted by Martina;
the storefront is WooCommerce-based and prices in ARS. The brand also offers
training, wholesale coffee and equipment.

## Address

- Buenos Aires, Argentina — full roastery street address not published on site.

## Roasting & Equipment

- The company's history says it bought its own Diedrich roaster in 2021;
  Martina develops the roast profiles and the stated aim is control over
  quality and freshness. The page does not publish the Diedrich model or
  capacity.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The storefront states that it ships throughout Argentina and shows free
  shipping from ARS 90,000. It also advertises express delivery in AMBA; no
  dispatch-day schedule was published in the fetched pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The stated vision is to raise quality standards across the coffee-consumption
  chain and teach more people in Argentina and Ibero-America about specialty
  coffee. The site describes its own bakery and pastry production centre as
  another way of controlling quality.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper starts from a single WordPress/WooCommerce page rather than a
  conventional coffee collection and sends a deliberately minimised soup to
  the AI extractor: title/meta, product name, price, short description,
  variations, image URLs and JSON-LD.
- Product content is translated to English during extraction. Cafés, equipment
  and other non-bean products on the same storefront therefore need the
  scraper's product filtering rather than assuming the whole shop is coffee.
