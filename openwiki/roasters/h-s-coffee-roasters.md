# H&S Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

H&S Coffee Roasters is a small-batch specialty coffee roaster based in Laramie, Wyoming, United States. Its Shopify shop offers single-origin coffees, espresso and blends, with current listings including Colombia, Ecuador, Kenya and Peru. The company also publishes an archive of past coffees and emphasizes economic equity for farmers and producers.

## Address

- Laramie, Wyoming, United States — full roastery address not published on the accessible site.

## Sustainability

- H&S says its bags are reusable and recyclable #4 LDPE and are made in a CO2-neutral facility. It uses a low-energy ESP unit to capture over 99% of roasting emissions, reuses bags and liners as packaging materials, and composts roasting byproducts.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- H&S states that it strives for economic equity for farmers and producers and chooses coffees with the intent of highlighting the work preceding the roast. No price-paid-to-producer or cost-breakdown data was found.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The storefront currently advertises free shipping once the cart reaches £60 in the displayed UK storefront. No roast or dispatch weekday was found.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The site combines an explicit producer-equity statement with an unusually concrete operational account of emissions capture, packaging reuse and composting.
