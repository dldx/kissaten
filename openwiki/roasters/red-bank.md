# Red Bank — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Red Bank Coffee Roasters (redbankcoffee.com) roasts in the English Lake
District (per their site) with a sourcing programme focused on traceable
coffees bought from a network of producers and importers. It runs on a
Shopify storefront selling a `our-coffees` collection (13 of 14 products
saved).

## Address

- Unit 12, Boundary Bank, Kendal LA9 5RR, United Kingdom (published on the
  site's contact page).

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The About page describes a sourcing programme "focussed on quality,
  provenance and relationships", selecting coffees from producers and
  importers they know and trust, and visiting producers year-on-year to stay
  better partners (per their site).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders received by 9am Monday are dispatched by Wednesday; orders received
  by 9am Thursday are dispatched by Friday.
- Orders are sent via Royal Mail Tracked 24, or DPD when over 2kg. No free-
  delivery minimum is published.

## Scraping Quirks

- Shopify `our-coffees` collection.
- 13 of 14 products saved; the `moonshine` product recurs as a persistent
  Gemini enrichment failure (guarded out-of-stock updates).
