# Tanat Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Tanat Coffee is a French specialty roaster in Paris; the site describes the
brand as a new chapter after Kawa. It sells coffees by use and score, from
permanent blends through limited 90+ microlots, and also operates cafés,
subscriptions, training and professional channels. Coffee pages publish
origin, variety, process and aromatic profile details.

## Address

- 189 Rue d’Aubervilliers, 75018 Paris, France — the published company/
  roasting address for VAYGA SAS; the site separately lists retail boutiques
  at other Paris addresses.

## Sustainability

- Tanat says it is reducing impact through sustainable agricultural practices,
  lighter packaging, rethought transport and restrained consumption. It also
  states that it will not make capsules, which it describes as incompatible
  with its environmental vision.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Tanat says it sources directly from farms, producers and cooperatives it
  knows, often over several years, and seeks traceability from origin through
  the lot. No price-paid-to-producer or per-kg cost breakdown is published on
  the reviewed pages.

## Roasting & Equipment

- The roasting page says coffees are roasted to order in the Paris workshop,
  with profiles calibrated, tasted, adjusted and refined; profiles evolve with
  harvests, terroirs and post-harvest processes. No roaster brand, model or
  capacity is published.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Tanat says parcels are shipped the day of the order and delivered within
  48 hours on the homepage; its delivery policy gives the more general
  preparation window of 1–3 business days, Monday to Friday, excluding
  holidays.
- The policy says it ships worldwide and groups destinations into France and
  zones A–Z, including a dedicated UK zone and zones for the EU, North
  America and Asia. At review time it said the page was being updated and that
  final shipping charges would be shown at checkout, so no public destination
  rates or free-delivery threshold are recorded here.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Tanat frames roasting as revealing, rather than imposing, the coffee's
  character. It has kept the Kawa name visible in parts of the site while
  presenting Tanat as the current brand.
