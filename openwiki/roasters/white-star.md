# White Star Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

White Star Coffee (whitestar.coffee) is a coffee roaster and machine
specialist based in the heart of Belfast, on a custom Shopify storefront. It
sources speciality coffee and works with farmers and cooperatives who are
progressive in their practices and farm in sustainable, transparent ways,
delivering "exclusive and bespoke coffee and machine solutions". The
catalogue holds 17 saved coffees.

## Address

- Belfast, Northern Ireland — United Kingdom (full street address not
  published on the site).

## Scraping Quirks

- Domain correction: the canonical storefront is whitestar.coffee; the
  whitestarcoffee.co.uk host 404s storefront routes.
- Custom Shopify storefront where the scraper keeps the required
  `?id=<variant>` query parameter on product URLs — the storefront 404s the
  query-less form, so the query string must not be stripped.
