# Kanso Coffee Lab — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Kanso Coffee Lab is a specialty coffee business based in Munich, Germany. Its Shopify storefront offers a changing coffee lineup, subscriptions, coffee omakase and brew guides. The site describes a calm, intentional coffee experience and says that new coffees are continually introduced as fresh harvests and origins become available.

## Address

- Munich, Germany — full roastery address not published on the accessible site.

## Sustainability

- Kanso says it works closely with producers who share its standards of respect, transparency and ecological responsibility, with an aim to uplift communities and landscapes. The site does not publish specific project amounts, certifications or packaging details on the pages checked.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Kanso frames its approach as “less clutter, more clarity, more connection” and describes coffee as a calm, intentional craft rather than simply a product. Its distinctive offering is a coffee omakase: a curated tasting journey presented without a fixed menu.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper targets the availability-filtered `coffee-lineup` collection and removes product-page recommendations before AI extraction. It excludes URL matches for `gift-card`, `tote-bag` and `subscription`; other products in the Shopify shop are therefore not automatically catalogue targets.
