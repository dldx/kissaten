# Kaffeelix — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Kaffeelix is an Austrian specialty roaster based in St. Pölten and led by Felix Teiretzbacher, the site’s 2022 World Coffee Roasting Champion. It says clean cup profiles have been its focus since its beginnings in 2012 and sells special editions, espresso, filter coffees, equipment, workshops and subscriptions through Shopify.

## Address

- Werkstättenstraße 3a, 3100 St. Pölten, Austria — published as the Kaffeelix roastery address.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Austria: delivery is stated as 2–4 working days, €5.50 shipping, free from €40. In St. Pölten, the CO₂-neutral bicycle courier costs €5.90 and is free from €50. International EU orders are shipped with Austrian Post or DHL Worldwide Express; the site links a country-by-country shipping spreadsheet, with fees shown at checkout. The roastery outlet is open Fridays 11:00–15:00.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Kaffeelix says its roasts are intended to highlight each coffee’s origin and natural aromas rather than impose a generic roast character. Coffee is normally shipped whole bean, but customers can request a fresh grind for their preparation method in the order note.
