# Recent Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Recent Coffee (recent.coffee, trading as Recent Beans) is a local coffee
roastery, shop and espresso bar on a Shopify storefront. The bean catalogue
is built by unioning several origin and style collections — origin-africa-
middle-east, asia-pacific-islands, latin-america-carribean (a double-r store
slug), blends and decaffeinated — totalling 14 products saved.

## Address

- 97-99 Westgate, Wakefield WF1 1EL, United Kingdom (published on the site's
  Roasteries page as the Wakefield Roastery).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Free 24-hour shipping with Royal Mail on all orders over £9.
- Orders placed before 2pm on working days are shipped the same day where
  available.

## Scraping Quirks

- The bean catalogue is a multi-collection union rather than a single
  collection; the "latin-america-carribean" collection slug carries a double
  -r typo ("Carribean") that must be matched as-is.
- 14 products saved across the union.
