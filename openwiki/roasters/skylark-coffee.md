# Skylark Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Skylark Coffee describes itself as "the only 100 percent non-profit, 100
percent transparent specialty coffee brand in the world" (per their site). It
is a registered charity (#1142607, under the OCB Initiatives / One Church
Brighton umbrella) and a roaster at the foot of the South Downs on the edge of
Brighton, giving away all profits at year end. It sells on a Shopify storefront
at skylark.coffee priced in GBP.

## Address

- Unit 1, Fairway Business Centre, Westergate Road, Brighton BN2 4JZ — United
  Kingdom.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Pays "top specialty prices at twice the Fair Trade rate or more, then donates
  another £1 per kilo" (per their site).
- Named importing partners: Falcon Coffees and Raw Material.
- Argues there is "no such thing as Direct Trade" (per their site).
- Publishes annual financial Transparency Reports (a 2024 report exists as a
  PDF; figures not extracted here).

## Schedules & Shipping

- "We aim to ship all orders within one business day of purchase via Royal Mail
  Tracked 48hr." (per their site).
- Local orders are delivered by bicycle.
- Free UK shipping over £24.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Registered charity #1142607; not a B Corp, social enterprise or CIC.
- Beneficiaries include Knepp Wildland Foundation (rewilding), The Stumping
  Project (Technoserve/Falcon Ethiopian tree regeneration), Pro Baristas
  (Sussex youth barista training), Bette Buna (Ethiopia living income) and
  In Her Hands (women's woodworking).
- Brand art by Jackie Morris, poetry by Robert Macfarlane; "The Lost Words"
  inspired the name (per their site).
- Switched to zero-plastic bags.
