# Nylon Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Nylon Coffee is a Singapore specialty coffee roaster with a Shopify storefront
for filter and espresso coffees, subscriptions, equipment and merchandise. The
company describes its work as sustainable, traceable and transparent across the
coffee chain, and sells coffee from a shop in Everton Park. The reviewed site
does not publish a roaster model or capacity.

## Address

- Singapore — the site publishes 4 Everton Park #01-40, Singapore 080004 as a
  visitor/shop address, but does not explicitly identify it as the roastery;
  full roastery address not published on site.

## Sustainability

- Nylon says 1% of annual revenue supports environmental-cause projects.
- Its sustainability statement focuses on traceable relationships, fair pay and
  helping producers move toward economically sustainable livelihoods.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Nylon describes relationships across pickers, producers and logistics
  partners, and says it is committed to paying producers fairly. No per-lot
  FOB, farm-gate or price-paid figures were found on the reviewed pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Regular coffee is roasted in small batches every Wednesday and Friday and
  shipped directly from the roastery. Monthly subscriptions close on the 28th,
  roast on the first Monday and ship the following Wednesday. Free local
  shipping is advertised for orders over SGD 50; economy and DHL worldwide
  options are available, but destination-specific rates are not published on
  the reviewed page.
