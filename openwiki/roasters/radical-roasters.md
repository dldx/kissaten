# Radical Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Radical Roasters (radicalroasters.co.uk) describes itself as a micro
roastery based in Bristol (per their site). It runs on a Shopify storefront
selling a `coffee` collection of single origins, blends, experimental
co-ferments and decafs, with 10 products retained from 12 discovered.

## Address

- Full street address not published on site — Bristol, England, United
  Kingdom (self-described "micro roastery based in Bristol", per their
  site).

## Roasting & Equipment

- Coffees are roasted on a Diedrich 5kg, and all bags are hand-screenprinted
  by a local designer (per their site).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Free UK shipping over £45.
- Allow 24 hours to process; collection available Monday–Friday.

## Scraping Quirks

- Shopify `coffee` collection; merch products are excluded from the bean
  catalogue.
- Two coffees (the Powerhouse and Wild Thing products) recur as persistent
  Gemini enrichment failures; the scraper skips out-of-stock updates when a
  listing fetch fails.
