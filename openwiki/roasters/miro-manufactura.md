# miró manufactura de café — Roaster Profile

## Overview

miró manufactura de café is a boutique roastery and café founded in Zurich in 2013 by brothers Dani and David Sanchez. The roastery began with a 5 kg Probat in a disused garage and is now at Brauerstrasse 58, alongside the flagship café and training facilities. The site says it roasts seasonal coffee fresh every week and supplies several Zurich locations.

## Address

- Brauerstrasse 58, 8004 Zürich, Switzerland.

## Sustainability

- miró says most of its coffees are fair trade and organic, with some certified or direct trade, and describes a focus on improving producers’ living conditions. Subscription packaging is recyclable and subscription shipping is included.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The roaster says it imports green coffee and maintains close contact with sustainable coffee producers. It does not publish FOB, farm-gate, or price-paid-to-producer figures on the reviewed pages.

## Roasting & Equipment

- The origin story identifies the original machine as a 5 kg Probat. Current equipment capacity is not stated on the reviewed pages. The flagship site also houses the roastery and training labs.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Coffee is freshly roasted every week; the shipping page says an out-of-stock product may be roasted in the next cycle. Orders are sent within two days, with Swiss delivery taking approximately two days. Swiss B-Mail rates are CHF 8.30 up to 2 kg, CHF 10.30 up to 5 kg, CHF 10.80 up to 10 kg, CHF 16.80 up to 20 kg, and CHF 22.80 up to 30 kg; orders from CHF 100 ship free. International orders were temporarily directed to order@mirocoffee.co rather than given a published rate.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The company describes its aim as making outstanding coffee accessible while changing how coffee is produced “from seed to cup”. It also partners with SWISS airlines, which it says has served miró as freshly brewed specialty coffee since April 2021.
