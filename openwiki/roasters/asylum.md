# Asylum Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Asylum Coffee is a Swindon, Wiltshire specialty roastery — self-described as
"Wiltshire's Specialty Roastery" — based at Unit 64a BSS House, Cheney Manor,
Swindon SN2 2PJ. Its tagline is "**Escape the Ordinary / Drink Better Coffee**".
The live storefront is `www.asylumcoffee.co.uk` (the checklist's
`asylumcoffeehouse.co.uk` is dead). Note there is also an **unrelated**
Singapore company, **Asylum Coffeehouse**, trading at
`www.asylumcoffeehouse.com` — it is a separate business and is covered by its
own scraper and [profile page](asylum-coffeehouse.md).

## Address

- Unit 64a BSS House, Cheney Manor, Swindon SN2 2PJ, Wiltshire — United Kingdom

## Sustainability

Their homepage frames roasting as a means to an end: "our mission goes beyond
roasting... ethical sourcing, transparency, and giving back — educational
initiatives, infrastructure development, fair wages." The sustainability pledge is
expressed as a domain commitment rather than a list of credits.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Small-batch specialty roasting in a Swindon roastery.
- Range includes the house **Great Western Blend (GWB)** and **Eruption**, the
  **MAD series**, single origins (Ethiopia Guji Adola, Hamasho, Gedeb Lalesa,
  Mirazu Geisha, Sesongot Gayo, Bajo Canet, El Paraiso P-02), the Thai
  **Asadong**, and a **Sugarcane Decaf**.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The "Escape the Ordinary" tagline carries into their bean naming — the MAD
  series in particular feels like a personality quirk.
- Sustainability is framed as a mission statement rather than a certification
  checklist (see above).
