# Ripsnorter Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Ripsnorter Coffee is a Dutch specialty coffee roaster focused on high-quality single origins. The registered shop is the Dutch storefront at ripsnorter.nl. The consulted site was rate-limited during research, so no further unverified claims about its history, equipment or sourcing are included here.

## Address

- Netherlands — full roastery address, including town, was not published on the consulted pages.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses the front-page collection with `country=NL` rather than a generic catalogue URL.
- It excludes drip bags, subscriptions, gift cards and experiences by URL and product name. It also deliberately excludes the `delirious-blend-washed` slug because the product has no origin information.
