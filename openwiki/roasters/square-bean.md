# Square Bean — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Square Bean Coffee (squarebean.co.uk) is "an independent specialty coffee
micro-roastery based in Pershore, Worcestershire" (per their About page),
roasting small batches of single-origin and specialty-grade beans to order
and shipping direct. The catalogue spans origins such as Peru, Uganda,
Ethiopia and Colombia — whole bean and grind options are variants of the same
products — on a Shopify storefront in GBP.

## Address

- Pershore, Worcestershire, United Kingdom ("based in Pershore,
  Worcestershire", per their About page; "Founded by Kevin — whose obsession
  with coffee spans more than 30 years — and shaped by Lily's Peruvian
  roots"); full street address not published on site.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Free shipping on all orders over £25 (per the site's announcement bar);
  roasts small batches and ships direct to the door within 3 days (per their
  About page).

## Scraping Quirks

- Shopify — scraped JSON-only from the curated `all-coffees` collection,
  which holds only coffee beans; no `exclude_slugs` filtering needed.
- No tasting-kit products in the feed, so nothing flows through the review
  queue.
