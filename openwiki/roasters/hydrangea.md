# Hydrangea Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Hydrangea Coffee Roasters is a United States specialty coffee storefront focused on “light, fruit-forward, experimental coffees.” Its Shopify site groups coffees by process, terroir and a curated discovery selection, with single-farm offerings such as Finca El Paraiso and Hacienda La Esmeralda. The site also sells subscriptions and brewing gear.

## Address

- United States — city and full roastery address not published on the consulted site page.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The storefront advertises free shipping over $50 within the United States. No roast cadence, dispatch days or destination-specific rates were published on the consulted page.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The homepage describes its process as enhancing flavour development through modern fermentation techniques. Its catalogue separates “Rested Coffee” from the main selections, and also offers a Roaster’s Choice subscription.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses Playwright for extraction because the Shopify storefront is JavaScript-heavy. It excludes URL patterns for kettles, concentrate, subscriptions, rested coffee, garage-sale items, roaster’s choice, brewing, equipment and accessories; tasting-kit products are not part of this custom exclusion list.
