# Blackfinger Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Blackfinger Coffee Roasters is a queer-owned, worker-run cooperative café and
roastery in Camberwell, London, that opened on 13 June 2026. It roasts
small-lot coffees (Colombia, India, Indonesia) and runs a monthly "Coffee club"
subscription, on a Shopify storefront at blackfingercoffee.co.uk.

## Address

- 345 Camberwell Station Road, London SE5 9JN — United Kingdom.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Worker-run cooperative café and roastery.
- Offered free coffee to Trans Pride marchers, volunteers and donors over the
  weekend of 25–28 July 2026, with proof of participation required.

## Scraping Quirks

- Listed domain blackfinger.co.uk is an unrelated long-lapsed domain
  (2005–2009 captures) — the roaster is at blackfingercoffee.co.uk (Shopify,
  new, no Wayback captures).
- Catalogue is tiny (3 beans); the "Coffee club" subscription is **not** in the
  `/collections/coffee` collection (it only appears in `/collections/all`), so
  it is kept excluded.
- Only The Black Finger is in stock; King Khan and Thousand Hands are out of
  stock.
