# Poma Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

POMA Coffee Research is a Copenhagen-based coffee research and cultivation company. Per its storefront, it runs controlled trials in Denmark and with partner farms around the world, and the coffees it roasts are a direct result of that work. The Shopify shop combines coffee releases with research publications, advisory work, and a public research overview.

## Address

- Copenhagen, Denmark — full roastery address not published on site.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The site publishes research-scale context rather than a conventional price-transparency breakdown: its fetched research overview reports 34 active trials, 12 partner farms, 11 countries, and 14 publications. Individual coffee pages identify farms, varieties and processes for particular releases.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Releases are presented as scheduled drops; the storefront showed a release at “July 31, 12.00 (UTC+1)” for the Humidity Experiment coffee. Shipping is calculated at checkout; no free-delivery minimum or destination-specific rate was published in the fetched pages.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses the `/collections/all/products.json` catalogue and also fetches product pages. It excludes drip products, bundles, minerals, drippers, filters and other non-coffee goods, including the single-dose vial slug.
