# Dark Arts Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Dark Arts Coffee is an East London roastery founded in 2014 (per press
coverage of founder Brad Morrison) and steeped in skate, heavy metal and
occult visual culture — the webshop is as much a merch store (snapbacks,
jerseys, cassette-styled coffee boxes) as a coffee shop. Coffee names are
loud and creative (GRACE, BLUE MUSHROOM, MOTHER TONGUE, NEED 2), each listing
producer names on the front of the box. Since 2020 the roastery has doubled as
the Dark Arts HQ, home to all roasting, quality control, production and a
takeout espresso bar; a second site since 2022 is a "moody coffee stand"
inside The Great Frog in Shoreditch, surrounded by jewellery and a custom
Playboy x TGF Harley chopper. The Shopify storefront serves UK and EU
customers.

## Address

- Dark Arts HQ (roastery), 1–5 Rosina Street, off Homerton High Street,
  London E9 6JH — United Kingdom (moved to Rosina Street in 2020)

## Sustainability

- Wholesale customers get a **reusable coffee tub program** — returnable
  tubs replacing single-use packaging — and London deliveries are made by an
  **electric delivery van**, with fully recyclable retail packaging.

## Schedules & Shipping

- **Roasted to order, Monday to Friday.**
- Orders placed before 7AM are dispatched the same working day; after 7AM,
  the next working day. Click & collect orders placed before 7AM can be
  picked up same day.
- Free UK delivery thresholds — per their site: free UK shipping over £20 and
  free next-day delivery over £45 (site-wide banner); the shipping policy
  page states free DPD Next Working Day shipping on UK orders over £50.
- Other rates are calculated live at checkout (UK: DPD and Royal Mail;
  international: Royal Mail tracked/untracked or DHL).

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Brand identity draws on biking culture, heavy metal, the occult and 1970s
  hippy culture — espresso-bar-meets-record-shop.
- The **LDN Series** is a London-collaboration line; the
  **"All Coffees Are Beautiful"** coffee is a joint release with post-punk
  band IDLES and the Skateistan charity.
- Their Shoreditch outlet lives inside The Great Frog, a rock'n'roll jewellery
  shop — arguably the only coffee stand sharing a room with a Playboy x TGF
  chopper.
