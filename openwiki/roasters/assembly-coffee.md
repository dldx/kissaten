# Assembly Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Assembly Coffee is an independent specialty roaster founded in April 2015 and
based in Brixton, London. Its origin story is unusual: in 2014 a small team
from established south London roastery Volcano Coffee Works gathered the UK's
leading independent coffee shops and restaurants to design what a roastery
built around wholesale partners should be. The result launched as headline
sponsor of the inaugural Coffee Masters at the London Coffee Festival. A team
of ~30, B-Corp certified, with a Shopify storefront at
[assemblycoffee.co.uk](https://assemblycoffee.co.uk) and an on-site café,
Door – Coffee Bar. Their coffee is served at venues such as Omotesando Koffee,
the NoMad Hotel and Four Boroughs.

## Address

- 244 Ferndale Road, Brixton, London, SW9 8FR, United Kingdom (the Brixton
  roastery HQ; Door – Coffee Bar is on site)

## Sustainability

- **B-Corp certified** business.
- Publishes a **Supplier Code of Conduct** — described as a "living document"
  committing suppliers to high social and environmental standards across the
  specialty coffee supply chain.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Small-batch roasting on a **15kg Loring Smart Roast**.
- Q-grade certified staff run quality control on every batch in the
  roastery's **Roaster's Lab**.
- The roastery doubles as a training hub and event space for wholesale
  partners, and hosts **SCA training programmes** (brewing, barista, sensory)
  led by their Head of Education.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders placed before midnight Monday–Thursday are packed and dispatched the
  next day; Friday orders dispatch on Monday (excluding Bank Holidays).
- Royal Mail and DPD with tracking; **complimentary UK shipping over £50**.
  Rates per their delivery page: Royal Mail tracked 2–4 days £3.45 (orders
  £0–£29.99) or £2.45 (£30–£49.99); DPD 2-working-day tracked £6 (same-day
  dispatch for orders before 12pm, Monday–Friday, not on equipment orders).
- Europe typically within a week or two after dispatch; over 2kg to Europe is
  charged a higher rate; rest of world a few weeks. Import duties are the
  customer's liability.
- Best-before guidance: use up to 5 weeks after roasting.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Two core beliefs printed on their about page: **"The whole is greater than
  the sum of its parts"** and **"Everything can always be better."**
- **The Assembly Suite** — a loyalty programme where points earned as a
  customer unlock complimentary gifts and experiences.
- Illustrated packaging with a house design language, documented on an
  "illustrations explained" page.
- Team competition pedigree: Jake Mummery won the Irish Brewers Cup
  Championship 2026 (per their journal).
