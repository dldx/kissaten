# JBC Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

JBC Coffee Roasters is a Wisconsin, United States specialty roaster with a WooCommerce storefront for coffee, subscriptions and merchandise. Its stated philosophy combines quality, a human story and sustainability; the team says it is 50% women-owned. JBC offers single origins and blends, including a designated Twisted Espresso blend, and says all of its coffees are light to light-medium roasted.

## Address

- Wisconsin, United States — the consulted contact and FAQ pages identify the site as a roasting location but do not publish its city or street address.

## Sustainability

- JBC says it purchases sustainable coffees directly from producers and through brokers that share its goal of making coffee a sustainable commodity. It says financial and environmental balance are part of that purchasing decision.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- JBC says it pays “4, 5, 6, 7 times (and much more!)” the rates mandated by Fairtrade for its specialty coffees, describing this as a sustainability-through-quality model rather than Fairtrade. It also says it has two Q Graders, evaluates green and roasted samples extensively, and tests water activity and moisture at purchase, arrival, release and quarterly storage checks. No per-lot price table was published.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders before 10 am CST Monday–Friday ship the same day; later Monday–Thursday orders ship the next day, while orders after 10 am Friday through Sunday ship the following Monday. US orders of three or more coffee bags qualify for free USPS shipping with the `freeship` code. International orders use USPS Priority International and typically arrive in 7–12 days.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- JBC does not print roast dates on its packaging, but says coffee is shipped within five days of roasting; customers may request the roast date. It also says coffee is not labelled by roast level because the company wants customers to discuss origin and flavour rather than a dark/light category.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper targets the WooCommerce `/product-category/coffee/` archive and excludes product URLs containing `third-wave-water`, `gift-card` or `gift`. Future archive changes should preserve that narrow exclusion rather than treating every non-coffee product as a coffee listing.
