# Klover Club — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Klover Club, presented on its site as Klover Coffee Paris, is a specialty coffee roaster in Paris, France. The Shopify storefront is organised around All Products, Klover Selection and Limited Edition collections. Its stated positioning is lightly roasted coffee that celebrates origin and craftsmanship.

## Address

- Paris, France — the accessible site publishes a Paris showroom address, but not a separate roastery address; the full roastery address is not published.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The wholesale page says coffee is **freshly roasted every Wednesday**. If the latest roast is available, an order may ship immediately; otherwise it is included in the following week’s roast schedule.
- The storefront banner states **free shipping over €100 within France and across Europe**. No destination-specific paid rates were verified on the accessible pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Klover’s short description is “Elevate Your Daily Happiness”; it links light roasting to preserving the character of origin and craftsmanship. The site’s Paris showroom draws explicitly on Korean, Japanese and Danish coffee cultures and Scandinavian design.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper reads `/collections/all`, removes products whose card’s parent text contains `Sold out`, and then filters URL matches for merchandise, equipment, subscriptions, gifts and gift cards. This is a URL-and-card-text filter rather than a general Shopify catalogue import.
