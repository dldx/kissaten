# Substance Café — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Substance Café is a small specialty coffee shop and roastery in Paris. Joachim
is the site's sole barista and roaster; the bar offers seated coffee experiences
by reservation, while the shop sells its coffees and selected brewing equipment.
The company says it began roasting very lightly eight years before the current
site copy, to preserve terroir.

## Address

- 30 rue Dussoubs, 75002 Paris, France.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Substance says it uses a **Loring S7** in the workshop inside the shop and
  describes its approach as **ultra-light roasting**. Production volumes are
  small, with priority given to quality and consistency.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The bar deliberately offers seating around the bar only: no takeaway cups,
  sugar, pastries or music, in order to focus on the coffee and its terroir.
