# Nomad Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Nomad Coffee is a Barcelona specialty-coffee company that began as Jordi
Mestre’s London coffee cart in 2011 and established itself in Barcelona in
2014. Its Shopify storefront sells espresso, filter, competition and limited
edition coffees, plus subscriptions, equipment and academy courses. Nomad says
it became a Certified B Corporation in 2023 and operates cafés as well as its
roastery.

## Address

- Carrer Marroc, Poblenou, Barcelona, Spain — the history page identifies this
  as the Cal Nomad roastery site but does not publish a street number.

## Sustainability

- Nomad publishes a climate action plan and says it makes an annual contribution
  to World Coffee Research. It also reports B Corp certification and frames the
  plan around measuring and reducing environmental impact.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Since 2020 Nomad has published annual transparency reports; it says the 2021
  report achieved transparency for 100% of its menu. The reports include FOB
  prices, producer relationships, historical price comparisons, expenses and
  team information. Nomad also describes travel to origin and work with
  importers and producers.

## Roasting & Equipment

- The history page records a restored and upgraded Probat UG22 acquired in 2017
  and says the 2020 Cal Nomad move grew the operation from one roaster to two
  by 2023. No capacity is published on the reviewed pages. Nomad also describes
  its own quality-control protocols.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders are prepared and shipped Tuesday through Thursday; Nomad does not
  roast on weekends or holidays. Delivery is estimated at 24–48 hours in
  mainland Spain and the Balearics, while the rest of the world is estimated at
  48–96 hours. Free shipping is offered over EUR 30 in mainland Spain,
  Portugal and the Balearics (excluding the Canaries, Ceuta and Melilla), and
  over EUR 80 for coffee orders to the listed European countries.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Nomad describes its enduring values as closeness and respect for producers,
  careful process, and keeping the roast in-house to express each coffee’s
  qualities. Its coffee education programme, The Coffee Academy, extends that
  aim to professionals and home brewers.
