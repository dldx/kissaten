# Aura Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Aura Coffee Roasters is a specialty roastery serving Santiago and its surrounding area. Its own history describes a planned café project that became a roastery instead, and its shop presents coffees with names such as Gato Griego, Gato Espartano and Gato Yeti. The storefront is a Jumpseller shop, with coffees and accessories sold separately.

## Address

- Santiago, Región Metropolitana, Chile — full roastery address not published on the consulted site pages.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Aura says it has historically roasted only light coffee. Its own scale uses three brackets: medio-ligero below 95 Agtron, ligero at 95–110, and ultra-ligero above 110 Agtron; the published guidance includes recommended rest periods for each bracket.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The shop publishes CLP 1,990 shipping for Gran Santiago orders over CLP 20,000, free delivery over CLP 40,000 in Gran Santiago, and a CLP 3,000 shipping discount for orders over CLP 30,000 to other regions. Its pickup information says orders generally take 1–5 business days and that Friday orders may be handled the following week.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Aura says its aims are specialty-coffee quality, sustainability, social responsibility and customer education. Its product naming uses recurring cat identities, and the roast article explicitly frames light roasting as a way to preserve the bean's character and origin.
