# Calendar Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Calendar Coffee is a seasonal coffee roastery based in Galway, Ireland. Zarah
Lawless and Daniel Boobier started the project after meeting in London, moving
to Galway in 2017; the About page dates the roastery's dedication to its current
environmental and farmer-income goals from 2018. Its Shopify shop specialises
in light-roasted seasonal coffees, subscriptions and wholesale.

## Address

- Galway, Ireland — full roastery address not published on the consulted site
  pages.

## Sustainability

- Calendar says it dedicates its resources to reducing environmental impact and
  to ensuring producers are profitable. It publishes annual impact reports
  covering coffee prices, environmental footprint and finances, and keeps
  separate responsibility and initiatives pages for ongoing work.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Calendar's stated goal is for the farmers it buys from to earn a sustainable
  income and ultimately for all coffee farmers to be profitable. Its Impact
  page links reports for 2021–2025 and describes them as detailed analyses of
  prices paid for coffee, environmental impact and finances. The consulted page
  does not itself reproduce the report's individual price figures.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Calendar says it roasts to order every week. The shipping policy says domestic
  deliveries usually arrive 2–3 days after dispatch; international orders under
  1 kg use untracked An Post and may take up to 21 days, while orders over 1 kg
  use tracked couriers. The shop banner gives free shipping over €30 in the
  Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland. No other country-specific free
  threshold or rate was published on the consulted pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Calendar treats coffee as a seasonal product and says its purpose is to
  champion fresh harvests while reducing environmental and social impact. Its
  product range includes the year-round “Teamwork” seasonal espresso and the
  “Big Nothing” decaffeinated espresso.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses only the Shopify `fresh-harvests` JSON feed, excludes the
  `test-roast` and `wilfa` handles, and rewrites products to the same collection
  path. It therefore deliberately omits the site's test roast and grinder even
  though both appear in the storefront search.
