# Bluebird Coffee Roastery — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Bluebird Coffee Roastery is a South African specialty roaster based in the KZN Midlands. Its purpose-built 2022 building in Howick contains the roastery, coffee bar, training centre, offices and QC lab. The WooCommerce shop offers single origins, special releases, espresso blends, subscriptions, pods, equipment and merchandise, and says it releases two carefully selected specialty coffees each month.

## Address

- 36 Main Street, Howick, South Africa.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Bluebird publishes a transparency explainer distinguishing commodity C-price from FOB and farm-gate pricing. It says it does not simply pay FOB in every case: some direct-trade coffees are FOB plus shipping and clearing, while other coffees involve additional exporter/importer links. It says it aims to trade 100% direct and transparent, and publishes farm-gate prices where possible; the page does not give one universal price paid for all coffees.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Bluebird says new coffees are released twice a month. The shop offers free South African shipping over ZAR 699. Its coffee-bar and roastery are open seven days a week, but the site does not state that roasting or dispatch occurs every day.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Founder Dario Scilipoti says the company prioritises people, environment and quality before profit, and describes the business as a passion project built around generous, long-term relationships with producers. Coffee is grouped into relatable quality bands—Very Good (83–85), Excellent (86–88) and Outstanding (88+)—rather than relying only on an exact score.

## Scraping Quirks

- Bluebird's product pages use a large Elementor/WooCommerce template. The scraper keeps product summary, specification, origin-description and metadata sections while stripping repeated brew guides, FAQs and related-product carousels before AI extraction. It uses Playwright because the site is JavaScript-heavy and scrapes three separate tag/release archives: single origin, special releases and espresso blend.
