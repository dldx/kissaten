# Lot Zero — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Lot Zero is the specialty coffee label of Sevengrams, a Milan, Italy coffee company. The official site says Sevengrams has produced coffee in Milan since 2009 and that Lot Zero coffees are selected and artisanally roasted by Chiara Bergonzi. The WordPress/WooCommerce shop presents Lot Zero as a dedicated specialty-coffee category alongside the parent company’s blends, single origins and coffee equipment.

## Address

- Milan, Italy — full Lot Zero roastery address not published on the site; Sevengrams publishes a Milan office/coffee-studio address, which is not treated as the roastery address here.

## Sustainability

- Sevengrams says it selects suppliers with Fairtrade, Rainforest Alliance, Organic, CSC, FSC and FSSC 22000 certifications, promotes lower-impact agriculture, and aims for traceability across the supply chain.
- Its sustainability page describes **Blockchain 4 Sustainability** digital traceability with a QR code on every package, research into fully recyclable packaging and compostable paper pods. These are parent-company claims rather than Lot Zero-specific project figures.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The parent company says its goal is a more transparent and sustainable Italian coffee supply chain and that its coffees come from farms using sustainable agricultural practices. The pages checked do not publish Lot Zero per-lot FOB, farm-gate or price-paid-to-producer figures.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper reads two paginated English WooCommerce category URLs, skips loop items carrying the `outofstock` class, and trims product pages to the first two Elementor elements for AI extraction. It excludes brew bags, drip bags, capsules and pods, so those products are not treated as whole/ground-bean catalogue entries.
