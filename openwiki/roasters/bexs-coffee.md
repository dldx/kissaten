# Bex's Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Bex's Coffee is a café and bean shop in Huddersfield (West Yorkshire), at 72D
Westbourne Road, Marsh, that has hand-roasted its coffee in-house since the café
opened in 2015. The bean shop migrated from its previous "HUD Coffee Co." Wix
storefront to a Shopify storefront as "Bex's Coffee" around December 2023, and
carries roughly 18 coffee profiles priced in GBP — a home blend at
£7.50/250g and single origins around £7.65–£7.95.
