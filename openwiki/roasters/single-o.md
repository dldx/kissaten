# Single O — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Single O is an Australian specialty coffee roaster and cafe business founded in
Sydney in 2003. Its Shopify shop separates single origins, blends, decaf,
Parachutes drip bags, subscriptions and brewing goods; the signature blends
include Killerbee, Collider and Reservoir. The company also operates cafes in
Australia and Japan, including its original Surry Hills cafe.

## Address

- Sydney, New South Wales, Australia — full roastery address not published on site.

## Sustainability

- Its **No Death To Coffee** programme includes B Corp certification, support for
  World Coffee Research, circular-economy projects, and a stated goal of halving
  carbon emissions by 2026 and reaching Net Zero by 2030.
- The site says its roastworks use 100% GreenPower, including its own solar
  generation. It also describes Giving A Puck, a coffee-grounds upcycling
  programme run with Reground.

## Roasting & Equipment

- The founders bought their first **15 kg roaster**, named “Big Boris”, in 2003.
  The current machine and its capacity are not identified on the pages checked.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Australian orders over **$65.00 AUD** receive free standard shipping; otherwise
  standard shipping is **$10.00 AUD**, or express shipping is **$15.00 AUD**.
- Orders placed before 12pm Monday-Friday are dispatched within **2 business
  days**. Weekend orders are also dispatched within **2 business days**; during
  peak promotions, the festive season and NSW public holidays, the site asks for
  **3-4 business days**.
- Australia Post standard delivery is stated as **2-6 business days after
  dispatch** and express as **1-4 business days after dispatch**. International
  orders placed before 12pm Monday-Thursday are dispatched within 2 business
  days; orders after 12pm Thursday or over the weekend are dispatched Tuesday.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Single O says it showcases around **three new origins per month** and selects
  for deliciousness, farm quality and sustainability practices. Its Art Bag
  Project has commissioned more than 25 artist collaborations.

## Scraping Quirks

- Registry key: `single-o`. The Shopify scraper uses the curated `coffee`
  collection, canonicalises collection product URLs to `/products/<handle>`,
  pins currency to AUD, and excludes Parachutes drip-bag products and the Filter
  Show subscription.
