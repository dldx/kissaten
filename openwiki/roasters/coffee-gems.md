# Coffee Gems — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Coffee Gems (branded "Coffee Gem Artisan Roasters") is a UK specialty coffee
roaster in Chippenham, Wiltshire, on a Shopify storefront at coffeegems.co.uk.
Its positioning is "WE ONLY ROAST THE TOP 1% WORLDWIDE SCORING SCA 86+", with
curated single origins, blends and decafs plus 90+ competition coffees from
Café Granja La Esperanza (Finca Zarza, Cerro Azul, Las Margaritas). The merged
coffee collections carry 19 products (17 beans + 2 "Taster Trio" kits).

## Address

- Roastery: Unit C11, Bumpers Way, Bumpers Farm Industrial Estate, Chippenham SN14 6LH — United Kingdom
- Coffee house: 20 Borough Parade, Chippenham SN15 3WL; also at 1 Market Place, Chippenham SN15 3HD.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Claims to source and roast only beans scoring SCA 86+, with 90+ competition
  lots from Café Granja La Esperanza.
- Pays farmers premium prices "significantly exceeding Fair Trade minimums"
  and visits farms personally to build producer relationships (per their
  site).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Dispatch within 1 working day; Royal Mail 48hr £2.95 (UK mainland), free UK
  delivery over £25 (or 2kg), DPD express upgrade, local collection at the
  roastery (per their delivery page).

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Founder story: a grandmother's copper-pot roasting ritual in childhood,
  leading to 18+ years in specialty coffee and a "Brewing Curious Minds"
  tagline.
- Decaf processed via the sugarcane method.

## Scraping Quirks

- Shopify 2-collection merge (`coffee`, 16, + `coffee-blends`, which adds the
  NONNA EMMA blend and two "Taster Trio" kits) → 19, deduped on canonical URLs.
- Equipment/umbrella collections (vouchers, experiences, machines, containers,
  mugs) and the Cascara Crunch fruit snack are deliberately not crawled.
- The Taster Trio kits are flagged `is_tasting_kit` / `requires_review` (their
  handles carry a `taster-trio` token) for the admin review queue.
- Canonical product URLs are `/products/<slug>`.
