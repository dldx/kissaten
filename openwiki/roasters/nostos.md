# Nostos Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Nostos Coffee is a London-based specialty roaster and café group taking its name
from the Greek word *nostos* — homecoming. It runs two London locations: the
original café in Battersea ("where Nostos feels most at home") and Nostos St
James's near St James's Park, the self-styled "Home of the Experience Bar".
The storefront is Shopify at [nostoscoffee.co.uk](https://nostoscoffee.co.uk).
The company was incorporated in November 2020 as Nostoscoffebattersea Limited
(per Companies House). The shop leans into standout lots — the current pick at
the time of writing was a fully washed Geisha from a young-farmers cooperative
in Cajamarca, Peru.

## Address

- 10a Battersea Park Road, London, SW8 4FF, United Kingdom — registered office
  per Companies House; the site publishes no separate roastery address (the
  Battersea site is also their original café).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Sitewide banner: **free delivery on all UK orders above £40**.
- No roast/dispatch schedule is published on the site.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The name and identity revolve around homecoming — "a return to what truly
  matters: authenticity, quality and community" (per their site and location
  pages).
- The St James's site is framed as an innovation space: "bringing you the world
  coffee competition stage on your daily routine", showcasing different brew
  methods and techniques beyond standard café service.

## Scraping Quirks

- **Additive/water-product filtering**: the scraper excludes, by URL pattern,
  the water-treatment products the shop sells alongside coffee — Aquacode
  mineral concentrates and the Apax Lab box set (`aquacode`,
  `apax_box_set_3x_20ml`, `mineral-concentrates`) — along with the usual
  equipment/gift/subscription items.
