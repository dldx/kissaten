# Fritz — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Fritz Coffee Company is a Korean coffee company established in 2014. Its
storefront offers single-origin coffees, blends, cold brew, drip bags, capsules,
decaf and a coffee club, alongside merchandise and classes. The Korean site
labels some coffee products "DIRECT TRADE" and provides an English overseas-
shipping storefront link.

## Address

- South Korea — full roastery address not published on the consulted site pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The coffee-club notice says dispatch is the following Wednesday; the English
  club notice says "SHIPPING IS NEXT MONDAY". These notices appear to refer to
  different club views, so no single general roast schedule is asserted here.
- The Korean product pages state that orders are shipped after payment is
  confirmed and that mountainous or island regions incur an additional 5,000-
  9,000 KRW. The consulted pages do not publish a general free-shipping minimum.
