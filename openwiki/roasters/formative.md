# Formative Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Formative Coffee is a specialty coffee roaster and café in Victoria, central
London ("hidden between Westminster and Victoria"), opened in **March 2019** by
founder Ian. The shop took runner-up in Sprudge's "World's Best New Cafe" awards
in its first year. Ian has won multiple national championships — UK Barista
Champion 2023, winner of the **World Espresso Championship 2024** (per the site's
award banner) — and took 6th place at the World Barista Championship 2024. The
Shopify storefront sells coffee in four tiers (Benchmark, Exceptional, Future,
Competition), subscriptions, and gear, with a separate wholesale programme.

## Address

- London, United Kingdom — full roastery address not published on the site; the
  customer-facing store is at Butler Place, London SW1H 0RH (a café, open
  Mon–Fri 07:30–17:00, Sat–Sun 09:00–16:00)

## Schedules & Shipping

- **Free shipping on all orders within the UK** (no minimum stated); also ships
  across Europe.
- "Order before 12pm for next day dispatch" (per the site's shipping banner).
- Orders ship with tracking details provided by the courier.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Coffees are sold in four named tiers: **Benchmark** (reliable, classic
  profiles), **Exceptional** (bright, expressive lots), **Future** (pioneering
  processing and cutting-edge genetics), and **Competition** (rare lots with
  competition-stage potential).
- Nearly all coffees are **omni-roasted** — designed to work as both espresso
  and filter — with a focus on "clarity and balance".
- They recommend resting coffee at least **7 days** before brewing, with the
  cup at its best for up to 5 weeks after roast.
- Coffee bags are recyclable with **soft plastics** (supermarket collection
  points).
- Sourcing stance: they "work directly with some of the best coffee producers
  in the world, many of whom we know personally" (per their FAQ).
