# Ancoats Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Ancoats Coffee Co. was founded in **2013** in Manchester at the historic
**Royal Mills**, Ancoats M4 5BA. It is a WordPress + WooCommerce + Divi
storefront at [www.ancoats-coffee.co.uk](https://www.ancoats-coffee.co.uk). The
roastery is deliberately **open to view** — "each process is laid bare" — part of a
transparent approach to roasting and business.

## Address

- Royal Mills, Ancoats, Manchester M4 5BA — United Kingdom

## Sustainability

Ancoats has a dedicated sustainability page, unusual in its specificity:

- **Reusable plastic tubs** for wholesale deliveries — they claim to be the first
  Greater Manchester roastery to pioneer this
- Upcycled cardboard boxes for packaging
- Coffee chaff composted rather than wasted
- Imperfect roasts donated to local charities (e.g. the Ancoats-based
  Mustard Tree)
- GrainPro green-coffee bags reused as bin bags
- 20p discount for reusable cups, plus fully compostable single-use cups/cutlery
- Seasonal local ingredients; sourcing via direct trade when possible, with
  Rainforest Alliance or Organic certifications otherwise

## Roasting & Equipment

- **6kg Giesen roaster**
- Daily **QC cupping of every batch** — a ritual many roasters reserve for new
  lots only. Their site puts it bluntly: "Great coffee is no accident, it is the
  sum of every single act that goes into its production."

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Acts as a genuine circular-economy roastery (above).
- Sells a **"Global Tasting Experience"** tasting kit, which is flagged
  `is_tasting_kit` / `requires_review` and held out of public search until an
  admin approves it — per the [scraping-system tasting-kit policy](../scrapers/scraping-system.md).
