# Simple Kaffa — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Simple Kaffa (興波咖啡) is based in Taipei City, Taiwan. Its site combines an online shop with a journal and a multi-location café programme; the shop offers competition, light, and medium/dark roast categories as well as drip bags and accessories. The company says founder Berg Wu won the 2016 World Barista Championship, and lists Lu Chia-Chi as co-founder and an international coffee judge.

## Address

- No. 27, Sec. 2, Zhongxiao E. Rd., Zhongzheng Dist., Taipei City 100, Taiwan — published by Simple Kaffa as its contact/registered business address; the consulted pages do not identify it specifically as the roasting facility.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Simple Kaffa says paid orders are dispatched within three working days, excluding weekends and public holidays. Published delivery estimates are 2–3 working days within Taiwan, 3–7 days to China, 2–5 days to Hong Kong and Macau, 4–7 days to Singapore and Malaysia, 4–7 days to Japan and Korea, and 7–14 days to the US, Australia and New Zealand. Taiwan orders over **NT$1,500** ship free; Taiwan island orders over that amount receive a **NT$150** shipping rate; Hong Kong orders over **NT$2,500** ship free.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Simple Kaffa says its goal is to turn the company into a base for passing on craft and to maintain a consistent quality floor while leaving room for each barista's style. Its online orders do not offer grind-to-order service; customers in Taipei can bring an order to a shop for grinding.

## Scraping Quirks
