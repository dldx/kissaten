# Decaf Before Death — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Decaf Before Death (DBD) is a UK-based, decaf-only specialty coffee business
founded by Maria, a former "death before decaf" drinker who developed a
caffeine sensitivity and set out to prove decaf can be exceptional. It began
as "the first subscription in the world focused on exceptional decaf and low
caf coffee" and now sells one-off **Coffee Drops**, a quarterly **Decaf
Discovery Box** (4×60g, £28/quarter) and a low-waste **Sampler Box**
(5×35g, £10), alongside small-batch roasts from its own "nanoroastery". The
storefront is Shopify at
[decafbeforedeath.co.uk](https://www.decafbeforedeath.co.uk); founder Maria
was praised by Lance Hedrick as running "the coolest new subscription on the
block" (per their site).

## Address

- United Kingdom — full address not published on site (the contact
  information page lists only an email address, maria@decafbeforedeath.co.uk)

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Curates exceptional decafs and low-caf coffees from international and UK
  roasters, with guest line-ups in the Discovery Box including Hydrangea,
  Onyx, Fritz Coffee Company, Coffee Libre, Rogue Wave, Hatch and Zennor
  (per their site).
- Claims a European/US first: the **first EA (ethyl acetate) decaf Gesha**,
  sourced via Coffee Libre — offered nowhere else in Europe or the US
  (per their site).

## Roasting & Equipment

- Sources most coffees from partner roasters but also roasts some releases
  in-house in their small "nanoroastery" (e.g. their own Nogales and Bella
  Vista Pacamara decafs); the roasting machine is not named on the site.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Drops operate on a **pre-order model: pre-orders open Sundays at 12:00
  (UK time)** (site-wide banner).
- Discovery Boxes ship at the end of February, May, August and November;
  coffee is roasted fresh before shipping (UK roasters' coffee arrives within
  days of roast; international selections may be 1–2 weeks off roast).
- **Free UK delivery on orders over £36**; delivers to the UK, select EU
  countries and the USA, all tracked; international customers are responsible
  for VAT, duties and customs fees.
- Whole beans only — no ground coffee.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The name is a direct challenge to the "Death Before Decaf" motto — no
  compromises, "definitely no bland cups of disappointment".
- Exclusive focus on decaf and low-caf: no generic "Colombian decaf"; no
  dark roasts or chocolate-nutty defaults — expect El Paraiso thermal shock,
  pink bourbons and experimental processes.
- The founding cup: a natural-processed decaf by Jairo Arcila that "changed
  everything I thought I knew about decaf coffee".
- Subscriptions were discontinued ("We don't do subs anymore") — the business
  now centres on coffee drops and the quarterly Discovery Box.
