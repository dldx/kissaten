# Coffee Lab — Roaster Profile

## Overview

CoffeeLab is a specialty coffee roastery in Warsaw, Poland, built around
quality, precision and flavour. The official site is a PrestaShop storefront
with espresso, filter, omniroast, cascara, BrewLab and Magic Beans categories.
It says the business has been roasting for over a decade and also provides
barista training and equipment support.

## Address

- Roastery: ul. Bruszewska 28, 03-046 Warsaw — Poland.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- CoffeeLab says it selects raw coffee from plantations around the world and
  works with both classic single origins and less-obvious origins. No
  price-paid-to-producer or cost-breakdown figures were published on the
  reviewed pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The site advertises free delivery from 149 zł within Poland and from 449 zł
  in the rest of the EU. It says orders are shipped within 48 hours; the
  delivery page says packages are generally dispatched within two days after
  payment and sent by UPS with tracking.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Its three stated pillars are quality, precision and taste. The shop presents
  “Magic Beans” as a series for unusual varieties, processes and flavour
  profiles, alongside the more conventional espresso and filter lines.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper crawls two pages of the English coffee listing and recognises
  product links across several category paths (`filter`, `espresso`, `magic-
  beans`, `brewlab`, `cascara` and `omniroast`).
- `zestaw-brewlab` URLs are explicitly excluded as tasting-set products; other
  equipment, tea, accessories and gift categories are not part of the coffee
  URL selection.
