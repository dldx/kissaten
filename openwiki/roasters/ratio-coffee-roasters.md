# Ratio Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Ratio Coffee Roasters is a Salzburg specialty coffee roaster and coffee shop. Its site positions the business as “from locals for locals,” offering light and medium roast beans, guest coffees, natural wine and house-made food. The shop runs on WooCommerce and the scraper’s shop collection is the `/shop/` page.

## Address

- Salzburg, Austria — full roastery address not published on site. Linzergasse 72a is identified by the site as the coffee shop and is not used as a roastery address.

## Sustainability

- Ratio says its electric roaster is powered by green energy and describes it as Austria’s first zero-emission coffee roaster. It also says it sources local products where possible and looks for environmentally conscious concepts.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Ratio says it evaluates coffees and the circumstances of their trade before buying, and describes direct sourcing, small-batch importing and personal contact with farmers as the basis of its approach. No producer-price figures or cost breakdown were published.

## Roasting & Equipment

- The roastery page identifies a Giesen WE 6 series electric roaster. Ratio says it roasts small batches with a maximum of 6 kg, using a gentle process and personal control of the parameters affecting flavour.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The brand organises its thinking around “Quality. Transparency. Balance.” and explains the name through the mathematical idea of a ratio. The café also explicitly welcomes pets, including dogs, cats and llamas.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses custom WooCommerce selectors and retains only links matching `/product/`; its `postprocess_extracted_bean` method pins extracted currency to EUR.
