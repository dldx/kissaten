# Zero to One — Roaster Profile

## Overview

ZeroToOne (zerotoonecoffee.co.uk, Shopify) is a London specialty coffee shop
and roaster describing itself as **"London's first Vietnamese specialty coffee
shop"**. Its stated mission: "To change how the world sees Vietnamese coffee"
— sourcing curated beans directly from innovative farms across Vietnam and
roasting them in-house in London, rather than letting Vietnam's image stay tied
to mass-market blends. The company (Zerotoone Coffee Limited) was incorporated
in August 2023 per Companies House. The Widegate Street site is both café and
roastery — beans, drinks, matcha and events all under one roof — and the shop
was named among "The Best Coffee Shops in the United Kingdom" in both 2025 and
2026 (per their site).

## Address

- 19 Widegate Street, London E1 7HP — United Kingdom (the roastery sits in the
  basement of the shop, near Liverpool Street station)

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Sources beans **directly from pioneering/innovative farms across Vietnam**,
  spanning specialty arabica and fine robusta (per their site).
- While Vietnam is the focus, a **monthly special bean** series celebrates other
  origins.
- No price-transparency figures (FOB/farm-gate) are published.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Beans are roasted **by hand, in small batches, in the basement of the London
  shop**; no roasting machine brand or capacity is published.
- Roast-to-bar feedback loop: the same coffee is served in the café, retail and
  wholesale — "if a coffee doesn't work on bar, it doesn't move forward."
- They describe testing, tracking and using "data and AI-supported insights" to
  refine flavour and consistency (per their site).

## Schedules & Shipping

- **Roasted weekly**; orders usually processed within 1–3 working days and
  dispatched within 3–5 days of the roast date.
- Standard UK delivery 2–3 working days after dispatch.
- Free UK shipping: **over £20 for subscription orders, over £30 for one-time
  purchases** (shipping policy, updated 1 Dec 2025; note the site's checkout
  banner advertises £35/£25 — the FAQ also once quotes £25 — so figures vary
  across the site).
- **UK-only shipping** at present; international "coming soon".

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Championing Vietnamese coffee beyond cà phê sữa đá: the café menu runs from
  condensed-milk classics to robusta cortados, pomelo espresso and
  ceremonial-grade Kyoto matcha, with water custom-mineralised per brew (per
  their site and reviews).
- Award-adjacent culture: in their first year, team members took **1st Place at
  the English AeroPress Championship 2025** and **2nd Place at the CAFEC Brewers
  Cup 2025** "while representing our home".
- Whole beans only — no grinding — "to maintain maximum freshness"; coffee is
  best enjoyed within 4–6 weeks of roast.
- Coffee cannot be returned (fresh food product); damaged/incorrect orders are
  replaced if reported within 48 hours with photos.
