# Coffever — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Coffever is a small specialty roastery in Bath, England, run by people who
"are from Hong Kong and used to have our coffee shop back in Hong Kong from
2018". They moved to the UK and opened the shop at Northumberland Place, a
"cute alley" in central Bath, in April 2023, and began roasting their own
beans on-site in 2024. The name plays on both "**Coffee Ever**" and "**Coffee
Fever**" — "both shows our freaking love to coffee". Shopify storefront.

## Address

- 14 Northumberland Place, Bath, BA1 5AR, Somerset, United Kingdom (shop and
  on-site roastery; registered office of COFFEVER LTD)

## Roasting & Equipment

- Roasts on-site at the Northumberland Place shop (started roasting their own
  beans in 2024).
- **Light roast focus** — "to preserve the natural flavors from the coffee
  beans", with an East–West angle: "Hong Kong is a unique mix of Asian &
  Western culture. We hope to bring you guys some new sensory experience."

## Schedules & Shipping

- **Roast every Wednesday; orders shipped Thursday.**
- Cut-off is **10pm every Monday** — orders placed after that are prepared and
  shipped the following week.
- Orders are dispatched with stock roasted within a maximum of 7 days prior;
  they recommend brewing from 7 days after roast date, peaking at 14 days.
- No free-delivery minimum or per-region rates are published on the shipping
  policy page.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- "Coffever, it's all about the love of coffee" — the shop name reads as both
  *Coffee Ever* and *Coffee Fever*.
- The name was carried from the 2018 Hong Kong shop, so the Instagram handle
  is still `coffever2018`.
- Single-origin-led range with a strong Asia-Pacific presence for a UK
  roaster — e.g. Ijen Highlands (Indonesia) alongside Colombia, Ethiopia and
  Kenya lots (such as Kenya Gatomboya AB and a Colombia Geisha from the Attia
  producer).
- Waffle-y newsletter voice: "We are together on a journey to explore the
  diversity of the coffee world."
