# Swerl — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Swerl is a Swedish micro-roastery founded in Falkenberg in **2019** by Beatriz
and Daniel. It began as a converted 1972 Mercedes mobile coffee bar, closed its
coffee shops in 2023 and now focuses on seasonal filter, espresso, odd-bean and
test-roast coffees. The Shopify shop also offers a coffee subscription and
wholesale.

## Address

- Falkenberg, Sweden — the site describes its new roastery as near the harbour
  but does not publish a visitor/dispatch street address; the registered address
  is explicitly marked “no visitors”.

## Sustainability

- Swerl says it uses DHL Express's GoGreen Plus service and sustainable aviation
  fuel for shipments to reduce associated CO2e emissions.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Swerl's approach emphasizes seasonality, traceability and long-term
  relationships. It names direct-trade partners including the Barreto family in
  Brazil, importer Semilla and Colombian producer Jhon Jairo, while noting that
  it also works with importers. No FOB or producer price figures are published
  on the sourcing page.

## Roasting & Equipment

- In 2021 Swerl bought a second-hand **15 kg Giesen** roaster. In 2023 it moved
  to a larger facility and added a colour sorter for quakers, burnt and broken
  beans. The current roasting machine is not separately identified.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Swerl aspires to roast to order and says it will not ship coffee more than 14
  days from roast. Weekend orders are sent no later than the following Thursday;
  the target is shipment within four working days. Normal delivery is **1-3
  working days in Sweden**, **3-7 in Europe** and **5-10 outside Europe**.
- Sweden shipping is **69 SEK**, free over **550 SEK**; EU shipping is **12 EUR**,
  free over **85 EUR**; UK shipping is **10 GBP**, free over **85 GBP**; USA
  shipping is **32 USD**, free over **200 USD**. Rest-of-world shipping is
  **15-35 EUR**, calculated at checkout.
