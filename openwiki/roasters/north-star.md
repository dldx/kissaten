# North Star Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

North Star was founded in **Leeds in 2013** with the belief that "coffee can
and should create lasting value for everyone connected to it". It has grown
from a small specialty roastery into a business spanning sourcing, roasting,
wholesale, hospitality and education — operating the **North Star Academy**
training platform plus several cafés across Leeds (Leeds Dock, Sovereign
Street, Victoria Quarter, Great George Street). A certified **B Corporation**
whose stated focus is "impact over profit". The Shopify storefront is at
[northstarroast.com](https://www.northstarroast.com).

## Address

- Unit 10, 280 Tong Road, Armley, Leeds LS12 3BG — United Kingdom (the
  roastery; visits limited to collections, events or invited experiences).

## Sustainability

- **First coffee company to introduce home-compostable retail packaging in
  2022** — 250g bags from cellulose, kraft paper and sugarcane with
  compostable inks, valves and seals; 1kg bags currently recyclable LDPE4.
  Used bags can be returned to the roastery for processing.
- **Reusable 4kg tin system** for local wholesale partners — tins are
  collected, cleaned and refilled in a circular delivery loop, removing
  single-use wholesale packaging; local wholesale deliveries run on their
  own **electric vehicle**.
- Waste minimisation: coffee grounds offered to customers as fertiliser,
  sacks donated for reuse, coffee chaff repurposed for agricultural
  composting.
- Delivery partners with lower-emission logistics; roasting on Loring
  Smart Roast for reduced emissions.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Long-term relationships: returning to the same producing partners year
  after year, with **prices set by the producer or trusted exporter at a
  rate that they identify to be viable**.
- **Impact Fund** supporting social, environmental and community initiatives
  at origin and closer to home.
- **Rwanda "Ijabo" project** (since 2021, with export partner Tropic Coffee
  and wholesale partner Baltzersens): 20 young people at Muremure Farm,
  Nyamasheke each received coffee trees to manage, organic compost, agronomy
  and financial-literacy training across 8 hectares with 20,000 trees
  planted — zero dropout so far.
- **El Salvador project**: invested in processing infrastructure for producer
  Maria, who has tripled her income from coffee.
- Decaf comes from a long-standing Brazilian partner using CO2
  decaffeination.

## Roasting & Equipment

- All coffees roasted in Leeds on **Loring Smart Roast** technology
  (hot-air, low-emission roasting).
- Roast date printed on every bag; coffees split into a **Base Range**
  (balanced, chocolate/nut) and an **Explore Range** (brighter, fruitier).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders placed **before 12pm Mon–Fri are dispatched the same working day**;
  weekend orders process from Monday.
- **£4.10 DPD** (next working day, fully tracked) or **£3.50 InPost locker**
  delivery for orders under £25; **free shipping over £25**.
- Subscriptions roast and ship fresh from Leeds on the chosen schedule.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Tagline mindset: "better coffee systems can create better outcomes for
  everyone connected to them" — B Corp certification formally embeds
  stakeholder responsibility into the company structure.
- Runs a working **Theory of Change** and publishes impact work, insisting
  "businesses should not be celebrated simply for doing the things they
  should already be doing."
- **North Star Academy**: hands-on coffee training for home brewers and
  hospitality professionals.
- The Docks house blend recently rebranded with an illustration by North Star
  barista and illustrator Jesse, inspired by morning walkers at Leeds Dock.

## Scraping Quirks

- **Non-coffee handle exclusion**: `subscription`, `bundle` and
  `variety-pack` substrings are dropped — chosen because they match every
  multi-bag/gift/subscription handle while no coffee-bean handle contains
  them (verified against the full catalogue).
- Product URLs are canonicalised to
  `https://www.northstarroast.com/products/<handle>` so duplicate listings
  across the site's overlapping coffee collections never get scraped twice.
