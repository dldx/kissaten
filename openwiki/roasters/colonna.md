# Colonna — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Colonna is a UK specialty roaster whose coffee journey began with the Colonna &
Small's shop in Bath in 2009 and whose roastery launched in 2015. It sells
rotating Foundation, Discovery, Rare, Gesha, SOLO and decaf coffees, as well as
capsules and instant coffee, from a Shopify storefront. Its Colonna & Small's
shops in Bath and London are retail locations, not the roastery.

## Address

- Bath, England, United Kingdom — full roastery address not published on site.

## Sustainability

- The FAQ says all coffee bags are recyclable. UK customers can recycle
  Colonna's plastic-free aluminium capsules through Podback; compostable
  capsules require commercial composting or a compatible local food-waste
  service.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Colonna says it has co-authored research on water science, frozen grinding,
  sensory science and sustainability. Its FAQ says coffees are roasted
  throughout the week; espresso/filter refers to the roast approach, and some
  coffees are treated as omniroasts.

## Schedules & Shipping

- UK standard Royal Mail delivery is estimated at 2 working days after the
  shipping notification. International Royal Mail Standard International times
  are shown at checkout, and tracked courier options are available for UK and
  international orders. Coffees are roasted throughout the week and fulfilled
  as quickly as possible.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Colonna describes its work as exploration and innovation. Its research-led
  projects include specialty-coffee capsule technology and the Peak Water
  Project. The shop's monthly coffee slots rotate the underlying coffee rather
  than keeping a permanently fixed origin.

## Scraping Quirks

- Colonna keeps stable Shopify handles for rotating coffee slots. The scraper
  appends a hash of each product's `body_html` to the URL fragment so an origin,
  process or tasting-note change is treated as a new product. It keeps the
  legitimate `beans-discovery` rotating coffee slot while excluding the
  `beans-all-3` bundle.
