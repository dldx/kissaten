# Ona Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Ona Coffee is an Australian specialty roaster based in Canberra. The registered
Kissaten scraper describes a catalogue centred on single-origin coffees and
distinctive blends; the store is the roaster's own online shop.

## Address

- Canberra, Australia — full roastery address not published on the consulted site pages.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper checks six separate Shopify collections (filter, espresso,
  milk-based, reserve, rare coffee and special release), then uses AI extraction
  on the resulting product pages. It excludes subscriptions, gifts, equipment,
  drip bags, candy, merchandise, brewing accessories, wholesale, bundles,
  `the-dog-house-coffee-collection` and Paragon products.
