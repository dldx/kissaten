# Bonanza — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Bonanza Coffee Roasters is a Berlin specialty roaster that says it has been working in European specialty coffee since 2006. The shop presents seasonal single-origin coffees and blends such as Gogogu and Gogogu Decaf, plus subscriptions, wholesale coffee, and four Berlin cafe locations. Its stated focus is clarity, sweetness, and expression in coffees roasted in Berlin.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Bonanza allows 2-5 business days for processing and says coffees are roasted within eight days before shipping unless stated otherwise. Germany shipping is free from EUR 40 or 700 g of coffee; Europe shipping is free from EUR 75 or 1.5 kg. Published delivery estimates are four business days in Germany, 7-10 business days in Europe, and 5-15 business days to the USA and Canada.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The current site uses the phrase "Unnecessarily Good Coffee" and describes the company as unnecessarily driven, curious, and precise. Its menu combines fresh seasonal coffee with curated sets and a reusable Gogogu refill jar.

## Scraping Quirks

- Bonanza's Shopify JSON endpoint is the coffee collection, but product URLs returned under a collection path are rewritten to the canonical `/products/<handle>` form. The scraper does not fetch product pages and explicitly translates German product content to English; set, subscription, equipment, merch, capsules, and other non-bean slugs are excluded.
