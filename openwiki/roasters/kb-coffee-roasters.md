# KB — Roaster Profile

## Overview

KB is a French specialty coffee company that began in Paris in 2010 and
started its own roasting operation in 2015. It runs the Back In Black coffee
shop and sells single origins, espresso and ground or whole-bean coffee from a
Shopify storefront; its site also offers a monthly subscription.

## Address

- Paris, France — full roastery address not published on site.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The storefront advertises free delivery in France from EUR 99.00. The
  reviewed shipping-policy page does not publish dispatch days or delivery
  times.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- KB describes its coffees as unusual and memorable, and its catalogue includes
  technical and experimental processes alongside more classic offerings.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper fetches the Shopify `all` collection but selects products by the
  `Coffee` tag, because the collection also contains books, equipment, gifts
  and merch. It pins the store currency to EUR, translates French extraction
  to English, and canonicalizes product URLs to `/products/<handle>`.
