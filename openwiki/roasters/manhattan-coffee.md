# Manhattan Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Manhattan Coffee Roasters is a specialty roaster rooted in Rotterdam, Netherlands,
established in 2017. Its WooCommerce storefront presents coffees by categories such
as Specialty, Rare and Exceptional, including single origins, blends and limited
projects. The company says it works with producers and traders to push coffee
craftsmanship, with provenance, flavour, ethics and sustainability central to its
approach. Its contact page identifies the premises as a factory, not a shop or cafe.

## Address

- Rotterdam, Netherlands — full roastery address not published on site.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Manhattan describes its buying as responsible and ethical, and says it builds
  long-term partnerships with producers who use thoughtful cultivation and
  processing practices. No per-lot FOB or farm-gate price figures were found on
  the reviewed pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The roastery operates Monday-Friday, 09:00-17:00 CET. Orders placed before 09:00
  are roasted, hand-packed and sent within 3 business days. DHL eCommerce is used
  in Europe and DHL Express worldwide. Shipping times and prices vary by location;
  no free-shipping threshold is published on the shipping page.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The name references Rotterdam's historic Holland America Line connection with
  New York and the local phrase "Manhattan aan de Maas."

## Scraping Quirks

- Registry key is `manhattan-coffee`; the scraper uses the WooCommerce
  `/products/coffees/` category, skips cards marked `outofstock`, narrows product
  pages to `div.product`, and forces prices to EUR because the pages do not expose
  a usable currency meta tag.
