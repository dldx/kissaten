# Ovenbird — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Ovenbird (ovenbird.co.uk) is a specialty coffee roaster that started in
Glasgow in 2013 (per their site) and still roasts there in small batches,
shipping across Scotland and beyond. It runs on a Shopify storefront in GBP;
the whole-bean catalogue merges single origins and blends into roughly 10
products (whisky tie-in collections, gift items and machines are filtered
out of the bean catalogue).

## Address

- Full street address not published on site — Glasgow, Scotland, United
  Kingdom (roasts in small batches in Glasgow, per their site).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Free UK shipping on orders over £34.
- Orders are shipped the day after processing, Monday to Friday via Royal
  Mail (except holidays).

## Scraping Quirks

- Shopify — scraped via JSON only.
- The whole-bean catalogue merges single origins and blends; whisky
  tie-in coffee collections, gift products and machines are excluded so
  only genuine whole-bean coffee lands in the catalogue.
