# Standout Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Standout Coffee AB is a Swedish specialty roaster based in Stockholm, using a
Shopify storefront. It says its role is to select rather than simply roast:
the team tastes thousands of coffees each year and releases a small selection
of specialty, espresso and competition coffees. The company says it has
shipped to more than 90 countries and was named Best Nordic Roaster 2024 in
the Nordic Roast Awards public-choice category.

## Address

- Stockholm, Sweden — the site publishes Frihamnsgatan 24, 115 56 Stockholm
  as its contact address, but does not identify it separately as the roastery
  address.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Standout describes long-running relationships with progressive producers and
  names producers such as Jamison Savage, Nestor Lasso and Hacienda La
  Esmeralda. No price-paid-to-producer or cost-breakdown figures were found on
  the reviewed pages.

## Roasting & Equipment

- The site says coffees are roasted fresh every week in Stockholm. It does not
  publish the brand, model or capacity of the roasting machine on the reviewed
  pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The site advertises free shipping in the EU over €100 and free tracked EU
  shipping on subscriptions. It says subscriptions generally arrive in 3–5
  days in Europe; destinations outside Europe can take 8–12 days.
- PostNord and UPS are identified as the shipment carriers. International
  rates vary by destination and are not presented as a complete public rate
  table on the reviewed pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The company positions selection as its central craft: only coffees that
  “stand apart” after extensive tasting become Standout coffees. Its catalogue
  unusually combines rare competition lots with regular subscriptions.

## Scraping Quirks

- The Shopify scraper reads `/collections/all/products.json`, does not fetch
  individual product pages, and canonicalises `/collections/all` product URLs.
- A long explicit slug deny-list removes equipment, accessories, capsules,
  subscriptions and other non-bean products. Weight corrections change 311 g
  to 250 g and 1007 g to 1000 g in extracted options.
