# Caribe Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Caribe Coffee Co. is a family-run roaster in Morpeth, Northumberland,
started by **Wilmer and Elle Carcamo**. Wilmer grew up in Honduras (his
grandmother farmed coffee) and met Elle — a Northumberland native with an
MBA from Durham — while working in retail in the Cayman Islands; the couple
moved to the North East of England and imported their first batch of
Honduran speciality coffee in **July 2018**. They built the business through
farmers' markets (Wilmer won the National Young Market grocery award in
2019) before focusing on retail. The Shopify storefront is at
[caribecoffeeco.com](https://www.caribecoffeeco.com) (caribecoffee.co.uk
redirects there), and the company also operates a second roastery in George
Town, Grand Cayman.

## Address

- Caribe Coffee Co. Roastery, Fairmoor (Fivehills), Morpeth, Northumberland,
  NE61 3JL — United Kingdom

## Sustainability

- Checkout **tips section** raising funds that go directly to partner
  farmers and their families — equipment to improve farming practice.
- **WWF jaguar adoption** for their Jaguar coffee customers, and WWF
  donations for every Christmas gift pack sold.
- Currently recyclable packaging, with a stated aim of moving to fully
  home-compostable and biodegradable packaging.
- Refill (jar refill) option for local customers via click-and-collect to
  reduce packaging.
- All partner farms use environmentally sensitive farming practices that let
  native flora and fauna cohabit the land.
- Sponsors a local kids' football academy.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Direct partnership with **Honduran coffee farm cooperatives covering over
  5,000 farms**, personally visited; each harvest ships with a unique farm
  profile detailing the coffee's history (see their "Our Farms" blog).
- Direct importing cuts out intermediaries — competitive prices without
  compromising producers; coffee is speciality standard, **Q-graded 84+**.
- Their "**Direct Coffee Partners**" platform lets others buy and import
  green coffee directly — from a single 60kg jute sack to 5+ containers per
  calendar year, using Caribe's spare container space.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Own roastery and warehouse in Morpeth; roast profiles developed in-house.
- The roaster machine is named **"Roasty McRoast Face"** — running since
  their first day of in-house roasting.
- Coffee is **fresh roasted and packed to order**.

## Schedules & Shipping

- **Free delivery with no minimum spend** (per their shipping page).
- Orders dispatched within **5 working days** (roast-and-pack-to-order lead
  time); UK mainland generally sent via **Royal Mail 48 hour** delivery; an
  optional 24-hour delivery upgrade is available at checkout.
- Local delivery by their own van from the Fairmoor roastery for nearby
  communities; click-and-collect available.
- Orders placed after 12pm Saturday are dispatched on Monday; international
  delivery quoted per country and order size.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Wilmer's story is the brand: a Honduran childhood, paying off his mum's
  mortgage by 20, rising from shelf-stacker to store manager in the Cayman
  Islands, then bringing proper Central American coffee to North East
  England.
- Guided by the "three Ps": **People, Planet and Profit** — and a mission to
  push back against coffee as a "poverty crop".
- Runs a "Married to the Boss" podcast and markets through coffee
  ambassadors and market events across the North East.

## Scraping Quirks

- The store sells far more than coffee — a coffee van hire, Fracino espresso
  machines, water filters, cookies, compostable cups, AirTag key rings and
  more — so the scraper carries a long Caribe-specific slug exclusion list
  (`coffee-van`, `coffee-machine`, `water-filter`, `cookie`, `blender`,
  `knock-box`, `compostable`, `boiler`, …).
- The site is scraped **JSON-only**: `products.json`'s `body_html` already
  carries the full bean detail (SCA/cupping score, certifications, origin,
  altitude, varietals, process, tasting notes), so product pages are never
  fetched.
