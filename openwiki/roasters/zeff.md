# ZEFF. Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

ZEFF. Coffee is a Swiss specialty coffee micro-roaster whose Shopify shop sells filter and espresso coffees, decaf, gear and limited projects. Its current storefront highlights a four-coffee Fermentation Project made with James Hoffmann and Lucia Solis, alongside coffees such as Colombia Red Bourbon, Ethiopia Yirgacheffe, Colombia Chiroso and its Daily Driver house selection.

## Address

- Wetzikon, Switzerland — the roaster’s town is identified in the registered scraper declaration, but the consulted public site does not publish a full roastery address.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The storefront advertises free shipping for all orders from CHF 100. No roast cadence, dispatch days or destination-specific rates are published on the consulted page.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- ZEFF’s product range includes experimental coffees and controlled-comparison projects: the Fermentation Project presents one farm and harvest processed four ways and roasted the same way so customers can compare fermentation effects.

## Scraping Quirks

- The Shopify scraper reads the all-products JSON endpoint, canonicalizes collection product links to `/products/<handle>`, and excludes subscriptions, gifts, wholesale items, equipment, brewing accessories, apparel, capsules/pods, cold-brew cans, easy-pour products and the Daily Driver handle. Product pages and optimized screenshots are used for AI extraction.
