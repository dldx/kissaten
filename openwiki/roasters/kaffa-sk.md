# Kaffa Roastery SK — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Kaffa Roastery SK is the Slovak specialty-roasting brand of GASTROVIA group s.r.o. Its WordPress/WooCommerce shop presents filter coffees, modern espresso, traditional espresso and limited editions, with product pages describing farm, variety, process and flavour. The company’s stated values are consistency, sustainability and authenticity.

## Address

- Budovateľská 1288, Vranov nad Topľou, Slovakia — the site labels this location “Kaffa Pražiareň” (Kaffa roastery). A separate Gastrovia Lab and pickup/business address is not used here.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Kaffa says it chooses green coffees from farms whose stories reflect their place and people, seeking varieties and processing that show the farmer’s care. No price-paid-to-producer figures, certifications or cost breakdown were published on the consulted pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The terms say orders are generally dispatched within three working days of confirmation by courier or personal pickup. Current delivery prices are shown in the checkout; no destination-specific rates or free-delivery minimum were published in the consulted terms.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The roastery describes coffee as fruit whose character should be developed in the roast while preserving origin, with a desired balance between sweetness and fruitiness and between cleanliness and body.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper checks two paginated `/shop/` pages and extracts WooCommerce `/produkt/` links. Merchandise is mixed into the shop grid, so it excludes Slovak and English apparel, bag, gift-card and subscription slugs. Stock is not visible on the listing; sold-out handling is left to product-page extraction and the normal diff flow.
