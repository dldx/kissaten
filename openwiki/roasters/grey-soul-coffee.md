# Grey Soul Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Grey Soul Coffee is an Indian specialty coffee roaster with a Shopify store. Its coffee catalogue says coffees are sourced, hulled, profiled and roasted from regions including Nagaland, Tripura, Mizoram, Assam, Meghalaya, Chikmagalur, Coorg, Kodaikanal and Yercaud. The business also operates café locations in Pune and Mumbai, which are separate from the roastery address.

## Address

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Grey Soul’s catalogue names Indian growing regions and presents roast levels such as Ultra-Light, Light, Light-Medium, Medium and Medium-Dark. No producer-price, FOB, farm-gate or cost-breakdown figures were found.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The shop links a dedicated Shipping & Delivery page and a shipment-tracking service. Shipping rates, thresholds and dispatch cadence were not accessible in the pages checked.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The range includes named, profile-led coffees such as Pink Lemonade (Ultra-Light), Supernaturals (Light) and Roasters Espresso (Medium), alongside ready-to-brew drip bags and handmade sensory cups.
