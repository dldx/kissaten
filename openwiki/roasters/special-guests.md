# Special Guests Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Special Guests Coffee is a London roastery "for those who seek their daily
cup, or something entirely new", founded in **2022** by **Paul Ross**, two-time
UK Barista Championship winner. The Shopify storefront sells a seasonally
curated lineup of rare and competition-level lots — Panama Geishas (Kotowa,
Lerida, Janson), Inmaculada Signature Sudan Rume, Gesha Village, Acacia Hills —
organised into **Limited**, **Award-Winning** and **Quality** collections, with
numbered releases (e.g. "Release 068", "UN084") and a Past Releases archive.
The Marylebone café at 8 Aybrook Street was shortlisted for Best Independent
Coffee Shop Europe 2025 by Allegra. Wholesale runs through OrderSpace.

## Address

- London — United Kingdom (roastery address not published on site; the
  Location page lists only the Marylebone café at 8 Aybrook Street,
  London W1U 4AW, which is a café rather than the roastery)

## Schedules & Shipping

- Per their FAQ: coffee aims to arrive **within one week of roasting**.
- Subscriptions are **dispatched on the 1st of the month** (or next business
  day).
- UK parcels go via **Royal Mail**; international via a tracked service.
- **Free UK shipping over £25** (site-wide banner: "Complimentary UK Shipping
  over £25").
- Whole bean only — no ground-coffee option (per their FAQ).

## Philosophy & Quirks

- A champion-barista's shop window: lots are "selected by a team of UK Barista
  champions" and pitched as "the most unique, rare and hard to find lots from
  the world's most prized producers".
- Release numbering is archive-friendly: each coffee is a numbered release
  with a Past Releases page giving brew guidance, and brewing starts from a
  60g/litre ratio baseline (per their FAQ).
