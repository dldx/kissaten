# Kurasu — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Kurasu is a Kyoto specialty coffee company whose name comes from the Japanese
word for living or lifestyle. Founded as a homeware shop in 2013 and focused
on coffee from 2015, it now runs online and physical stores, a global
subscription and a Shopify storefront for coffee, tea and Japanese brewing
equipment.

## Address

- 537-7 Sujikaibashicho, Kamigyo-ku, Kyoto, Japan.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Kurasu moved its roastery from Fushimi Inari to Nishijin in August 2021. The
  Nishijin site uses a Giesen W6A and a Loring S35 Kestrel; the current global
  stores page describes the Loring as a 35kg roaster. The roastery is open to
  the public once a month and has a 24/7 coffee-bean vending machine beside
  the entrance.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders including coffee beans are dispatched in 5–10 business days. Kurasu
  counts those orders on Monday afternoon JST and dispatches them the Friday
  of that week; support and shipping are closed weekends and national
  holidays.
- International shipping is primarily DHL, with EMS, FedEx and UPS available
  in some countries. The published estimate after shipping is 1–5 business
  days in Asia, 2–7 in Australia and New Zealand, 2–7 in the Middle East,
  2–7 in North America, 3–14 in South America, 2–7 in the EU and UK, and 3–7
  in Africa. EMS typically adds a week.
- The stated worldwide flat rate is $35 USD, with free shipping over $120
  USD. Import duties and taxes are the customer's responsibility.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Kurasu's stated vision is to spread coffee culture from Kyoto to the globe;
  its overseas branches are locally managed while sharing that core mission.
