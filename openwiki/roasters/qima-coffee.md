# Qima Cafe — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Qima Cafe is a London coffee company whose Shopify storefront presents a “tree to cup” model. It sells coffees in Daily Ritual, Best Served Bold and Revolutionary ranges and operates cafés in Fitzrovia and Covent Garden. The site connects its coffees to work in Yemen, Colombia and Peru, including an Origins Blend that combines coffees from those three countries.

## Address

- London, United Kingdom — full roastery address not published on site. The listed Warren Street and New Row locations are cafés and are intentionally omitted.

## Sustainability

- Through Qima Foundation’s Coffee Tree Per Cup programme, Qima says every cup sold funds a young coffee tree for a farmer. Its storefront also says the company cultivates and distributes young coffee trees, and describes connected planting, processing, roasting and pouring across its supply chain.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Qima publishes unusually detailed lot narratives: product pages identify origin, district, producer context, variety, altitude and processing. It describes its work as building coffee “from the ground up” rather than sourcing anonymously, but the fetched pages did not publish producer prices or a cost breakdown.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The site’s organising idea is “tree to cup,” with the repeated stages Plant, Process and Pour. Its coffee ranges deliberately span everyday coffee, expressive coffees and experimental “Revolutionary” releases.
