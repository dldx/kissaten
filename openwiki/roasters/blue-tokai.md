# Blue Tokai Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Blue Tokai Coffee Roasters is a Delhi-based Indian specialty coffee roaster. Its Shopify storefront sells roasted coffee, single origins, blends, subscriptions, easy-pour and cold-brew formats, concentrates, equipment and gifts. The site also maintains dedicated pages for its farms, roasteries, packaging and partnerships.
