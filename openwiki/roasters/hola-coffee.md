# Hola Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Hola Coffee Roasters is a Spanish specialty coffee roaster based in Madrid, with a Shopify storefront in Spanish and English. The shop sells seasonal single origins, signature blends, decaf, cold brew, pods, packs and subscriptions, and also offers barista courses and professional ordering. Its footer identifies Avenida de Pedro Díez 21 as the Academy, Roastery & Head Quarters.

## Address

- Avenida de Pedro Díez, 21, 28019 Madrid, Spain.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Hola offers monthly subscriptions and says customers can select the delivery frequency that suits them; the English storefront advertises discounts of up to 10% for monthly subscriptions. A dispatch cadence, free-delivery minimum or destination-specific shipping table was not accessible on the pages checked.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The catalogue mixes enduring house blends such as Lucero and San Isidro with rotating seasonal coffees and an explicit “Roasters pick” subscription. A discovery pack contains four coffees of 100 g.
