# Asylum Coffeehouse — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Asylum Coffeehouse is a Singapore specialty coffee shop selling through a
Shopify storefront at [www.asylumcoffeehouse.com](https://www.asylumcoffeehouse.com),
presented in **SGD** with an en-SG locale. Its lineup is filter-coffee focused:
Colombian filter coffees (Yellow Sudan Rume Natural, Pink Bourbon Honey + Rose,
Caturra Natural, Castillo Honey + Green Apple), a **Sunrise Decaf**
(washed + maceration + sugarcane process), and a **Peru Washed Gesha** — plus
the house **Keluak** espresso blend. The shop also stocks April brewing gear
(the **April Plastic Brewer** and April filter papers) and its own house cup,
the **Asylum Bloom Cup (A.B.C.)**. This company is unrelated to the UK roaster
"Asylum Coffee" (Swindon, `asylumcoffee.co.uk`) — see
[Asylum Coffee](asylum.md).

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Taglines per their homepage: "**Crafted with Care, Brewed with Passion**",
  and for the filter line "From First Drip to Last Sip – Brew Like You Mean It".
- **FREE DELIVERY ON LOCAL ORDERS ABOVE $50** (per their site).
- The espresso blend is named **Keluak** — per the product page, a nod to a
  Singapore Peranakan dish, *Ayam Buah Keluak* (chicken in tamarind gravy with
  the fruit of the keluak seed).
- Sells the **April Plastic Brewer** and **April Filter Papers** as house
  brewing gear, alongside the **Asylum Bloom Cup (A.B.C.)**, a bloom-enhancing
  cup with a wide flared lip and narrow waist.
- Site nav organised into Espresso Beans / Filter Beans / Coffee Gear /
  Capsule Coffee / Our Approach / Our Community (per their site).
- Instagram: [@asylumcoffeesg](https://www.instagram.com/asylumcoffeesg) (per
  their site footer).

## Scraping Quirks
