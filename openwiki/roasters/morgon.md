# Morgon — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Morgon is a specialty coffee roaster founded in Gothenburg, Sweden, in summer
2018 by Markus Vestergaard and Gabriella Sand. The Shopify storefront offers
seasonal filter and espresso coffees, subscriptions, bundles and gifts. The founders
describe the coffees as bright, seasonal and sustainably sourced, selected through
long-term work with producers they have known for years.

## Address

- Anders Carlssons Gata 30, 417 55 Gothenburg, Sweden.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Webshop orders ship weekly on Tuesdays and Thursdays; subscriptions ship monthly
  on the first Thursday. The site states average delivery times of 1-2 days in
  Sweden, 5-7 days in Europe and 7-10 days in the rest of the world. Free tracked
  shipping is offered in Sweden for two 250g bags and internationally for four to
  seven 250g bags.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Morgon's square blue bags are inspired by shipping containers around the old
  shipyard beside its Gothenburg roastery, reflecting the journey of coffee from
  origin to the docks.

## Scraping Quirks

- Registry key is `morgon`. The scraper combines the `coffee` and `espresso`
  Shopify collections, strips collection segments from product URLs, excludes
  non-coffee slugs, removes `Accept-Language` to retain base-market SEK prices,
  and forces the extracted currency to SEK.
