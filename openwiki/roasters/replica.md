# Replica — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Replica is a Belgian specialty coffee roaster based in Leuven. Its Shopify storefront focuses on carefully sourced single-origin coffees and seasonal selections, with subscriptions and wholesale alongside the public shop.

## Address

- Wijgmaalsesteenweg 168, Leuven — Belgium.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The storefront publishes free-shipping thresholds of €50 for Belgium and the Netherlands, and €60 for Germany and Luxembourg. The fetched banner did not state the paid rates below those thresholds or the dispatch cadence.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper reads the Shopify `products.json` catalogue, fetches product pages, and uses an optimised extraction mode. It excludes subscriptions, merchandise, apparel, capsules/pods, equipment, gifts and the `test-roast` slug.
