# Weekenders Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Weekenders Coffee is a Kyoto coffee roaster whose site traces the business from a café start in 2005 to in-house roasting in 2011 and a roastery move in 2019. The shop sells origin coffees and brewing goods through a small, largely static Japanese/English online storefront. The site says the former Tominokoji store permanently closed in April 2026 and separately lists the roastery.

## Address

- 682-7 Ishihudonocho, Shimogyo-ku, Kyoto 600-8047, Japan

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Weekenders says it selects green coffee through origin buying, draws out each coffee’s character through roasting, and is pleased that the proportion of processes in which it can participate increases over time. No price-paid-to-producer figures, importer names or certification claims are published on the consulted pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- A site news notice states that online-shop shipping days are Tuesday and Saturday. The roastery is described as open irregularly, with visitors directed to its roastery Instagram for current information. No free-delivery minimum or destination-specific shipping rates are published on the consulted pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The roaster’s stated aim is to contribute a new coffee culture while respecting Kyoto’s machiya buildings, traditions and kissaten culture. It describes its target style as coffee with pronounced sweetness and acidity, lingering finish and dimensionality.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper reads a static HTML shop, constructs product URLs from `beans/` links and appends the parent directory as a URL fragment. It excludes URLs containing `tasting`, `driphome` and `iced-`; in particular, any tasting product currently matching that rule will not reach the catalogue and should be reviewed if the scraper is changed.
