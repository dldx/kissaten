# Nubra Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Nubra Coffee Roasters is registered as a Spanish specialty roaster with a
Shopify storefront. Its English site presents coffees from Central and South
America and Africa, with product pages that include variety, process, region,
altitude, sensory profile and producer information. Nubra’s stated philosophy
is to complete the circle between origin, roasting and service; the reviewed
pages do not publish a city or full roastery address.

## Address

- Spain — full roastery address not published on site.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Nubra explicitly labels a site section “Traceability and Transparency” and
  publishes substantial producer and farm context on product pages, including
  information for Janson Coffee, Cata Community Huila and Café Granja La
  Esperanza. The reviewed pages do not publish price-paid or FOB figures.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The site banner states that Nubra roasts every Tuesday and Thursday. The
  reviewed pages did not expose a public dispatch timeframe, free-delivery
  minimum or destination-specific shipping rate.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Nubra says it selects coffees, roasts them to enhance their individual
  character and serves them as part of one experience linking origin, roasting
  and service.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper consumes Shopify’s `/en/collections/all/products.json`, rewrites
  product URLs to the localized `/en/products/<handle>` form, uses optimized
  Shopify extraction when available, and translates the AI-extracted product
  data to English.
