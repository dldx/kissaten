# HEX — Roaster Profile

## Overview

HEX is a specialty coffee roaster in Charlotte, North Carolina. It began as a
pop-up in 2015 and now operates a coffee, kitchen and natural-wine venue; the
online shop is a Shopify storefront. Its stated focus is bright, vibrant
coffees whose provenance and producer work remain visible.

## Address

- 2923 Griffith St, Charlotte, NC 28203, United States.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- HEX says it prioritizes direct relationships over Fair Trade or Organic
  labels and pays growers above standard rates, including Fair Trade rates.
  It does not publish a per-lot price breakdown on the pages reviewed.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders are aggregated Monday at midnight for shipment that week; orders
  after the cutoff ship the following week. Outgoing orders ship on Wednesdays.
- Free US shipping is advertised on orders of $40 or more.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- HEX describes its goal as refining each roast to highlight the coffee's
  unique character and provenance, rather than imposing a house flavor.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses the coffee-only `cofefve` collection, then strips its
  collection segment because canonical product URLs are `/products/<handle>`.
  It also narrows product-page extraction to the coffee documentation block.
