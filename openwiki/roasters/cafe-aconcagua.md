# Café Aconcagua — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Café Aconcagua is a Chilean coffee roaster and Shopify shop. Its catalogue is
organised into traditional, prime, alternative, special and high-specialty
coffee collections, with coffees sold in named lots such as Gran Aconcagua,
Futaleufú and Torres del Paine. The site also offers project advice and a café
experience, but those locations are not treated as the roastery address here.

## Address

- Chile — full roastery address, including city, not published on the consulted
  site pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The shop banner says dispatches are made in under 24 business hours across the
  Santiago metropolitan region and that the business reaches all Chile. A
  separate WhatsApp-purchase message says dispatches are once a month and free
  to most of Santiago; the site does not publish a destination-by-destination
  rate table or a general free-shipping order minimum.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The storefront separates traditional, prime, alternative and high-specialty
  lines rather than presenting one undifferentiated coffee catalogue. It also
  advertises direct WhatsApp buying, including fresher coffee and barista
  recommendations.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper merges five Shopify collection JSON feeds. It excludes handles
  containing `pack-`, canonicalises collection links to direct `/products/`
  URLs, and translates Spanish product content to English. The pack filter is
  intentionally narrower than the full “pack degustación” collection, so
  verify future handle changes carefully.
