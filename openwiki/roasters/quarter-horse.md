# Quarter Horse — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Quarter Horse (quarterhorsecoffee.com) is a Birmingham-rooted coffee
roaster and café that roasts in the West Midlands (per their site). It runs
on a Shopify storefront selling a compact `beans` collection of single
origins and house blends (13 products saved), plus subscriptions, brewing
equipment and masterclasses.

## Address

- Per the site's contact page, the registered/roastery address is Unit D8,
  Salwarpe Business Park, Droitwich, Worcestershire WR9 9BN, United Kingdom.
  The brand is Birmingham-rooted, with the roastery in the West Midlands.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The shop advertises direct trade ("long-lasting & ethical relationships
  with producers at origin") and a push for more sustainable solutions (per
  their site).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Packing facility operates Monday–Friday (excluding bank holidays); orders
  placed before 13:00 are dispatched the same day.
- Free UK shipping over £20 via Royal Mail 48.
- Overseas shipping is not currently offered.

## Scraping Quirks

- Shopify `beans` collection.
- Coffee Samples Set is a tasting kit: flagged `is_tasting_kit` /
  `requires_review` so it lands in the admin review queue, never excluded.
- Two sampler/kit products (the Explore Pack and a mixed Roaster's
  Selection) recur as persistent Gemini enrichment failures; the scraper
  guards against out-of-stock updates when a listing fetch fails.
