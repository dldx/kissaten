# Apollon's Gold — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Apollon's Gold is a Japanese specialty coffee roaster based in Tokyo. Its
Shopify storefront at shop.apollons-gold.com presents a front-page collection
of coffees, including high-end single-origin and geisha offerings. The site
also carries non-coffee products and gift formats.

## Address

- Tokyo — Japan; full roastery address not published on the accessible site pages.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper reads the Shopify `frontpage` JSON collection without scraping
  individual product pages, and excludes subscriptions, gifts, equipment,
  brewing accessories, merchandise and the `9-stars` handle.
