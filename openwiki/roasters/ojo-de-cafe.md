# Ojo de Café — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Ojo de Café is a Swiss specialty roaster founded by Debora Knieriemen and
Cristian Conci, sourcing and roasting coffees from Latin America. Its Wix
storefront sells light-roast micro lots, espresso blends, decaf and capsules,
as well as equipment. The business also operates a Bern showroom with Nativa
Wine; that showroom address is not used as a roastery address below.

## Address

- Bern, Switzerland — full roastery address not published on site.

## Sustainability

- Ojo says producer coffee is grown ecologically and in mixed cultivation, its
  packaging is aluminium-free and recyclable, printed matter uses recycled
  paper/cardboard, and shipments are climate-neutral. It also accepts returned
  bags and handles recycling.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Ojo says it trades directly and pays farmers around three to six times what
  they would have received in the Fairtrade market. It publishes the price paid
  to farmers on its website and says it helps producers use improved
  post-harvest processes to achieve higher prices. No per-product payment
  figures were captured on the reviewed pages.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Ojo identifies a Stronghold S7 Pro for competition coffees and nano lots, and
  a Loring Kestrel S35 for blends and single origins at scale. It describes the
  Stronghold as a convection/conduction/infrared hybrid and the Loring as a
  clean heat-recirculation system that reduces emissions and energy use.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Ojo sends weekly climate-neutral Economy shipments or almost-daily Priority
  shipments. Subscription shipping costs CHF 3.40; Economy is free from CHF
  119, otherwise CHF 8.50, and Priority is CHF 10.50. Coffee is usually 0–4
  weeks old, occasionally up to six; customers can request fresher roast and
  wait for dispatch. International rates are shown at checkout rather than
  published by destination.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The founders’ stated aim is to let terroir and producer work show through,
  with clarity and balance rather than masking origin character. Their values
  page also explicitly rejects labels as the sole sustainability test for small
  producers.
