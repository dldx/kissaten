# Leaves Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Leaves Coffee Roasters is a Tokyo, Japan roastery led by representative roaster Yasuo Ishii. The site sells single-origin coffees, blends and a two-coffee subscription through its English storefront. Leaves says its aim is to maximise origin character through sweetness, aroma, balanced acidity and a lasting finish.

## Address

- 1-8-8 Honjo, Sumida-ku, Tokyo 130-0004, Japan.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Leaves says the roastery started in **January 2019** and currently runs two production machines: a **Probat UG-15** and a **Stronghold S7X**. No capacity is stated for either machine on the page checked.
- The roastery says it is open four days a week—Friday, Saturday, Sunday and Monday—and describes its roast style as maximising the ingredients’ potential for a clean drink with balanced acidity and sweetness.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The four-day opening/roastery schedule is Friday through Monday, 10:00–17:00; holiday hours can change. The accessible pages did not verify a roast-day promise, dispatch cadence, free-shipping threshold or destination-specific rates.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The name “LEAVES” is explained as a growth metaphor: fallen leaves become nutrients for new leaves. Ishii is described as a self-taught barista and roaster, and the site notes Leaves’ Sprudge Annual Award of Notable Roaster nominations and his second-place finish at the 2025 Japan Brewers Cup.
