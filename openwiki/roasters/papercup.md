# Papercup — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Papercup Coffee (www.papercupcoffee.co.uk) is a Glasgow-based coffee roastery
and café group on a Shopify storefront in GBP. It roasts on set days (Mondays
and Wednesdays), sells a modest bean range alongside equipment, merch and
gift cards, and operates cafés in Glasgow and Motherwell (per their site).

## Address

- Great Western Road café — 603 Great Western Rd, Glasgow G12 8HX, United
  Kingdom.
- Roastery — Arch 17, 100 Eastvale Pl, Glasgow G3 8QG, United Kingdom.
- Motherwell café — 77 Manse Rd, Motherwell ML1 2PW, United Kingdom.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Roast days are Mondays and Wednesdays.
- Order before 14:00 GMT on a roast day and the order is processed and
  dispatched that working day if in stock (weekends and bank holidays
  excluded).
- Free shipping on orders over £50.

## Scraping Quirks

- Shopify; 11 of 12 catalogue products saved.
- The `roasters-selection` collection is a persistent Gemini kit-extraction
  failure (known residual) — the item is flagged rather than silently
  dropped, awaiting admin review.
