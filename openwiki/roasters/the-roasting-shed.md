# The Roasting Shed — Roaster Profile

## Overview

The Roasting Shed is a small-batch roastery on Hornsey Road in London,
United Kingdom, run by founders with Colombian roots — co-founder Ricardo
grew up around his family's coffee plantations near Armenia, Colombia. The
business started in 2013 roasting in a garden shed in Homerton, Hackney,
later moved to Mick's Garage in Queens Yard, Hackney Wick, and opened its
current roastery and café on Hornsey Road around Christmas 2020 (per local
press). The storefront is Shopify, and the company was incorporated in
September 2014 (per Companies House).

## Address

- 322 Hornsey Road, London, N7 7HE — United Kingdom (roastery and café;
  customer service hours Monday–Friday 8am–4pm, Saturday 8am–1pm)

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The site emphasises a "fully transparent and traceable" supply chain,
  built on "close working relationships with responsibly-minded importers,
  growers and co-operatives" committed to looking after their workforce and
  sustaining the environment — no price-transparency figures are published.

## Roasting & Equipment

- All coffee is **roasted and packed to order** in small batches at the
  Hornsey Road roastery (per their Terms & Conditions).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders are processed within **1–2 business days**; collections happen
  every weekday. Orders received **after 1pm** are packed and collected the
  following day; orders after **1pm Thursday** arrive Monday the following
  week (per their Terms & Conditions).
- **Wholesale orders are despatched on Wednesdays** for nationwide delivery
  Thursday/Friday.
- No free-delivery minimum or per-region shipping rates are published.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The name is literal: the business began in the founders' **garden shed**
  in Homerton, and "our philosophy since the start has remained the same:
  uncompromising quality of product and exceptional customer satisfaction".
- Alongside retail, they run a full **wholesale program** — build-out
  consultancy, equipment selection, menu development and training — and
  sell commercial equipment and barista courses through the site.
- Local press captured the founders' motto early on: "life is too short to
  drink bad coffee" (per the Hackney Gazette).

## Scraping Quirks

- **In-stock only**: the scraper fetches the collection with Shopify's
  `?filter.v.availability=1` and additionally drops any product whose grid
card contains the text "Sold out" — so sold-out beans never enter the
catalogue, unlike scrapers that record out-of-stock diffjson updates.
