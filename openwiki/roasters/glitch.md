# Glitch Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Glitch Coffee & Roasters is a Japanese specialty coffee roaster with an English-language Shopify store. Its catalogue is organised into Competition, Hard to Find, Innovation, Traditional and COE Collection categories, and includes lots from farms and producers in countries such as Panama, Ethiopia, Colombia, Kenya, Myanmar and China. The site’s stated line is “To serve a cup for your valuable time”.

## Address

- Japan — full roastery address not published on the accessible online-store pages. The site lists Tokyo, Jimbocho, Ginza, Nagoya and Osaka stores separately; those café/store locations are not used as a roastery address.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The store provides international-shipping and shipping-policy pages and offers country selectors for a defined set of destinations, but rates and free-delivery thresholds were not accessible in the pages checked. No roast or dispatch cadence was found.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Glitch’s shop distinguishes experimental/innovation lots and competition coffees from its traditional range, rather than presenting one undifferentiated coffee list.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper checks two English `/en/collections/all` pages, supports several custom product-link selectors, and retains only `/en/products/` links. Its URL filter excludes merchandise and equipment terms including `collaboration`, `brewing`, `kettle`, `grinder` and `subscription`.
