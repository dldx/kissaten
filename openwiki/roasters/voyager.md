# Voyager Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Voyager Coffee (www.voyagercoffee.co.uk) is a Devon coffee roaster based in
Buckfastleigh, running a WordPress + WooCommerce storefront (Store API). It
sells its own coffee and tea alongside brew kits, gifts, domestic machines
and barista/wholesale training, with ethical-sourcing and sustainability
policies published. The catalogue holds 12 saved coffees.

## Address

- Unit 6, Mardle Way Business Park, Buckfastleigh, Devon, TQ11 0JL — United
  Kingdom.

## Scraping Quirks

- WooCommerce Store API-backed storefront (the public JSON endpoint drives
  the shared WooCommerce scraper).
