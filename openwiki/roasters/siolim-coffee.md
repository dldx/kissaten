# Siolim Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Siolim Coffee is an Indian specialty roaster whose Shopify storefront presents
single origins, microlots, blends, samplers and the barrel-aged Madeera line.
The site describes its coffee as small-batch and farm-direct, and says each
order is packed with a roast date. Its range includes Arabica, Robusta,
Excelsa and blends.

## Address

- India — full roastery address not published on site.

## Sustainability

- The site describes its cascara as a circular-economy product, using the
  coffee fruit by-product rather than discarding it.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Siolim says it works directly with farmers and presents its sourcing as
  fair-trade and sustainable. No price-paid-to-producer or per-kg cost
  breakdown is published on the reviewed pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The site says coffee is roasted to order, orders are normally processed and
  dispatched within 1–3 business days, and delivery time varies within India.
- Free shipping is advertised for orders above ₹1,500.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Madeera is a distinctive limited-batch line of coffee aged in oak barrels
  previously used for rum or single malt whisky.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses the Shopify `roasted-coffee` products.json endpoint and
  removes `/collections/.../` from product URLs before storage.
- Cascara is explicitly excluded from the catalogue by the scraper. Other
  non-coffee shop lines (equipment, Madeera and merchandise) are not part of
  that endpoint's intended collection.
