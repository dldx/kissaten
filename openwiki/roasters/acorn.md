# Acorn Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Acorn Coffee ("Acorns Coffee & Cake") is a small-batch roaster based in Bordon,
Hampshire, founded in **February 2021** by Tracey and Dayne. Its origin story is a
whirlwind one: it started in a **converted horsebox** — "serving coffee in all
weather... opened in the thick snow of February 2021" — then grew via a shipping
container to a permanent home called **The Shed, Bordon**. The business roasts
in-house, in Bordon, and ships UK-wide. It is a family business (son George
features in the story). Motto: "From Tiny Acorns. Mighty Oaks Grow."

## Address

- The Shed, Bordon, Hampshire — United Kingdom (roastery at their Bordon
  café/shop; started life as a converted horsebox, then a shipping container)

## Roasting & Equipment

- Small-batch roasting in-house in Bordon.
- The business is a **4× Great Taste Award winner**.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- "Coffee is theatre" — Dayne, one of the founders.
- The site frames single-origin coffee as storytelling: "single origin coffee
  tells a story."
