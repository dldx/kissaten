# Scenery Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Scenery Coffee Roasters is a specialty roaster in Southwark, London, founded
by a trio who "all worked in specialty roasteries before" and share roughly
three decades of industry experience between them. Their café, kitchen and
roastery share one site at the Jerwood Space (a performing-arts rehearsal
complex in a converted Victorian school building), with dine-in opening in
March 2025 per their Sprudge Build-Outs feature — making it one of the most
central working roasteries in London. The Shopify storefront at
[scenery.coffee](https://scenery.coffee) sells the Facility house blend, the
Colourful seasonal blend and rotating single origins, plus brewing gear, art
prints and apparel. Per their site they were Best Design honourees at the
16th Annual Sprudgie Awards.

## Address

- Scenery, 171 Union St, London, SE1 0LN — United Kingdom (café and roastery
  on the same site, attached to the Jerwood Space).

## Sustainability

- Builds "circular economy thinking" into the business and plans a
  straightforward yearly report so customers can hold them accountable.
- Recyclable shipping materials; coffee bags are mixed-material (waste
  stream 7) plastics recyclable with thin-film collections at large
  supermarkets — labels should be removed first. Bags are currently virgin
  plastic; they are working on more sustainable materials.
- Per their Sprudge interview: all furniture on site is upcycled, café
  coffee is served from reusable buckets and containers, last-mile London
  deliveries run in an electric van, and they are collecting jute sacks for
  recycling into insulation.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Commits to purchasing coffee **early, while still in origin** —
  pre-contracting at harvest and working about six months ahead — releasing
  coffees as they land in the UK, giving producers surety of sale.
- Import partners are named for each coffee in the product information.
- Plans to publish green coffee costs (Farmgate / FOB / Ex-Works) once they
  can do so in a way that is commercially respectful to each tier of the
  value chain.
- Particular interest in coffees offering higher value addition for
  producers, high-impact buying, and progressive processing.

## Roasting & Equipment

- **Loring S35 Kestrel** convection roaster, with an optical sorter recently
  installed at the roastery (per Sprudge).
- Roast categories describe the amount of roaster "influence" you can taste
  rather than colour: **Lightest** (lighter than Nordic, not quite
  ultralight), **Light**, **Medium** (their espresso roast) and **Heavy**.
- "We don't believe in roasting for sweetness" — they buy sweet coffees and
  let them speak for themselves.
- Production roasting on set days (typically Tuesday–Thursday) in optimised
  batches, with some stock held in airtight bulk storage so bags are filled
  as needed; bags carry a roast date from dispatch day up to three weeks
  prior.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Ships within 24 hours of order Monday–Friday (orders after 1pm Friday may
  ship the following Monday).
- Free UK shipping on coffee orders over **£30**; UK rates are Royal Mail
  Tracked 48 (200g max) £1.95, Tracked 48 £2.95, Tracked 24 £3.95.
- Ships worldwide via Royal Mail international services, with rates
  calculated at checkout; DHL Express also offered domestically and
  internationally.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Anti-gatekeeping: "our philosophy as Scenery is not to teach, but to
  share" — engage as much or as little as you like.
- Deliberately ungrandiose: "We won't assert that we are the best, the most
  sustainable, ethical, unique roasters around" — wins and failures are
  presented openly.
- Each coffee gets an illustration from artist **Lucas Garcia** depicting
  the farm's landscape, geography and flavour "vibe" — the designs earned
  them a Best Design honouree at the 16th Annual Sprudgie Awards.
- Whole-bean only (no grind options), with "best rested for" guides on every
  product page; coffees are best within 3 months of roast.
