# Atkinson's Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

J. Atkinson & Co. — trading as Atkinsons Coffee Roasters & Tea Merchants —
was established in Lancaster in 1837 as **The Grasshopper Tea Warehouse**,
making it one of the oldest speciality coffee roasters and tea merchants in
the UK. The shop has stood on China Street since 1901, complete with a
terrazzo floor predating the building's days as the Lord Nelson Inn. The
family business was taken on by Ian Steel (self-styled "Guardian/Keeper of
the Flame") in the mid-2000s and now counts 42 staff across four Lancaster
locations (the original shop, The Music Room, The Hall and Lancaster Castle).
Certified B-Corp. The shop site is at
[atkinsonscoffee.com](https://www.atkinsonscoffee.com) with the online shop at
[thecoffeehopper.com](https://www.thecoffeehopper.com) (WordPress/WooCommerce).

## Address

- J. Atkinson & Co., 12 China Street, Lancaster, Lancashire, LA1 1EX,
  United Kingdom (original 1901 shop; the roastery sits between the shop and
  The Hall café)

## Sustainability

- **B-Corp certified**, committing to the triple bottom line of people,
  planet and profit.
- Publishes annual **Impact Reports** (24, 25) covering carbon-footprint
  reporting, staff welfare (Employee Assistance Program) and bag recycling —
  which hit a record for used coffee bags recycled.
- Roastery, shop and cafés run on renewable energy; coffee and tea are packed
  in LDPE recyclable plastic with a recycling-point finder for customers.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Relationship coffee: long-term producer relationships, forward contracts at
  premium prices, buying from the same growers year after year, and farm
  visits to verify social and environmental practices (per their about and
  sustainability pages).
- Signed up to **World Coffee Research**; the Impact Report breaks down
  purchases across a range of importers.

## Roasting & Equipment

- All coffee is roasted on a **Loring Smartroast** — low-emission and, per the
  company, roughly 80% more energy efficient than conventional roasters.
- Each coffee is roasted to its own repeatable profile for consistency, then
  bagged into recyclable valve bags.

## Schedules & Shipping

- **Free shipping on UK orders over £50** (site-wide banner).
- Coffee subscriptions (250g/500g/1kg, weekly/fortnightly/monthly) start from
  £11.50 including shipping and go out via Royal Mail 48-hour tracked.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- "**180 years old and still young at heart**" — the brand leans into its tea
  & coffee heritage while refusing "to get stuck in a heritage rut".
- Custodianship language: the owners "took up the baton as **Guardians of the
  Flame**" in 2005 after 168 years of continuous trading by previous owners.
- Named after the original 1837 Grasshopper Tea Warehouse — a heritage echoed
  in the shop's "Coffee Hopper" online store domain.

## Scraping Quirks

- **Two domains, one company**: the registry website is the online shop
  (`thecoffeehopper.com`), but the product catalogue the scraper fetches lives
  on the brand site at `atkinsonscoffee.com/all-coffee/`.
- The WordPress/WooCommerce site sits behind Cloudflare and requires JS
  rendering (Playwright) for the product list.
- Subscription, gift-voucher, gift-card, gift-set and `espresso-duo` products
  are filtered out by URL substring.
