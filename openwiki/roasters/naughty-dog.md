# The Naughty Dog — Roaster Profile

## Overview

The Naughty Dog is a Czech roastery opened in October 2018 by Petra and Gwilym
in the small town of Jílové u Prahy. The founders came from cafés and barista
competition: Petra has won the Czech Barista Championship and Gwilym won the
World Barista Championship. The site presents coffees by filter, espresso, omni
and special-edition use, while home orders are directed to Kofio or email rather
than a first-party cart.

## Address

- Jílové u Prahy, Czech Republic — full roastery address not published on site.

## Roasting & Equipment

- The site says the roastery uses an Italian IMF roaster combining hot air and
  drum technology, allowing the team to monitor and repeat profiles. It does
  not publish the IMF model or capacity.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Coffee is roasted on Mondays and Wednesdays and parcels are sent on Tuesdays
  and Thursdays. Prague cafés are delivered personally. No public free-shipping
  minimum or destination-specific rate was found on the reviewed page.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The name comes from the founders’ two sausage dogs, Jenny and Maya; the dogs
  also appear in the playful packaging and product naming. The roastery opens to
  visitors for coffee on Fridays from 10:00 to 13:00.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper is deliberately not an AI scraper: one landing page contains

  fields such as origin, process, variety and packaging in one fetch. Normal
