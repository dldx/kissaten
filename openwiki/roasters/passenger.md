# Passenger — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Passenger is a specialty coffee and tea roaster based in Lancaster, Pennsylvania,
with a flagship cafe and a roastery and coffee bar. Its Shopify storefront separates
Foundational Coffees, Reserve Lots and Education Lots, and also sells tea, equipment
and goods. The company says its green-buying programme concentrates most annual
purchases in seven long-term, farmer-focused partnerships across East Africa,
Central America, South America and South Asia.

## Address

- 131 N Plum St, Lancaster, PA 17602, United States.

## Sustainability

- Passenger keeps 100% of its green coffee, including microlots, Foundational
  offerings and blend components, in frozen storage. It says this preserves quality
  and lets the company buy more coffee from its core partners across a harvest year.
- Passenger says it has been a donor member of World Coffee Research since 2024.
  Its Heza partners at Long Miles Coffee Project are developing an organic compost
  programme for soil and plant health.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Passenger publishes annual reports with the price paid for every pound of green
  coffee contracted in the most recent harvest year, alongside lot and cup-quality
  data. It also joined the Specialty Coffee Transaction Guide as a data donor in
  2021. Passenger says it pays considerably more than the Fair Trade minimum and
  prefers fixed-price contracts over certification-based premiums tied to the C
  Market.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Passenger roasts Monday-Friday and ships orders the following day. Orders before
  06:00 ET on weekdays are roasted that day; orders after the cutoff or at weekends
  are processed on the next roasting day. Delivery typically takes 2-4 days after
  shipping. Each subscription includes complimentary shipping within the continental
  United States; regular international shipping is generally not offered, though
  exceptions can be discussed by email.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Passenger's light-roast approach is intended to show intrinsic sweetness and
  flavour complexity without roast-artifact flavours. Its preservation programme
  supports a stable, year-round Foundational menu.
