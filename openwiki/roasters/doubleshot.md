# Doubleshot — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Doubleshot is a Czech specialty coffee roaster founded in 2010. Its
`doubleshot.cz` storefront groups coffee by three flavour profiles and also
offers gear, subscriptions and courses; the business operates cafés in Prague
separately from the roastery. The site describes direct producer relationships
and a coffee-school/community programme.

## Address

- Roastery: doubleshot s.r.o., Dehtáry 37, 250 91 Zeleneč v Čechách — Czech Republic.

## Sustainability

- Doubleshot says it works with farmers who regenerate soil, save water and care
  for their communities. It presents this as part of its responsibility
  approach, alongside fair prices and long-term supply security.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Doubleshot says over 90% of its beans are sourced directly from producers it
  knows personally and visits regularly. It says it is often involved in harvest
  and processing and that each coffee has a clear origin. No numerical
  price-paid-to-producer or cost-breakdown figures were published on the
  reviewed pages.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Green coffee travels to the Prague roastery, where it is roasted on an IMF
  60 kg machine. Doubleshot says it measures, tastes and fine-tunes each batch,
  and freezes green coffee on arrival to preserve it through the year.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The shop says two or more bags of coffee qualify for free shipping within the
  Czech Republic. The terms list preparation and dispatch on business days,
  preparation of in-stock goods within three business days, Czech delivery in
  1–3 working days, Slovakia in 2–4 business days and listed EU destinations
  in 3–14 business days. Shipping prices are shown at checkout; the published
  free-shipping threshold is CZK 2,000 excluding VAT for Czech orders.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Coffee is organised into “Sweet and Creamy”, “Fruity and Elegant” and “Juicy
  and Exotic” profiles. The shop also sells a configurable tasting set of
  three 70 g coffees for 290 CZK including VAT.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper crawls two English coffee-taxonomy pages and filters URL tokens for
  steeped coffee, café gift vouchers, capsules and the holiday double rather
  than using a Shopify endpoint. It excludes sold-out cards before AI
  extraction and resolves relative links against the shop root.
