# Rish Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Rish Coffee Roasters is a small speciality roastery based in West London,
founded by Sharon Ip — an award-winning competitor, Great Taste Awards judge
and certified SCA Q-Grader who moved to the UK around five years ago (per their
site) — together with a co-founder. The team is competition-decorated: **Coffee
Masters 2026 Champion** and Coffee Masters 2025 runner-up (per their site).
It is a Shopify storefront at [rishcoffee.com](https://rishcoffee.com) selling
100g and 250g bags.

## Address

- West London, United Kingdom — production roastery only, not open to the
  public; full address not published on the site

## Schedules & Shipping

- Coffees are **roasted to order**; online orders are dispatched on
  **Tuesdays and Fridays**: orders placed after 10am Tuesday ship Friday;
  orders after 10am Friday ship the following Tuesday.
- All orders go via Royal Mail 48-hour tracked, typically 2–3 working days
  (no later than 7); urgent orders can be sent same/next day with postage
  covered by the customer.
- **Free UK delivery over £35**, otherwise £4 (up to 2 kg).
- UK-only shipping for now; wholesale enquiries via hello@rishcoffee.com.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The name **rish** is a blend of the two founders' names — with a playful
  twist: in Cantonese their accent can turn "r" into "w", so it sounds like
  **"wish"**. The site's contact/feedback page is accordingly titled "make a
  wish".
- **Every sample is blind cupped** — no bias about origin, varietal or score;
  "flavour speaks first".
- The range hunts for **rare or lesser-known farms, varietals and origins**
  and **rotates every couple of months** — "each coffee a small memorable
  adventure".
- Two certified Q-Graders in house; small 100g/250g bag sizes so limited lots
  can be tried at peak freshness without committing to a large bag.
- Awards (per their site): Coffee Masters 2026 Champion; Coffee Masters 2025
  runner-up; Hario Brewer's Cup.
