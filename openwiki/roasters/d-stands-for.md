# D Stands For — Roaster Profile

## Overview

D Stands For is a Vienna-based specialty roaster focused exclusively on decaf
coffee, according to the registered declaration. Its registered storefront is
the Shopify shop at decaf.at. A roastery street address and additional
operational details were not confirmed in the reviewed official pages.
