# Moklair — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Moklair is a French micro-roastery and coffee bar in Reims. The WooCommerce
storefront sells light-roasted coffees in French and English, from blends through
experimental and competition lots. Per its site, the business has won seven
trophies in three years, including French Barista and Brewers champion titles.
Its cafe is at 1 rue Andrieux; the legal notice gives the company's registered
office as 5 rue Andrieux, but neither page identifies that address as the roasting
site.

## Address

- Reims, France — full roastery address not published on site.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The site advertises free delivery to a pickup point in France from EUR 55. For
  destinations outside metropolitan France, it directs customers to contact
  livraison@moklair.com for the applicable information; no dispatch cadence or
  delivery time was found on the reviewed pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Competition is explicitly part of Moklair's identity: its site lists French
  Barista, Brewers Cup and Coffee in Good Spirits results from 2022-2025.

## Scraping Quirks

- Registry key is `moklair`; the scraper uses the English WooCommerce boutique
  pages `/en/boutique/` and `/en/boutique/page/2/`, skips `outofstock` cards,
  narrows product pages to `div[id^="product-"]`, and forces prices to EUR.
