# Bean & Bean Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Bean & Bean Coffee Roasters is a family-owned New York roaster founded by a mother-and-daughter team; the shop says the team has roasted in Queens since 2008 and that both founders are Q-Graders. Its Shopify catalogue includes blends, seasonal single origins, decaf, Cup of Excellence coffees, subscriptions and tea. The company describes its sourcing as traceable to farm and lot.

## Address

- 252-29 Northern Blvd, 1 Fl., Little Neck, NY 11362, United States — the site's locations page identifies this as the Queens Roastery & Cafe.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Bean & Bean says it sources microlots and nanolots from top producers and highlights Best of Panama and Cup of Excellence farms. Its shop marks selected coffees as USDA Organic and Fair Trade certified and describes sourcing down to farm and lot. The consulted pages do not publish a per-kg FOB or farm-gate price breakdown.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The shop advertises free USPS standard shipping on orders of USD 50 or more. Its subscription feature says coffee is roasted to ship and ships within 48 hours of roast.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The company foregrounds gender equity in coffee and its mother-and-daughter ownership. Its catalogue mixes everyday blends with rare competition coffees and coffee subscriptions selected by its Q-Graders.
