# Saint Espresso — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Saint Espresso (saintespresso.com) is a London coffee roaster that describes
itself as a "collection of neighbourhood spaces" serving coffee and food,
alongside a wholesale business and at-home coffee range (whole-bean, pods and
brew kits) on a Shopify storefront in GBP. Its roastery HQ is based in Hackney
Wick ("Coffee expertly roasted in Hackney, London"); the cafés span Angel,
Baker Street, Blackfriars, Hackney, New Street, St Pauls and Tower Hill.

## Address

- Roastery HQ at Hackney, Hackney Wick, London, United Kingdom — full street
  address not published on site (the café locations are named in the Overview).

## Sourcing & Transparency
