# Hardlines — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Hardlines began as a tiny pop-up in Cardiff, Wales in 2016, started by Matt
and Sophie, and has grown into a Welsh roastery-plus-cafés business: the
Canton café, an espresso hatch in Cardiff Central Market, and the roastery
shop on the outskirts of the city. The Shopify storefront at
[hard-lines.co.uk](https://hard-lines.co.uk) sells seasonal single origins,
"playful limited releases" and everyday favourites alongside subscriptions,
pods, equipment and barista courses. Their coffee has been featured in The
Guardian, GQ and the Financial Times (per their site).

## Address

- Roastery: Gwaelod y Garth, Cardiff, CF15 8LA — United Kingdom ("on the
  outskirts of Cardiff surrounded by mountains and a fairy tale castle";
  street-level address not published beyond the village, retail hours Mon–Thu)

## Sustainability

- Member of **1% for the Planet** — 1% of annual revenue is donated to
  environmental and community causes, with a focus on Welsh charities
  protecting local ecosystems and improving access to nature.
- The **Ground Level Initiative** supports grassroots projects and
  organisations across Wales, "from creative programmes to social action
  groups".

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Works "closely with producers around the world, choosing coffees with real
  character"; the site banner advertises **Direct Trade** and seasonal
  coffee. No farm-gate or FOB price figures are published.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Roasting started in the Canton café before moving to the dedicated
  Gwaelod y Garth roastery, where the team roasts on "our eco-friendly
  **Loring S35 Kestrel and S15 Falcon**" (fluid-bed roasters).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Free UK shipping on orders of £30 or more; UK Mainland flat rate otherwise
  £3.50.
- Orders usually dispatch quickly; during busy periods dispatch can take up
  to 3 working days, with delivery typically 1–3 working days via Royal Mail.
- Next-day service: the tracked 24hr option at checkout dispatches same day
  (Mon–Fri) for orders received by 1pm.
- International shipping via DHL Air Express (tracked, signed-for) to Europe,
  North America, Australia and Japan, 1–3 working days per checkout estimate.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Mantra repeated across the site in giant marquees: "**REALLY REALLY REALLY
  GOOD COFFEE!**" — the tone is, per their about page, "optimistic, a bit
  cheeky, and always focused on doing things properly".
- Community runs deep: a run club (with its own merch), the Sage Coffee
  School partnership for home-brewing tips, and in-house espresso & milk
  essentials barista courses.
- Customer review headline of choice: "Hard Lines is f\*\*\*ing awesome."

## Scraping Quirks

- **Heavily mixed catalogue**: the Shopify `all` collection is dominated by
  non-coffee products — equipment (AeroPress, Hario, Fellow Opus, Timemore,
  Orea), apparel (t-shirts, caps, mugs), courses, vouchers, a book, and even
  coffee liqueur. The scraper carries a long `exclude_slugs` list of handle
  substrings to keep only the actual bean products (e.g. `the-canton-blend`,
  `honduras-*`, `nicaragua-*`, `i-heart-decaf`).
