# WatchHouse — Roaster Profile

## Overview

WatchHouse was founded in **2014 by Roland Horne** in a former 19th-century
watch house on Bermondsey Street, London — a tiny building that once sheltered
the men guarding St Mary Magdalen's churchyard from grave robbers, and gave the
brand its name. The ambition is branded **"Modern Coffee"**: quality "for the
connoisseur and the casually curious in equal measure". From one site it has
grown into a House network across London, Bath, New York and Dubai, with the
roastery, training centre and HQ opening in a Maltby Street railway arch in
2020. The storefront is Shopify; coffees are organised into the **Rituals /
Ventures / Horizons** ranges plus **1829 Espresso** and **Páramo Decaf**.

## Address

- WatchHouse Roastery, 36 Maltby Street, London, SE1 3PA — United Kingdom
  (roastery, training centre and HQ in a repurposed railway arch; further
  Houses across London, Bath, New York and Dubai are listed on their site)

## Sustainability

- Compostable, plant-based takeaway cups and lids from Decent Packaging —
  "ensure we can unmake everything we make" (per their Spotlight blog).
- **Milkmate** precision milk dispensers to cut steamed-milk and plastic-bottle
  waste in the Houses.
- Leftover food routed to **Too Good To Go** and **OLIO** to keep food waste to
  a minimum.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Buys from importers who share its trading ideology and picks coffees for
  their "wider-reaching impact" on producers — hence unique origins and a
  greater frequency of regional lots (per their FAQ). No price-transparency
  figures are published.
- Long-term relationship with Ethiopian producer **Bette Buna**, featured in
  the first episode of their **Point of Origin** documentary series tracing the
  people and places behind the coffees.
- Roastery team led by Head Roaster **Nikol Novotná**.

## Roasting & Equipment

- A refurbished **1959 Probat UG22** drum roaster — bought from Bonanza Coffee
  Roasters, Berlin — visible to café customers through a feature glass wall.
- Every coffee is developed through constant tasting, profiling and cupping at
  the London (and New York) roasteries; the roastery doubles as a training
  centre with home-brewing courses.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders processed Monday–Friday at 3pm; Friday-afternoon-to-Sunday orders ship
  the following Monday.
- **Free UK shipping over £40**; otherwise Royal Mail 1st Class (24h) £4.80 or
  2nd Class (48h, up to 1kg) £3.70.
- International flat rates: **£15 Europe, £40 Switzerland & US, £70 rest of
  world**. Delivery 2–3 working days UK, 7–10 days Europe, 10–14 days
  international.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- **"Modern Coffee"** — quality "that took quality seriously, but left the ego
  elsewhere"; the first House's role as a graveyard-guard shelter is embraced as
  the brand's founding story ("No guard duty required").
- Filter coffees rotate seasonally under the stable Rituals / Ventures /
  Horizons ranges, with Horizons reserved for the most experimental lots.
- Press pick-up on the home page: Dezeen, Financial Times, Vogue, Wallpaper*,
  The Times.

## Scraping Quirks

- **Seasonal rotation under stable handles**: products like `rituals-filter`
  are reused for a different bean every few months, so the scraper suffixes
  each URL with a `#<title-slug>` fragment (e.g. `#nguvu-washed`). When a
  coffee rotates, the fragment changes, producing a fresh URL and letting the
  old bean drop to out-of-stock — preserving history instead of overwriting.
  (The per-image disambiguation used for some other roasters is deliberately
  not used — WatchHouse's multiple images are marketing shots of one bean.)
- Non-coffee products are excluded by slug: `subscription`, `bundle`, `1kg`,
  `nitro-cold-brew`, `roasters-spotlight`.
