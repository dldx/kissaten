# Santu Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Santu Coffee (santucoffee.com) was founded in 2018 by a Brazilian and a Brit
(per their site), importing specialty coffees from Brazil bought directly from
the farmers they lived and worked with. It roasts at a New Town roastery in
Edinburgh and sells direct to consumers and wholesale from a WooCommerce
storefront.

## Address

- Santu Roastery, 23 Union Street, Edinburgh EH1 3LR, United Kingdom
  (published on the contact page; there is also a Santu Coffee Bar at
  126 Canongate on the Royal Mile).

## Sourcing & Transparency

- All coffees come from Espírito Santo, Brazil — a mountainous state whose
  varied altitudes and climates allow its farming partners to produce a wide
  range of award-winning specialty coffees. Farmers include a Cup of
  Excellence finalist and Best of Brazil winner (per their site).

## Scraping Quirks

- WooCommerce (not Shopify) — scraped via the public WooCommerce Store API
  (`/wp-json/wc/store/v1/products`).
- 4 products saved (Brazilian micro-lots).
