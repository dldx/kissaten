# Monogram Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Monogram Coffee is a Calgary, Alberta specialty roaster founded by Ben, Justin and
Jeremy after they met as baristas. Its Shopify storefront sells coffees organised
around flavour moods, alongside subscriptions, workshops and wholesale; the company
also operates Calgary cafes. Monogram says it has earned 10 Canadian Barista
Championship titles and roasts for its "Sweet Spot": expressive flavour, smooth
balance and everyday drinkability.

## Address

- Calgary, Alberta, Canada — full roastery address not published on the reviewed site pages.

## Sustainability

- Monogram's Sustainability Project partnered with Daterra's Tree_llion project.
  Monogram says a portion of proceeds from every bag of Daterra coffee, including
  Daterra Pearl, funds tree planting in Brazil's Cerrado and reforestation efforts.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Monogram describes long-term relationships with producers including Finca Takesi,
  Finca Deborah, Elida Estate and Daterra. It does not publish lot-level FOB or
  farm-gate prices on the reviewed pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Monogram says it roasts every day in Calgary and ships directly from its roastery.
  Its shipping policy asks customers to allow 1-2 business days for processing and
  another 1-2 business days for fulfillment. Free shipping is stated as over CAD 50
  across Canada and over USD 70 in the United States; carrier charges to the USA
  may include duties, tariffs or taxes payable by the customer.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The founders started Monogram to make specialty coffee less elitist and easier
  for people to discover. Flavour-mood categories such as Chocolatey Comforts,
  Fruity Classics and Super Funky Adventures are the storefront's main navigation.
