# Taith Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Taith is an owner-operated coffee roastery, ceramic studio and bookshop on
the high street in Lewes, East Sussex, United Kingdom — one of the more
unusual combinations in the catalogue. The storefront is Squarespace, and
the tagline is **"Community not Commodity"**: "We find value in community
not commodity." The range leans rare and process-driven coffees (geishas
from Panama, Colombia, Peru, Tanzania and Guatemala; El Paraiso lots; Best
of Yemen; Gems of Araku India), plus a "Taith Reserve" 100 g subscription,
books, pottery and brewing equipment. The coffee bar is open Friday–Sunday,
9am–4pm.

## Address

- 84 High Street, Lewes, East Sussex, BN7 1XN — United Kingdom (the
  roastery, coffee bar, bookshop and ceramic studio share the site)

## Sustainability

- Wholesale orders support **charitable giving — 10% of all orders is
  donated to charity** (per their wholesale page).
- Shipping is by **Royal Mail or DPD (Carbon Neutral)**, all orders tracked.
- Packaging paper stocks are selected from **ethical suppliers**.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Roasts on a **Loring S15**, testing the **moisture and water activity of
  every batch of green coffee** and adjusting as needed (per their FAQ).
- Roasts **extremely light** — "present acidity, high sweetness and an
  overall aim of high clarity" — then runs every batch through a **colour
  sorter** to remove Quakers and defects.
- Every batch goes through an extended QC process: colour sorting, colour
  scaling, roast analysis, cupping, grading and brewing evaluation; coffees
  that don't meet the standard are pulled from release.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Roasted **to order in small batches**, with two published roast cycles:
  pick lists are generated at **9am Sunday** and **9am Wednesday**, and
  orders placed before each cut-off join that evening's roast (per their
  dispatch-schedule page). The FAQ describes it as roasting 2–3 days a week,
  QC the following day, then packing and shipping — aiming to have all
  orders shipped within **4–5 days**.
- Free shipping on all orders above **£30** (Royal Mail or DPD, tracked).
- Packaging (labels and materials) is designed and printed **in-house
  through their riso print studio**.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- "Thank you for supporting slower, more intentional roasting" — the whole
  operation is pitched against speed and volume, from roasting through to
  hand-printed packaging.
- One site, three crafts: the roastery shares a home with a **ceramic
  studio** (pottery sold in the shop) and a **bookshop** (fiction, poetry,
  zines and art titles sold alongside the coffee).
- Stocks **The Fermentation Project by Lucia Solis & James Hoffmann** as a
  pre-sale, alongside coffees selected on "what their purchase supports -
  not simply what is easiest to sell".
