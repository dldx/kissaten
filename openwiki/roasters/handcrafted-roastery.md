# Handcrafted Roastery — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Handcrafted Roastery is a Bhopal, India micro-roastery created in 2019 alongside Handcrafted cafés. The Wix storefront sells Indian estate coffees, blends and experimental lots, including anaerobic and frozen-cherry offerings. The company says it travels to estates, procures specialty beans and uses roast profiling and cupping to give each lot an individual profile.

## Address

- E3/75, Arera Colony, near Narmada Hospital, Madhya Pradesh 462016, India.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Handcrafted says it works closely with coffee plantations and procures from fine estates across India. Its site and linked posts name estates including Ratnagiri, Baarbara, Hoysala, Salawara and Gemblary; no producer-price or cost-breakdown figures were found.

## Roasting & Equipment

- The company describes a micro-roastery with strict roast profiling, roast-level assessment and cupping for each lot. No machine brand, model, capacity or roasting software is published on the pages checked.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Handcrafted says it began from a need for an alternative to burnt commercial coffee and frames coffee as a practice and an art. The store’s range includes unusually specific process-led names such as Gemblary Anaerobic Naturals and Baarbara Frozen Cherry.
