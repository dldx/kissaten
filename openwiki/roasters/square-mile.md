# Square Mile Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Square Mile Coffee Roasters is a London specialty roaster founded in **2008** by
Anette Moldvaer and James Hoffmann and now directed by Felicity Whitehead. Its
Shopify shop offers seasonal espresso, filter and decaf coffees, subscriptions,
equipment and education; the roastery also hosts tours and tastings. The company
describes its sourcing as ethical and sustainable, grounded in long-standing
relationships.

## Address

- Unit 13, Uplands Business Park, Blackhorse Lane, London E17 5QJ, United Kingdom.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders are usually processed at **8:30am**, roasted and shipped the same day.
  The site says this can vary during busy periods and bank holidays.
- UK Royal Mail Tracked 48 is free. Royal Mail Tracked 24 is **£4.35 GBP**;
  DPD next-working-day mainland UK is **£5.88 GBP**; DPD two-day Northern
  Ireland is **£12.00 GBP**. Expected UK delivery with Tracked 48 is **2-3
  days, Monday-Saturday**.
- International Royal Mail delivery is stated as **3 working days to 3 weeks**;
  DHL Express is typically **3-6 working days**. Royal Mail international
  parcels have a **2kg** weight limit. Germany is sent by a duties-paid service;
  the site gives no general international free-shipping threshold.

## Scraping Quirks

- Registry key: `square-mile`. Shopify coffee handles are reused for seasonal
  rotations. The scraper adds the first product image's Shopify version as a
  URL fragment so a new lot is treated as a new bean, while excluding the Summer
  Coffee Pack and Filter Tasting Pack.
