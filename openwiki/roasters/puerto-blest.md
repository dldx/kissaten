# Puerto Blest Tostadores — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Puerto Blest Tostadores is an Argentine specialty coffee roaster based in Buenos Aires. Its Spanish-language site says the business began in 2001 and describes it as the first specialty coffee roaster in Argentina. The shop sells coffees organised by origin, method, category and flavour profile, plus education, subscriptions and business services.

## Address

- Buenos Aires, Argentina — full roastery address not published on site. The published Castillo 894, Villa Crespo address is identified as the showroom café and is therefore not listed here.

## Sustainability

- Puerto Blest says it works with farms using sustainable water-management practices. A separate “Un Café con Impacto” campaign with the Un Árbol civil association reported 105 native trees planted in the AMBA after a limited house-blend collaboration, exceeding its initial 100-tree goal.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The roaster says it visits farms, selects lots in person and builds direct, long-term relationships with coffee growers, whom it describes as strategic partners. Product pages publish origin, region, variety, process, altitude and farmer/farm information for selected coffees; no prices paid to producers or FOB cost breakdown was published.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The storefront showed free delivery above ARS 61,000. Delivery options are calculated by Argentine postal code; no destination-specific international rates were published in the fetched pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The company frames each roasted coffee as a journey from distant lands to the cup and pairs roasting with education, sensory training and community-building.
