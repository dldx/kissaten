# Aery Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Aery Coffee is a specialty roaster in Busan, Republic of Korea. Its Cafe24
storefront is offered in Korean and an English/USD storefront, with categories
including Shop, Bold, Daily and MD. The current range includes Panama geishas,
Kenya and other origin lots; the brand says it selects coffees for “rarity and
clarity.”

## Address

- 3–4F, 47 Jungang-daero, Jung-gu, Busan 48957 — Republic of Korea (the site's listed roastery; the same footer gives the Korean address as Jungang-dong 2-ga 7).

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Aery says it chooses coffees whose producer intention, variety, environment
  and processing create a distinctive identity, and aims to reveal that
  identity clearly rather than create new flavours. No price-paid-to-producer
  or FOB figures were published on the pages checked.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The storefront lists customer-support hours as Monday–Friday, 10:00–18:00,
  with weekends and holidays off. It presents a world-shipping country selector,
  but no destination rates or free-shipping threshold were exposed in the
  accessible page.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper targets the Cafe24 category URL `/category/shop/67/`, excludes
  `brew-bag` and `ufo-dripper` handles, and skips cards marked sold out.
- Although the registry declares KRW, post-processing forces extracted bean
  currency to USD for the English storefront.
