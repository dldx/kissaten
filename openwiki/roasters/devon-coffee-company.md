# The Devon Coffee Company — Roaster Profile

## Overview

The Devon Coffee Company was born in **2011** as a co-operative of café owners
looking to improve their coffee quality, and has grown into a specialty roaster
supplying and supporting **over 100 businesses across the South West** of
England. After five years squashed into an old galley kitchen under Plymouth
Guildhall, they moved to their current Faraday Mill premises, built out into a
Roastery, Training room, Warehouse, Cupping room and Trade Counter. The
storefront is Shopify, and everything sold is **100% certified organic** —
signature products include the Seasonal Espresso range (Super Eight, Gumdrop)
and single origins such as Chelbesa (Ethiopia) and Sacaclí (Nicaragua). They
also provide barista training, technical support and a bespoke white-label
roasting service to the trade.

## Address

- Unit 195 Faraday Mill, Oakfield Terrace Road, Plymouth, Devon, PL4 0ST —
  United Kingdom (Roastery, Warehouse, Cupping room and Trade Counter)

## Sustainability

- **1% for the Planet** member: donates at least 1% of gross sales to vetted
  non-profits — currently Devon Wildlife Trust, Food 4 Farmers (food security
  for coffee-farming communities in Latin America) and Devon Environment
  Foundation.
- **Certified organic trader since 2022**; now only sources from certified
  organic farms and co-ops.
- Transitioning to a **fully electric fleet** — local deliveries already run on
  a Maxus 100% electric van; evaluating a Typhoon 20kg electric roaster to
  eliminate roasting emissions.
- All electricity is 100% renewable via **Octopus Energy**; plan to disconnect
  gas once the electric roaster is in place.
- Packaging complies with the EU industrial-compostable standard **EN 13432**
  (coffee bags, labels and pyramid tea bags).

## Roasting & Equipment

- 15kg **Besca classic double-drum roaster**, installed 2017, superseding their
  original 1kg machine; instrumental in creating the Seasonal Espresso range.
- Data-logging software profiles every roast, followed by "endless cupping
  sessions" for batch-to-batch consistency; the profile database lets them
  re-roast a coffee in a different style or degree on request.
- Roastery visits and tasting sessions can be booked.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Coffee is **roasted weekly based on the previous week's demand**; stocked
  products ordered by 12pm Monday–Friday ship the same day.
- Coffee should arrive on a 2-working-day service, but they ask customers to
  allow up to 5 working days to roast, pack and deliver.
- UK rates (inc. VAT): 225g bags/subscriptions via untracked Royal Mail
  letterbox **£2.25**; 1–2 day tracked up to 1.9kg **£6.23**; 3–5 day tracked
  up to 2kg **£5.23**; express next-working-day via UKMail/DHL **£9.60**;
  **free delivery over £100** (tracked, usually next working day).
- Free collection from the Faraday Mill address.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Origin story is unusual: a café-owners' co-operative that became a roaster,
  rather than the other way round.
- Customer-tailored ethos — "helping both coffee connoisseurs and filter
  freshers plunge into the world of specialty coffee".
- The trade side is substantial: wholesale supply, barista training, machine
  servicing (they distribute Ascaso Barista-T energy-efficient espresso
  machines) and white-label roasting alongside the retail range.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper targets the curated `/collections/home-coffee` products.json
  (10 coffee products); `/collections/all` mixes in tea, hot chocolate,
  syrups, equipment and trade-only collections, so the default Shopify `all`
  endpoint would pull in a lot of non-coffee stock.
