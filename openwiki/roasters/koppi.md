# Koppi — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Koppi is a specialty coffee roastery in Helsingborg, Sweden. Its Shopify shop is organised around filter coffee, espresso and subscriptions, and the site presents the business as Koppi Roasters. The public storefront describes the shop and its coffees but does not expose a detailed equipment or sourcing profile on the pages accessible during research.

## Address

- Helsingborg, Sweden — full roastery address not published on the accessible pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The storefront states **free shipping when buying two bags or more within Sweden**.
- It also states **free express shipping within the EU when ordering four bags or more**. No paid destination-specific rates or dispatch schedule were verified.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses the `frontpage` collection and a broad set of Shopify/product-card selectors, then removes URL matches for subscriptions, gift packs, merchandise, wholesale and brewing equipment. Equipment-like URLs are retained only when the URL also contains a coffee/bean/roast/espresso indicator.
