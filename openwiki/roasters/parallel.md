# Parallel — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Parallel Coffee (www.parallelcoffee.co.uk) is a specialty coffee company
based in Glasgow, Scotland, founded by dedicated enthusiasts and committed to
sourcing and roasting each season's highest-quality coffee (per their site).
It runs on Squarespace in GBP; the `coffee-beans` collection holds 15 beans —
single origins and blends, roughly £14.99 each, plus brewing tools and barista
training. The name nods to the equatorial "parallel" lines within which all
coffee grows.
