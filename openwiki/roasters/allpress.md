# Allpress Espresso — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Allpress Espresso was founded in **1989** in Auckland, New Zealand by
Michael Allpress. It has grown into a global roaster with roasteries and cafes in
Auckland, **London (Dalston Lane), Manchester**, Sydney, Melbourne, Tokyo and
Singapore. The UK shop is the Shopify storefront at
[uk.allpress.com](https://uk.shop.allpressespresso.com).

## Address

- 55 Dalston Lane, Dalston, London — United Kingdom (the London Roastery & Café;
  roasteries also in Auckland NZ, Sydney AU, Melbourne AU, Tokyo JP, Singapore SG)

## Roasting & Equipment

- Uses a distinctive **hot-air roasting method** that the brand credits for a
  "sweet, clean, consistent flavour... batch after batch" — the basis of their
  consistency promise.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Four company values: **Iconic Flavour, Cafe Leadership, Grounded in Good,
  Uniting Independent Thinkers**.
- The consistency promise is stated plainly: "The coffee you have today should
  taste the same as the coffee you had yesterday."
- Partners with **2,200+ boutique cafes/restaurants** worldwide.
- Publishes a **Human Rights Policy** and a **Whistleblower Procedure** — a
  governance layer rare among roasters this size.
- Range: Allpress Espresso, A.R.T. Espresso, The Good Brew, House Decaf, and
  Single Origins.

## Scraping Quirks

- **Wholesale dedup**: each blend/single origin appears twice in the UK shop —
  once as the retail product and again as a wholesale `-office` / `-3kg`
  duplicate handle. The duplicates are dropped, along with the
  `discovery-coffee-subscription` product.
