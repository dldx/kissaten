# Swan Song Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Swan Song Coffee Roasters (swansong.coffee) is a solo-operated, 100%
independently owned specialty micro-roastery in Salford, Manchester (per their
About page). The founder started Swan Song in 2018 after working up from pot
washing to barista trainer, and placed 2nd in the SCA Brewers Cup 2019. The
site footer notes the business trades as One Percent Coffee Limited. Seasonal
single origins are sold from a Squarespace storefront in GBP.

## Address

- Unit 4, Regents Trading Estate, Oldfield Road, Salford M5 4DE, United
  Kingdom (published on the site).

## Roasting & Equipment

- Roasts in small batches on roasting equipment produced by Stronghold
  Technology using entirely electric power — "technology-aided artisan
  roasting" (per their About page).
