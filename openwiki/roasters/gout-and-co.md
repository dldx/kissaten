# Goût and Co — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Goût and Co is a Chengdu-based specialty coffee roaster with a Shopify storefront. The shop describes itself as a micro-lot specialty coffee roastery and separates coffees into Ultra-light Roast and Recommend for Modern Espresso collections. Its public tagline is “Terroir tells, Roasting connects, Coffee speaks.”

## Address

- Chengdu, China — full roastery address not published on the site.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Goût and Co states that it ships worldwide. Its current Europe notice says free shipping applies to orders over USD 150 and that orders below USD 150 incur a USD 29.9 shipping fee. No roast or dispatch cadence was found.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The catalogue’s ultra-light and modern-espresso collections make the intended roast style explicit rather than relying only on individual product descriptions.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses Shopify’s `collections/all/products.json` endpoint and removes collection segments from product URLs before fetching pages. It excludes slugs for subscriptions, gifts, wholesale, equipment, accessories, merchandise, collaborations, cups and limited products.
