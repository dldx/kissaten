# Taller Café — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Taller Café is a Chilean specialty coffee roaster and coffee education
business in Valparaíso, with a Shopify storefront. Its coffee collection
includes single origins, blends, decaf and packs, while the wider store sells
barista courses, tea, equipment and café supplies. The site presents coffee
products in Chilean pesos.

## Address

- Valparaíso, Chile — the site publishes Yungay #2554, but does not separately
  identify that storefront address as the roastery; the roastery address is
  therefore not confirmed on site.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The business combines roasting with Saturday talks, barista training and a
  physical shop. Its catalogue therefore mixes coffee with courses, tea,
  equipment and café goods.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper targets only the `cafe-en-grano` collection and translates
  Spanish product content to English for AI extraction.
- It explicitly filters packs, Saturday-talk products, courses, equipment,
  accessories, merchandise, subscriptions, gift cards and wholesale items.
