# Fluir Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Fluir Coffee is a small, wholesale-focused specialty roastery "based between Surrey
and London" (per their contact page; their site meta bills them as a "London based
coffee curator"). The company was founded in 2025 and is run by a woman roaster —
a single mother who relocated from Asia to Surrey and started her new chapter as a
barista. The storefront is a minimalist Shopify shop at
[fluir.coffee](https://www.fluir.coffee) selling coffees named after cocktails
(Fruit Punch, Gimlet, Highball, Nightcap, Old Fashioned, Peach Spritz).

## Address

- Surrey / London, United Kingdom — described as "based between Surrey and
  London"; full street address not published on the site

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Motto: "**Sharing is love**, that's where everything begins for us."
- The founding story is central: the founder "relocated from Asia to Surrey with
  her young son and started her new chapter as a barista" — "with heart and
  determination, nothing is impossible" (per their about page).
- Their approach is explicitly inspired by the coffee cultures of **Taiwan and
  Japan** — "precision, balance, and respect for craft."
- Wholesale-first: the contact page pitches "tailor-made training and support"
  for like-minded coffee businesses rather than retail.

## Scraping Quirks

- **Sold-out products are filtered out at URL-extraction time** — the scraper
  skips any product card whose tile contains a "Sold out" label, so sold-out
  coffees (e.g. Fruit Punch, Peach Spritz at times) never enter the catalogue
  until they are back in stock.
