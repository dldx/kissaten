# Indigo Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Indigo Coffee Roasters began in 2016 as an idea by two students and hospitality and coffee workers, according to its Spanish-language history. It describes itself as an end-to-end specialty-coffee supplier covering green coffee, importing, roasting, storage, training, logistics and equipment. The Tiendanube storefront sells blends, single origins, microlots, limited editions and tasting boxes in Argentine pesos.

## Address

- O'Higgins 35, San Isidro, Buenos Aires Province, Argentina — published on the contact page, but the page does not explicitly label this as a roastery address.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Indigo advertises delivery to the customer within 48 hours of purchase with no extra charge on the consulted homepage. No roast cadence, dispatch days, free-order minimum or destination-specific rates were published there.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Its tagline is “Café posta” and its wholesale offer includes equipment advice, ongoing staff training and monthly supervision with reports for participating cafés and retailers. The shop also sells a three-coffee, 375 g tasting box.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper visits four separate archives (limited editions, single origins, microlots and blends), minimizes each product page to title/meta, the `#single-product` area and structured data, and asks the AI extractor to translate the Spanish content to English. The shop contains courses, mugs and other non-bean goods, so the collection boundaries—not the homepage—are the catalogue contract.
