# Salford Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Salford Roasters (salfordroasters.co.uk) was formed in September 2016 with the
mission to bring "proper coffee" to Salford and Manchester, founded by Nik
Storey (per their site). It roasts sustainably sourced specialty coffees on an
IMF machine and sells beans, subscriptions, chocolate and coffee kit from a
Shopify storefront in GBP.

## Address

- Unit E, Protector Lamp Business Park, Lansdowne Road, Eccles, M30 9PH,
  Salford/Manchester, United Kingdom (published on the site).

## Roasting & Equipment

- Roasts on an IMF roaster (the IMF patented Vortex system with a separate
  heat generator), which the site says needs no additional catalyser and keeps
  carbon emissions below legal limits (per their site).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Free coffee delivery over £28 (with code SR28).
- "Call in / collect your coffee" at the M30 9PH roastery — no need to call
  ahead.
