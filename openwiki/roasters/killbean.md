# KillBean Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

KillBean Coffee is a tiny London (Kilburn) microroaster selling on a
Squarespace storefront at killbean.co.uk (product URLs under `/shop/p/`). It
focuses on exotic, high-process-experimentation microlots — Hachi Project
"Symbiotic Process" and Cascade Fermentation Yunnan lots (around £19), plus
Hacienda La Esmeralda lots — and its own brewing-accessories line, including
lyocell filter papers. Prices are in GBP.

## Address

- 207 Belsize Road, London NW6 4AA — United Kingdom.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Not published: product pages name producers and farms (Andres Torres,
  Leonidas Jaramillo, Hacienda La Esmeralda, Jurutungo), but no FOB /
  farm-gate prices are published.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The site banner reads "FREE SHIPPING ON ALL UK ORDERS OVER £40. Our shipping
  day is Monday/Tuesday/Friday." (per their site).
- No roast-day schedule is published.

## Scraping Quirks

- Squarespace storefront: products are scraped from `/shop` with `/shop/p/`
  URLs.
- The scraper explicitly excludes KillBean's own brewing-accessories line
  (lyocell filter papers, V60, dosing cups, brewing pads) and merch
  (caps, t-shirts) so only coffee products land in the catalogue.
