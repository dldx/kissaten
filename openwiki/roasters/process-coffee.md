# Process Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Process (self-styled **Pr_cess**) is a specialty coffee roaster and "lifestyle
supply co" based in Belfast, Northern Ireland, founded in 2020 amid the
pandemic by Ben Hamilton, who previously co-founded and ran Belfast coffee &
donut shop Guilt Trip (2017). The Shopify storefront sells small-batch coffee
that is **single origin only**, alongside gear and apparel. Ben also works as
a coffee consultant, offering strategy and bar training to other ventures.

## Address

- Belfast, Northern Ireland — United Kingdom (full address not published on
  the site)

## Roasting & Equipment

- Roasted small batch, focusing only on single-origin offerings — "showcasing
  every coffee selected in its truest form", with attention to varietal,
  terroir and processing.
- Specific roasting hardware is not published on the site.

## Schedules & Shipping

- UK orders are shipped within 3 working days and usually arrive around 2
  days later via Royal Mail 1st Class.
- International shipping is offered, with costs calculated at checkout;
  customers are responsible for any import duties/taxes.
- No free-delivery minimum is published.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Motto: **"Future Fond Memories"** — the small, meaningful moments shared
  over a cup; also the name of their blog.
- Signature quirk: packaging inspired by **nostalgic VHS tapes** — sliding the
  tray out of the sleeve to reveal the coffee. Featured by *It's Nice That*
  ("rewinds to the days of VHS"), *Sprudge*, and *The Independent*, which
  awarded Process top marks for best coffee packaging: "we really thought it
  was an old school VHS tape."
- Branding by local Belfast studio Angel & Anchor; the wordmark renders the
  brand as "Pr_cess".
- Tagline energy throughout: "Get involved, have fun and stay rad!" 🤙

## Scraping Quirks

- **Sold-out products never enter the catalogue**: the collection-page
  extractor skips any `div.product-item` whose text contains "Sold out", so
  sold-out coffees are invisible to the scraper rather than being recorded
  and diffed.
- Non-coffee products (the Process x PCW collab, pick-n-mix, beanies,
  coffee-to-go, gift subscriptions, boxsets) are filtered out by URL pattern
  before AI extraction runs.
