# Code.194 Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Code.194 Coffee (code194coffee.com, trading as "Code.194 Coffee Roasters LTD")
is a UK coffee roaster on a Shopify storefront in GBP. The brand's mission, per
its own about page, is "to take you on a captivating journey of flavour
discovery through the world of coffee", and it stresses working with
specialty-grade importers to keep every bean "of the highest quality but also
ethically sourced". Locality is not disclosed on the site.
