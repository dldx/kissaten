# Perky Blenders — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Perky Blenders is an East London roastery that describes itself as "one big
family", family-formed in 2015 from a humble coffee cart in Walthamstow. Today
it supplies wholesale coffee to over 100 coffee shops, runs five of its own
Perky stores (Leyton, Leytonstone and Walthamstow kiosks, plus sites at CRATE
Walthamstow and the Royal Docks), and roasts everything at its East London
roastery in Leytonstone. Shopify storefront with blends, a rotating
**Coffee of the Month** (e.g. Bukonzo Dream from Uganda's Rwenzori Mountains)
and a subscription service featured in the Independent's "Indy Best".

## Address

- Unit 10, Acacia Business Centre, London, E11 3PJ — United Kingdom
  (the "East London Roastery"; Leytonstone area)

## Sustainability

- **Bike delivery**: all shops and wholesale customers within 10 km of the
  roastery get coffee delivered by cargo bike (via Wheely Goods) — over 21
  tonnes / 4,000+ miles per year taken by bike instead of car.
- **"The Buzz"** — their fully electric van for longer drops.
- Coffee waste: ~1.3 tonnes of grounds and chaff collected monthly, mostly
  cycled to local allotments as fertiliser; coffee itself is compostable.
- Packaging: fully recyclable LDPE 4 bags and cardboard sleeves, with bag
  return points at all Perky shops including the roastery.
- Working towards **B Corp certification**.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Self-described "no flings, and no shopping around for the cheapest price" —
  long-term relationships with farms, producers and co-ops, favouring suppliers
  who champion smallholders, pay decent wages and restore the environment.
- Product pages publish origin and supplier information for each coffee.
- Some suppliers (e.g. San Coffee, Minor Figures) are themselves B Corp
  certified.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Everything dispatched within 1 business day: orders before 8am (Mon–Fri)
  ship the same day, later orders the following day; Friday 8am–Monday 8am
  orders ship Monday.
- Delivery services: Royal Mail 24/48 (approx. 1–2 days from dispatch) and DPD
  (next working day). No free-delivery threshold is published; rates are
  calculated at checkout.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Family is the brand: "we were family-formed back in 2015" — and they still
  call customers the "Perky fam".
- Award history: two **Time Out Awards** as 'Best Coffee Place' within three
  London postcodes; a **Great Taste Producer with over 26 stars**.
- Sells the **Fermentation Project (James Hoffmann x Lucia Solis)** kit and
  Sage/Aeropress brew gear alongside the coffee range.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper fetches two curated collections (`filter-coffee` and
  `espresso-coffee`) rather than the full catalogue; these hold the blends, the
  rotating `singleorigin` product and `coffee-of-the-month`.
- Non-bean products are filtered by slug: `twin-pack`, `brew-bags`,
  `selection-box`, `cold-brew-can` and the `variety-club` subscription are
  excluded so they never reach the catalogue.
