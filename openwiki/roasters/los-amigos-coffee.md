# Los Amigos Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Los Amigos Coffee is a small coffee business associated with Albuquerque, New Mexico, United States. Owner Guillermo describes himself as a self-taught roaster and barista, with a focus on unusual, distinctive coffees and community around shared coffee. The Wix storefront has carried blends, single-origin and experimental coffees, including Colombian co-fermented lots; the registered scraper declaration says the roaster is no longer active.

## Address

- Albuquerque, New Mexico, United States — full roastery address not published on the accessible site.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Product copy on the live site identifies producers, farms, collaborators and detailed fermentation steps for some Colombian coffees. For example, its Colombia Coco/Mango listing names Pedro Nel Salazar, Finca Sicilia, Quindío, and collaborator Edwin Noreña; it does not publish the price paid to producers or an FOB cost breakdown on the pages checked.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Guillermo’s about page says the business grew from a personal interest in roasting and a belief that coffee builds community. The catalogue’s distinctive emphasis is on coffees that are “different, exciting, and sometimes a bit unexpected,” including multi-stage co-fermentations.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper is registered as `unavailable` and targets two Wix category pages: blends and a Tanzania specialty collection. It relies on Wix’s `data-hook` product-list/product-item selectors, checks the immediate parent text for `Out of Stock`, and excludes only URL matches for tees, stickers and general merchandise.
