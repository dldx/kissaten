# Aviary — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Aviary describes itself as a hyper-focused specialty coffee roaster founded on environmental responsibility, economic sustainability, progress and deliciousness. It is the roasting project of coffee buyer and consultant Christopher Feran, operating as a small-volume, direct-to-consumer project in Ohio. Its Shopify shop sells limited seasonal releases, with most public releases pre-sold before roasting.

## Address

- Ohio, United States — full roastery address not published on the consulted site pages.

## Sustainability

- Aviary says it selects farms using practices such as shade and agroforestry, water conservation and protection of native or traditionally Indigenous lands. It uses an electricity supplier made up solely of solar and wind generation, ships green coffee by ocean freight where possible, and says Shopify Planet makes each order shipped through the store carbon neutral. Its 200g and 1kg bags are made from recyclable LDPE; the outer box and inner card for 200g bags are also recyclable.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Aviary says it sources exclusively from philosophically aligned producers and pays prices well above the long-term cost of production. It does not publish the specific price paid for each release on the general Q&A page.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Coffee is roasted in small batches on a fully electric Roest P3000. Aviary says its coffees are light or very light roasts and that it tracks colorimetric readings internally without publishing the readings.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Aviary packages and ships immediately after roasting; each coffee's product page carries the relevant roasting and fulfilment schedule. It ships worldwide and offers free shipping on every coffee order within the United States. No non-US rate table was found on the consulted page.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Releases are one-offs or limited drops, prioritised for subscribers, and Aviary does not publish cupping scores. The name and quetzal mark are explained through the relationship between cloud-forest biodiversity, shade-grown coffee and threatened habitat.
