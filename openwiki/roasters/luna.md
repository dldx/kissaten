# Luna — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Luna is a coffee roaster based outside Vancouver, Canada, founded by Laura and
Nate in autumn 2017. The Shopify storefront centers on seasonal light-roast
coffees and a monthly subscription of two unique coffees plus a physical zine;
the site is currently showing a temporary pause caused by a roaster mechanical
failure.

## Address

- Outside Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada — full roastery address not
  published on site.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Luna's product pages identify farms, washing stations, cooperatives and
  producer counts for individual lots, including the 476 smallholder
  producers supplying Chelbesa One in Ethiopia. The reviewed pages do not
  publish FOB or price-paid-to-producer figures.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Subscriptions ship monthly; the site says shipping is included for Canada
  and the USA. Luna recommends resting beans for two weeks off roast and says
  the light-roast coffees benefit from an extended shelf life of about two
  months.
- The site currently states that one-time orders can ship to the UK through
  Canada Post and Royal Mail, with subscriptions available soon; it does not
  publish a general one-time-order dispatch schedule or free-shipping threshold
  on the reviewed pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Luna describes its work through "alive soils", experimentation with
  fermentation and a light touch of heat, while keeping watch on each lot's
  health before roasting.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses the coffee-only Shopify `coffees` collection and forces CAD
  because Shopify geolocates the storefront currency by request location. It
  canonicalizes collection product URLs to `/products/<handle>`.
