# Vibe With Coffee Roastery — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Vibe With is a Nottingham coffee-shop-and-roastery ("Coffee shops & roastery in
Nottingham", per their site) run by Vibe With Company Limited, incorporated in
March 2022. The founders — Vanessa, a former laboratory researcher, and Levi,
who has worked in coffee for around a decade — first met in their native Hong
Kong before moving to the UK and settling in Nottingham (per local press). The
site is a Wix storefront with a small e-commerce section.

## Address

- 15 Hounds Gate, Nottingham, NG1 7AA — United Kingdom (the only address the
  site publishes, for its coffee shop and roastery together; the shop moved
  here from Mansfield Road)

## Roasting & Equipment

- **Stronghold S7pro** roaster — entrusting "the repetitive roasting process to
  our reliable system" so the team can focus on the cup (per their about page).
- **Decent Espresso** machines with flow control and pressure curves.
- **Lighttells CM200** for measurement — "Discover the truth in numbers" — with
  results published on a **"Brewing Board"** the shop updates daily.

## Schedules & Shipping

- **Free UK shipping for orders above £35** (stated on the contact page).
- No roast/dispatch schedule, delivery timeframes or per-region rates are
  published on the site.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The guiding line, repeated on the about page: **"We value precision and
  scientific principles"** — traceability and consistency via "reliable
  machines", with quality data displayed publicly on the Brewing Board.
- The wholesale page reads as a manifesto as much as an offer: partnerships
  sought with brands that "espouse but also demonstrate" commitment to quality,
  and a belief that "every cup of coffee is an opportunity to foster meaningful
  connections".
