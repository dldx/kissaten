# Rafiki Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Rafiki Coffee (rafikicoffee.com) is an Edinburgh specialty coffee roaster
whose live storefront succeeds the former Tanifiki café at 44 Portobello High
Street — it is scraped under the checklist "Tanifiki" row. The whole-bean menu
centres on Rwandan and Burundian single origins (Rwanda Gisheke, Rwanda Shyira
Washed, Burundi Izuba at e2e) with a rotating seasonal selection, sold from a
Shopify storefront in GBP.

## Address

- 44 Portobello High Street, Edinburgh, EH15 1DA, United Kingdom — the
  published storefront/café address (the former Tanifiki premises); no separate
  roastery street address is published.

## Scraping Quirks

- Scraped under the checklist "Tanifiki" row: the storefront succeeds the
  Tanifiki café and the old tanifiki.co.uk domain no longer serves the store.
