# Homeground Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Homeground Coffee Roasters is a specialty roastery in the east of Singapore
and a community for home brewers. Its Shopify storefront sells espresso and
filter beans, drip bags, subscriptions and brewing equipment. The site says
its coffee is roasted in Singapore.

## Address

- 911 Bukit Timah Rd, Singapore 589622, Singapore.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Singapore orders are packed and shipped within 1–3 working days and usually
  arrive in 1–3 working days. Local courier working days are Monday–Friday,
  excluding public holidays.
- Free Singapore delivery applies from SGD 42.00. Same-day delivery can be
  arranged for an additional $22 per delivery, excluding Tuas, Sentosa and
  Pulau Ubin.
- International orders are dispatched within 1–3 working days; the site gives
  delivery estimates of 1–4 working days in Southeast Asia and 4–12 working
  days for the rest of the world. Duties, brokerage and clearance are the
  customer's responsibility.
