# Sango Speciality Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Sango Speciality Coffee is a micro-roastery based in Amsterdam, focused on ethical sourcing. The Shopify storefront groups its catalogue through product-type filters for coffee beans, competition, espresso, filter and “Filter - PEARLS”. Direct pages were rate-limited during this research pass, so no unsupported claims about machine, packaging, prices paid or shipping are recorded.

## Address

- Amsterdam, Netherlands — full roastery street address was not published on the consulted pages.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper visits two filtered Shopify collection pages, including page 2, and removes query strings from product links.
- On product pages it removes the featured-collection carousel before AI extraction. It skips products whose card says “Sold out” and excludes equipment/accessories, subscriptions, gifts, merchandise, capsules and other non-bean URL patterns.
