# Code Black Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Code Black Coffee is a Melbourne specialty roaster established in Brunswick in
2012. It sells signature blends, single origins and decaffeinated coffee, and
also operates cafes, wholesale supply and a training facility. The storefront
is Shopify; the site describes sourcing from partners locally and at origin and
offers both filter and espresso ranges.

## Address

- 15-17 Weston Street, Brunswick, Victoria 3056, Australia.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Code Black says it aims to respect the work behind each lot and to honour the
  producer through the final product. It does not publish FOB or farm-gate
  prices on the pages consulted.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Online orders are packed and dispatched within 48 business hours from the
  Melbourne roastery. Domestic metropolitan standard delivery is estimated at
  3-5 business days and express at 2-4 business days. International orders
  should arrive within 14 business days, although this varies by location.
- The site banner states free delivery for orders over $59.00. Australia Post
  handles domestic and international deliveries; import taxes and duties are
  the customer's responsibility.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses the `coffee` Shopify collection and canonicalizes collection-
  prefixed product URLs to `/products/<handle>`. It excludes subscriptions,
  bundles, gifts and wholesale products.
