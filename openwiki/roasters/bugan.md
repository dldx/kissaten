# Bugan Coffee Lab — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Bugan Coffee Lab is an Italian specialty coffee lab and micro-roastery founded
in Bergamo on 3 December 2014 by siblings Sonia and Maurizio Valli. Its Shopify
store sells specialty coffee and competition lots alongside academy training,
workshops and equipment; the business also lists cafés in Milan and Bergamo.
The founders say their first coffee business began in 2000 and that the lab
grew from Maurizio's origin visits and barista training.

## Address

- Bergamo, Italy — full roastery address not published on the consulted site
  pages. The site lists café locations separately in Milan and Bergamo.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Bugan says Maurizio visits a different plantation each year to select coffee
  directly at origin. It describes traceability and qualified sensory scoring
  as selection criteria, and groups coffees into approximately 80–90-point
  “for everyone” specialty coffees and 90+ competition coffees. No price-paid-
  to-producer figures or cost breakdown is published on the consulted pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The shop says specialty coffee proposals change weekly. Its banner states
  free shipping in Italy for orders over €59; no dispatch-day promise or
  destination-specific rate table was found on the consulted pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The name comes from buganvillea (bougainvillea), associated by Maurizio with
  nature and the lab's three-petal logo. Bugan also runs training and cupping
  activities and presents coffee as an emotional, accessible experience rather
  than only a competition product.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper reads the Shopify specialty-coffee JSON endpoint, rewrites direct
  product links into the English collection path, excludes cold brew and
  tasting-box/degustazione handles, and translates Italian product content to
