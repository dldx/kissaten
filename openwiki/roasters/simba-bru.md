# Simba Bru — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Simba Bru (simbabru.co.uk) is a specialty coffee roaster in Watford,
Hertfordshire, founded in 2020 after founder Abbas — with his wife Sayyeda —
brought green coffee beans back from her home country of Tanzania and started
roasting them in a pan (per their site). It sells single origins, blends,
seasonal roasts and decaf from a Shopify storefront in GBP.

## Address

- 61a Sutton Road, Watford WD17 2QE, Hertfordshire, United Kingdom (published

## Philosophy & Quirks

- "We're all about coffee, fun, and good vibes"; the story starts with beans
  from Tanzania, reflected in the brand's roots (per their site).
