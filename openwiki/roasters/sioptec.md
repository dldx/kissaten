# Sioptec — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Sioptec (sioptec.co) is a Manchester, UK coffee roastery focused on
seasonal coffees that highlight producers and coffees from all origins "with
clarity, character and sweetness" (per their site). It sells a small curated
range of single origins and micro-lots from a Shopify storefront in GBP.

## Address

- Manchester, United Kingdom ("all coffees are roasted in Manchester"; full
  address not published on site).

## Roasting & Equipment

- Roasts on a Loring S7, described as enabling "clean, bright and transparent
  profiles" (per their site).

## Schedules & Shipping

- All coffees are roasted in Manchester and shipped across the UK, EU and
  internationally (per their site).
