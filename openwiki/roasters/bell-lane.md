# Bell Lane — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Bell Lane is an Irish specialty coffee roaster whose site says its story began in 2012. The Shopify shop offers blend, single-origin, filter, espresso and decaffeinated coffee, plus subscriptions, equipment and training. Bell Lane describes itself as a certified B Corporation.

## Address

- Unit 19A, Zone C, Clonmore Business Park, Clonmore, Mullingar, Co. Westmeath, N91 A096, Ireland.

## Sustainability

- Bell Lane says its IMF roaster reduces emissions by up to 98% compared with traditional drum roasters. Its responsibility page also mentions eco-friendly packaging and suppliers with sustainable practices; the page does not specify the packaging material.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Bell Lane says genuine producer relationships are central to its work and that it partners with producers it knows and trusts. Its site presents this as a route to better coffee and positive impact in producing communities, but the consulted pages do not publish price-paid-to-producer figures or a cost breakdown.

## Roasting & Equipment

- The roastery uses an IMF roasting system. Bell Lane says it meticulously roasts beans to their full potential and opens the roastery for interactive brew classes; no machine capacity or roasting software is published on the consulted pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Bell Lane's “Grow Together” wholesale offering, B Corp status and producer-focused language make community and shared growth recurring themes in the site presentation.
