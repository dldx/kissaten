# Momos Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Momos Coffee is a Busan specialty coffee company that began in 2007 as a small takeaway shop. Its official English site now presents a roastery and coffee bar in Yeongdo, education, e-commerce, subscriptions, and several coffee categories including signature, seasonal, single, and Havana coffees. The company’s English brand story says it has expanded from direct trade in four Central American countries to work with more than 130 producers in over 16 countries.

## Address

- 160 Bongnaenaru-ro, Yeongdo-gu, Busan 49003, Republic of Korea — the company information identifies this as the business address and the brand story identifies the Yeongdo site as the roastery and coffee bar.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Momos says it travelled to origins, met producers, and began direct trade with four Central American countries in 2011. It now says it works with more than 130 producers in over 16 countries to build long-term relationships and sustainability. No price-paid-to-producer or FOB figures are published on the reviewed pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The brand story uses “Specialty for All” as its central message. The Yeongdo roastery is described as a place intended to improve the coffee-production environment, bring customers closer to roasters and baristas, and share specialty coffee culture.
