# La Tostadora — Roaster Profile

## Overview

La Tostadora (latostadora.coffee) is a London coffee roastery ("La Tostadora
Roastery" per its structured data) roasting from a railway arch at Poyser
Street in East London's E2. It runs on a Wix storefront in GBP with a small
whole-bean catalogue.

## Address

- Railway Arch 267, Poyser Street, London, E2 9RF — United Kingdom (published
  as LocalBusiness structured data on the site).
