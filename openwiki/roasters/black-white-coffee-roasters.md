# Black & White Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Black & White Coffee Roasters is based in Raleigh, North Carolina. Founded in 2017 by US Barista Champions Kyle Ramage and Lem Butler, it set out to make competition-level coffees more widely available. The store groups coffees by roast level and by its clean, mild, and funky "Shop by Funk" categories, alongside year-round and limited Black Label coffees.

## Address

- Raleigh, North Carolina - United States (full street address not published on the site pages checked)

## Schedules & Shipping

- The shipping page says orders ship the same day when processed and that the roastery roasts Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday. Orders placed before 12:00 AM Eastern Time roast and ship on the next roast day. US shipping is free over USD 40; wholesale orders are excluded.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Black & White says its values are Service, Community, and Excellence. It aims to connect people with interesting and approachable coffees while challenging assumptions about what specialty coffee should taste like.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper uses the `all-coffee` Shopify JSON collection, which sits beside the shop's separate roast-level, funk, instant, archive, and merchandise collections. Product-page extraction is enabled, while subscriptions, drip bags, bundles, pods, cascara, and gift cards are excluded by slug.
