# Café Amor Perfecto — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Café Amor Perfecto is a Colombian specialty coffee company founded in 1997 by
Luis Fernando Vélez. It describes itself as roasting at origin and sells
producer coffees, signature coffees, accessories and capsules through its
Shopify storefront. The company also operates experience centres and an
academy; those retail locations are separate from the roastery address.

## Address

- Bogotá, Colombia — the site publishes Carrera 65 #81–58, Parque Industrial
  Entre Ríos as its Bogotá address, but does not explicitly identify that
  address as the roastery; no separately labelled roastery address was found.

## Sustainability

- Amor Perfecto's 2025 sustainability report says it works directly with
  Colombian coffee producers, removes intermediaries and pays producers
  approximately twice the market rate. The same page says the company creates
  direct employment and bases producer relationships on trust, work and
  sustainability.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The history page names direct relationships with producers including Astrid
  Medina, Elías Roa and Diego Campos, and says the company searches Colombia's
  producing regions for exceptional coffees. It reports 15 coffee farmers in
  the company's current “Así somos” figures, but publishes no per-lot FOB or
  farm-gate price breakdown on the consulted pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Amor Perfecto says its origin-roasting project began exporting in 2013 and
  aims to let the world taste Colombian coffee roasted in origin. Its history
  also records its role in bringing barista training and national coffee
  championships to Colombia, including the 2021 world title won by its barista
  Diego Campo.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper reads only the Shopify `cafes-de-caficultor` collection, drops
  handles containing collection, organic, bundle, gift-card, accessory,
  coffee-drip-bag, tea or cafetera patterns, canonicalises product URLs, and
  translates Spanish descriptions to English. This means the site's signature
  coffee, accessory and capsule collections are intentionally outside the
