# Northside Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Northside Coffee Roasters (www.northsidecoffee.co) is a micro-roastery &
coffee shop in Morpeth, Northumberland, roasting espresso blends and specialty
single-origin coffees and selling whole-bean from a Wix storefront.

## Address

- 3G Coopies Field, Morpeth, Northumberland NE61 6JT, United Kingdom.

## Scraping Quirks

- Domain correction: the canonical shop is www.northsidecoffee.co — the `.co.uk`
  variant is not the live storefront.
- Wix storefront; the whole-bean catalogue lives on the single `/buy-coffee`
  listing page.
- Non-coffee items sold alongside the beans (Huskee cups, a camping mug, a
  machine-cleaning powder, a single-origin hot chocolate) are excluded.
- e2e: 19 saved, no kit flags.
