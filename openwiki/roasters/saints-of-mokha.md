# Saints of Mokha — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Saints of Mokha (saintsofmokha.com) is a young, family-run coffee business
established in 2016 in Leicester (per their site), sourcing and roasting what
it describes as the highest-quality coffee available. It sells beans,
subscriptions and equipment from a Shopify storefront in GBP and is known for
its Yemen-focused provenance alongside single origins and blends.

## Address

- 51-53 Belvoir Street, Leicester LE1 6SL, United Kingdom (published on the
  contact page).

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The site describes a sourcing approach that looks at how each coffee was
  grown and processed, its sustainability, and "the people and communities
  behind it" before cupping, preferring the exceptional over volume (per their
  site).

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders are roasted fresh and dispatched every Wednesday and Friday.
- Free shipping on orders over £30.
