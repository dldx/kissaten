# Round Hill Roastery — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Round Hill Roastery has been roasting in Somerset since **2012**, when founder
Eddie Twitchett — then working in Bath hospitality — put his savings into a
roaster in a Radstock industrial unit and sold weekly at Bath Farmers Market
until wholesale partners arrived (per third-party profiles; company
incorporated 16 February 2012 per Companies House). Today it is a small team on
a Shopify storefront at
[roundhillroastery.com](https://roundhillroastery.com), treating its offer list
as a **seasonal menu** that evolves with global harvest cycles — "coffee is a
fruit, and tastes best when in season".

## Address

- Unit 14 Midsomer Enterprise Park, Midsomer Norton, Radstock, Bath and North
  East Somerset (BANES), BA3 2BB — United Kingdom

## Sustainability

- Claims to have **eliminated roasted-coffee waste** at the roastery over the
  past decade.
- Packaging developed with Dutch Pack: coffee bags are **100% recyclable and
  carbon neutral**, sized to reduce surplus material, fit through a UK
  letterbox and keep coffee fresh.
- Delivers to local wholesale customers in **reusable tubs**, eliminating
  packaging waste.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Buys seasonally through **direct trade or trusted import partners at prices
  well above the C-market**, ensuring farmers get paid a premium.
- "Key relationship coffees": farms and producers bought from for the best
  part of a decade, committing to buy house coffees from single estates or
  cooperatives **at volume** — offering producers stability while showcasing
  their smaller, more unique lots alongside.
- Buying from single producers or cooperatives gives full transparency over
  where the coffees come from.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Roasts on a **direct-flame Probat**, combining traditional flame roasting
  with a Scandinavian-inspired approach celebrating clarity, brightness and
  origin character; the style is described as "expressive" — sweet, fruity,
  vibrant.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Roasts and ships **Monday to Friday**; cut-off is **8 a.m.**, after which
  orders ship the next working day. Friday orders ship the following Monday;
  closed on public holidays and over Christmas.
- Standard UK shipping: **Royal Mail 48-hour tracked** (3–5 working days from
  dispatch); a 24-hour tracked service and DPD next-working-day are also
  offered. Bags are letterbox-friendly.
- International: Royal Mail tracked, up to 28 days, with possible import fees.
- No free-delivery threshold is published on the site.
