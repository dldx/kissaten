# Rounton Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Rounton Coffee Roasters is an independent specialty roastery based in an old
granary building in East Rounton, near Northallerton, North Yorkshire. It was
founded in 2013 by David Beattie, a former chemical engineer who fell in love
with coffee during a visit to Sumatra; per a February 2026 Business Live
interview the roastery now roasts up to a tonne of coffee a day and packs
around 2,150 bags a week. The storefront is Shopify at
[rountoncoffee.co.uk](https://rountoncoffee.co.uk), selling blends, seasonal
single origins, decaf, subscriptions and brew gear.

## Address

- East Rounton, near Northallerton, North Yorkshire — United Kingdom (the
  converted granary roastery; full street address not published on the site).
  Order returns are handled from Unit 84, Willows Court, Teesside Industrial
  Estate, Stockton-on-Tees, TS17 9PP.

## Sustainability

- Member of **1% for the Planet**, donating 1% of annual sales to
  environmental causes.
- Carbon-neutral, home-recyclable coffee bags.
- Local wholesale deliveries run a "milk round" of reusable coffee tubs —
  per their site, thousands of coffee bags saved from landfill.
- Pledges an extra **60p per kilo** of Ugandan green coffee to Agri Evolve's
  **ACE 2030** programme (40p of it funds a new tree in their nursery; the
  rest backs community projects such as school pit latrines and water
  boreholes).
- Publishes a Sustainable Impact Proposal as a transparent, measurable
  roadmap; site badges include ACE 2030, Pause Zero and Race To Zero.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Works outside the C-market, paying a premium so more cash reaches
  producers; commits to buying volumes that "bring tangible benefits to
  producers at origin".
- Has worked with Rwenzori Coffee Co. / Agri Evolve in Uganda's Rwenzori
  Mountains since 2018; per their site, over 8,000 kg of green Ugandan
  coffee bought and over £5,000 contributed to ACE 2030 so far.

## Roasting & Equipment

- **Loring roastery**: all coffee roasted on energy-efficient convection
  roasters — a Loring S35 Kestrel and an S15 Falcon. A £150,000 commercial
  roaster investment (per Business Live) uses 80% less energy than
  traditional machines.
- Coffee is best brewed at least 10 days after roast to allow de-gassing.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Roasted to order: orders are checked and printed each morning at 8am and
  the day's roast schedule is built from them; an estimated dispatch date is
  shown at checkout.
- Free UK delivery over **£25** (after discounts; excludes gift
  subscriptions) via Royal Mail 48hr Tracked; parcels up to 2 kg go via
  Royal Mail, over 2 kg via DPD Next Day.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The promise: "to bring more accessibility to the world of specialty
  coffee... without any of the fuss."
- Yorkshire self-deprecation as brand voice: "We don't need to be the
  coolest, or take ourselves too seriously, so long as we're roasting dead
  good coffee."
- Founder David Beattie quit chemical engineering after falling in love with
  coffee on a visit to Sumatra; the early years were farmers markets and
  hand-scribbled roast profiles.
- Describes itself as multi-award-winning (no specific awards listed on the
  profiled pages).
