# Pala Kaffebrenneri — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Pala Kaffebrenneri was founded by Idar Ellingsen in 2018 and is run with Roy
Davidsen in Trondheim, Norway. Pala says its coffees are selected for clean,
distinct flavour profiles and that its range follows the seasons, with a focus
on Africa and South and Central America. The store is a Squarespace storefront.

## Address

- Kvenildmyra 6, 7093 Tiller, Trondheim, Norway.

## Sustainability

- On a current coffee product page, Pala says process water is recycled and
  coffee pulp is returned to farmers for use as organic fertiliser. The same
  page says the producer cooperative pays quality bonuses to its members.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Pala says it prioritises ethical trade and traceability and works with
  importers that share those values. Product pages identify origin, area,
  cooperative or washing station, altitude, varieties and process; no producer
  price or FOB figure is published on the consulted pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Pala advertises free shipping in Norway for orders over NOK 250. Its coffee
  subscription page says subscription parcels with two or more 250 g bags have
  postage included throughout Norway and leave the roastery on the first
  Wednesday of each month. No international shipping rate was published on the
  consulted pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- “Pala” is explained as deriving from Latin for sticking into the soil; the
  name points to the farmers' work. Pala assigns each coffee its own roast
  profile and uses a colour-based “Pala-paletten” to represent flavour
  experiences. It periodically invites visitors to an open roastery.

## Scraping Quirks

- Pala's Squarespace product pages expose key data in Open Graph/product meta
  tags and a `static-context` variants JSON blob, rather than a simple product
  description. The scraper compacts those fields for extraction, skips “Utsolgt”
  cards, excludes Norwegian courses, subscriptions, brewing hardware and gifts,
  translates product extraction to English, and forces currency to NOK.
