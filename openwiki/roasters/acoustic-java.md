# Acoustic Java — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Acoustic Java is a specialty coffee roaster based in Worcester, Massachusetts,
United States. Its Shopify shop separates coffee into light-, medium- and
dark-roast collections and also sells tea, equipment, subscriptions and gifts.
The site's navigation also links to mission, sourcing and community material.

## Address

- 3 Brussels Street, Worcester, MA 01610 — United States (the address published in the site's shipping footer).

## Schedules & Shipping

- The shop advertises free shipping in the lower 48 United States for purchases
  over **$75**. No roast or dispatch cadence was found on the accessible policy
  page.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper merges three Shopify JSON collections: `light-roast`,
  `medium-roast` and `dark-roast`.
- It excludes non-bean handles including subscriptions, gifts, wholesale,
  equipment, merchandise, tea, tumblers, collection boxes and several named
  products (`voyager`, `grouchys`, `roastmeister`) whose origin is not specific
  enough for the catalogue.
