# Revel Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Revel Coffee is a specialty roaster born in Billings, Montana, and says it has operated as a full roastery since 2007. Its BigCommerce shop offers blends and traceable single-origin coffees, including the Revel Blend and coffees from Ethiopia, Colombia and Costa Rica. The company also serves wholesale customers with equipment consulting, private-label blends and fundraising programmes.

## Address

- Billings, Montana, United States — full roastery street address not published on the consulted site pages.

## Sustainability

- Revel says its 35 kg Loring Kestrel roasting technology significantly reduces environmental impact compared with conventional roasting.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Revel says it selects traceable coffees from respected farms and cooperatives and follows ethical sourcing and responsible-trading practices. No price-paid-to-producer figures were published on the consulted pages.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Coffee is roasted to order on a **35 kg Loring Kestrel**. Revel describes the machine as state of the art and links it to consistency, clarity, sweetness and reduced environmental impact.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Revel says it roasts every week, Monday through Thursday. Orders are normally placed on the next roasting day's schedule; it ships via USPS, or FedEx for larger orders. The shop advertises free shipping on orders over **$40 USD**.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The roastery says its goal is to roast exceptional coffee “and do it the right way”, with an emphasis on quality, craftsmanship and honoring people at origin. It reports consistent 90+ Coffee Review scores, a Good Food Award and recognition as Montana's best coffee roaster by *Food & Wine*.
