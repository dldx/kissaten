# Hatch — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Hatch is a specialty coffee company whose Coffee Lab, Roastery and Brewery is
in Markham, Ontario. The company says it moved into its roastery and brewery in
2015 and began with cold brew before expanding its coffee programme. Its shop
offers Foundation, Seasonal, Premium and Peak coffees plus cold brew,
concentrates, workshops and subscriptions.

## Address

- 802B Cochrane Drive, Markham, ON L3R 8C9, Canada.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Hatch lists a Loring 35 kg roaster from 2015 and a Loring 70 kg roaster from
  2022, plus a Stronghold S9X from 2022. It also lists a Sovda Mini process
  system from 2023 and a fully automated bagging line from 2023.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The shop currently states free Canadian shipping on orders with 3 or more
  bags of coffee, and free shipping to Hong Kong and Japan with 4 or more bags.
  It also announces that US orders have resumed. Prices are presented in CAD;
  no general roast-day or delivery-time schedule is published on the consulted
  pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Hatch describes its work as driven by quality, innovation and experimentation.
  The company lists HACCP certification, seven active Q-Graders, and a Carbon
  Neutral badge on its site. Its awards list includes Th3rd Wave Coffee Roaster
  of the Year 2022 and Coffee Collection Innovation 2023.
