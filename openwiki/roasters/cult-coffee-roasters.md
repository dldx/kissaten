# Cult Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Cult Coffee Roasters began in 2014 as a wee café on Buccleuch Street in
Newington, Edinburgh — a short walk from the University of Edinburgh and The
Meadows — and grew into a roastery that now supplies cafés and businesses
across the UK. The café (Cult Espresso) still trades at 104 Buccleuch Street.
The brand plays on its name: it insists it is "not an actual cult", just
coffee people building community from farmers to casual enthusiasts. The site
is a Shopify storefront at [cultcoffeeroasters.com](https://cultcoffeeroasters.com).

## Address

- Unit 13, Turnhouse Court, Brownrigg Yards, Newbridge, Edinburgh EH28 8TQ — United Kingdom

## Schedules & Shipping

- Roasting happens **Monday to Wednesday**; wholesale enquiries should be made
  by Monday evening so orders are released by the next roast day.
- Site banner: **free shipping on orders over £45** (GBP); the shop delivers
  worldwide.
- No per-region rates or other delivery tiers are published on the site.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Tagline: "**Serving Coffee People Since 2014**" and "straight-up coffee
  people brewing and roasting in Edinburgh since 2014".
- The "Not an Actual Cult" section of the about page is the brand's running
  joke — the ethos is building relationships "from farmers, to exporters, to
  the casual coffee enthusiast".
- Strong focus on **seasonality**: every batch is treated as unique and the
  catalogue changes with arrivals.
- Sells an "**Inner Circle**" apparel/enamel-pin line alongside the coffee.

## Scraping Quirks

- The catalogue mixes lots of non-coffee merch, so the scraper excludes
