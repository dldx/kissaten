# 51 Degrees North — Roaster Profile

## Overview

51 Degrees North ("51° Coffee Roasters") is a specialty coffee operation based at
Unit 9 Atlantic Court, Chivenor, Braunton, Barnstaple EX31 4FP in North Devon. It
runs a [Squarespace storefront](https://www.51degreesnorthcoffee.com) selling
single-origin specialty coffees such as African Moon Coffee, Eratoi, Aragash Gelcha,
the Lot 20 Decaf Project AA and Fazenda São Lucas.

Alongside retail beans the business offers **wholesale** accounts for businesses
and a range of **courses and workshops** — coffee roasting, pour-over and home
barista workshops — as well as barista training.

## Address

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The site quotes a wholesale customer — Theo, Good Food and Drink Co. — as saying:
  "The 51° team produce a quality product, delivered with consistent knowledge and
  care."
- Quirk: the site runs a **"Partner Wholesale Login"** alongside its retail shop
  and course/workshop offering, unusual for a roaster this size — the storefront
  doubles as a training academy hub.
