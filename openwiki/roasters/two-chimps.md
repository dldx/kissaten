# Two Chimps Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Two Chimps Coffee (twochimpscoffee.com) is a coffee roaster based in Oakham,
Rutland, running a WordPress + WooCommerce storefront in GBP. It brands
itself "Awesome Coffee Doing Awesome Things", with a target of Net Zero by
2040, and sells whole-bean and ground coffee, tea, hot chocolate, brewing
devices and subscriptions. The catalogue holds 24 saved coffees plus 10
sampler packs flagged for review.

## Address

- 3a, 51 Pillings Road, Oakham, Rutland, LE15 6QF — United Kingdom.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Free UK Tracked 24 delivery on orders over £29; orders under £29 cost
  £4.49.
- Free sitewide UK delivery for coffee subscribers.
- Roasted wholebean or ground to order.

## Scraping Quirks

- WooCommerce storefront scraped with the shared WooCommerce flow.
- Ten sampler / tasting packs are tasting-kit products: they must flow
  through the review queue (`is_tasting_kit` / `requires_review`), never
  excluded.
