# Intelligentsia — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Intelligentsia is a Chicago-rooted specialty coffee company founded by Doug
Zell and Emily Mange in 1995. It grew from an in-store Probat L12 into a
multi-city operation with coffeebars, training labs, grocery distribution and
a Shopify online store offering blends, espresso and seasonal single origins.

## Address

- Chicago Roasting Works, 7300 South Central Avenue, Bedford Park, IL 60638,
  United States.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Intelligentsia says it sources from Direct Trade suppliers in 14 countries,
  traveling to producing communities and building direct relationships. It
  describes the model as promoting monetary transparency and socially and
  environmentally progressive practices, but the pages reviewed do not publish
  FOB or price-paid-to-producer figures.
- Its Chicago facility is described as kosher-certified, and the site carries
  USDA-certified organic coffees.

## Roasting & Equipment

- The original 1995 cafe roasted in-store on a Probat L12. The current
  roasting and packaging facility is in Chicago and roasts to order. The site
  does not identify the current production roaster.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders placed by 3PM CT roast and ship the following business day. The
  facility does not roast Saturday or Sunday; orders after Thursday 3PM and
  before Saturday 3PM ship Monday, and orders after Saturday 3PM and before
  Monday 3PM ship Tuesday.
- Free US shipping is offered from $49. Subscribers receive free shipping on
  subscription orders.
