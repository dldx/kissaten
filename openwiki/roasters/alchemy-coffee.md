# Alchemy Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Alchemy Coffee is an independent, family-run London roastery founded by Anne
and Joe O'Hara, who after a 2000 backpacking trip around Central America left
their corporate jobs and built a coffee-events business from 2004. They have
made the coffee for artists backstage at every Glastonbury since 2005, and
since 2008 have roasted their own coffee. Today they run a WooCommerce
storefront at [alchemycoffee.co.uk](https://alchemycoffee.co.uk), a wholesale
and coffee-events/engineering arm, and three London cafés (Shoreditch,
Spitalfields and Ludgate in the City) — the cafés are retail outposts, not
the roastery.

## Address

- Unit 16, Riverside Business Park, Lyon Road, London, SW19 2RL — United
  Kingdom (visits by appointment only, per their site)

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Roughly **70 percent of the coffee they roast is sourced through direct
  trade relationships** (per their site).
- In direct-trade partnerships they pay the farmer directly with no middle
  men, in the farmer's own currency, straight into the farm's bank account —
  so producers don't lose a portion of their income to a broker.
- Long-term partnerships yield coffees exclusive to Alchemy; their YouTube
  channel shows visits to direct-trade farmers at origin.

## Roasting & Equipment

- They roast on an **85-year-old reconditioned roaster named Brynhildr** —
  "fittingly after 'the fat lady who sings' at the end" of Wagner's Ring —
  rescued and restored after "a long and arduous battle to find this piece of
  history".
- Roast style is light, "designed to bring the best out of some amazing
  specialty coffees".

## Schedules & Shipping

- Orders placed before 9am are, as an aim, processed and dispatched on the
  next working day (not guaranteed).
- Subscriptions are sent via Royal Mail only, on the first UK working day of
  each month.
- Retail delivery is priced on weight, added at checkout; no free-delivery
  minimum is published.
- Wholesale: no minimum order; free shipping on orders over 12kg, otherwise
  next-day trackable courier.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Everything is themed on alchemy: the logo adapts the alchemical symbol for
  gold, with triangular shapes behind it representing the symbols for earth,
  water, air and fire.
- From the events years: over 4,000 events in 16 years, steaming some 600,000
  litres of milk and using 95,000 kilos of coffee.
- La Marzocco trained and authorised dealers, maintaining a fleet of more
  than 40 La Marzocco machines; wholesale support includes training at the
  roastery and an engineering team.
- Publishes an "A to Z of Coffee" blog series ("B is for Balance", "A is for
  Acidity", …).

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper only walks the **filter** and **espresso** product categories;
  gift sets, subscriptions, tea/chai, treats and equipment never enter the
  catalogue, so the Kissaten rows are strictly the filter/espresso
  selections.
