# 96B Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

96B Coffee is a Saigon-based Vietnamese cafe, roastery, coffee education centre,
green-bean producer and exporter. Its Wix site says the project was founded in
2016, became 96B cafe & roastery after a 2020 renovation, and evolved into a
coffee company in 2024. The shop focuses on Vietnamese Liberica/Excelsa,
Robusta and Arabica and sells roast coffee, cold drinks and gear.

## Address

- Roastery: 96B Phan Ngu, Tan Dinh Ward, Ho Chi Minh City, Viet Nam. The site
  publishes this as the address of “96B cafe & roastery”.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- 96B says it works with Vietnamese coffee grown and processed in Vietnam and
  began producing a fermented natural lot with a small Da Lat farm in 2017.
  Its story describes later exclusive Liberica and Catimor lots and a 2024
  expansion into green-bean production and export. No price-paid-to-producer
  figures or formal cost breakdown was found on the consulted pages.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The roastery/café page lists opening hours of 08:00–18:00 every day. No
  online dispatch schedule, destination-specific shipping rates or free-
  delivery minimum was published on the consulted pages.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- 96B deliberately works with Liberica/Excelsa and Robusta as well as Arabica,
  and presents coffee education as a core activity: the site says its programme
  grew to ten workshops from 2022. The business also serves the coffees as
  espresso, drip and cold brew.
