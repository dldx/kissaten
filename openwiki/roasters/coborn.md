# Coborn Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Coborn Coffee is a London micro-roastery "roasted in London 〰️ sourced from
responsible growers around the world", on a Squarespace storefront. It was
founded by Kenny and Justin, born from a conversation in an East London pub
on Coborn Road that lent the roastery its name; per their site and Companies
House records, COBORN COFFEE LTD was incorporated in 2022. Justin Eniola
carries the self-styled title "Head of Mischief".

## Address

- London, United Kingdom — full roastery address not published on site

## Sourcing & Transparency

- **Every coffee comes from a named female producer or a majority
  women-owned farm** — the shop page tagline is "Sourced From Women Roasted
  For Everyone". The rationale: "Women make up the majority of the coffee
  production labour force but represent a shockingly small number of farm
  ownership."
- They acknowledge the economics: paying farmers well "whiles not making the
  coffee completely unaffordable" means they "feel the pinch a bit more".
- Producer detail on product pages is unusually rich — e.g. Rwamatamu's
  Tuzamurane mu Guhinga Kawa women's cooperative (Rwanda, started 2020),
  honeybee pollination of the Nyungwe-forest coffee lots, the Chacón family
  (Costa Rica) pioneers of honey/natural processing, and Paola Trujillo's
  Patio Bonito Pink Bourbon via Cafe Imports.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Tagline: "**Coffee for Everyone**" — the aim is consistently high-quality
  coffee that stays accessible to as many people as possible.
- Naming quirks: Rwanda "Lot 3" was renamed **"Court 3"** after Justin watched
  a game of tennis; **"Honeybee"** credits the native honeybees of the Nyungwe
  forest; **"Rugori"** ("Women's Crown") honours an all-women anaerobic lot.
- Product pages are laced with dry humour: "Recommended for espresso...but
  what do we know?"
- The site carries an **editorial interview series** ("What's brewing?")
  profiling people around the roastery — long-form conversations about music,
  tattoos and North London Cypriot life, not coffee marketing.
- Justin publishes the playlist he roasts to: "What Justin listens to whilst
  he roasts."
