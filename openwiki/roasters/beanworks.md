# Beanworks Coffee Roasters — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Beanworks ("Beanworks Coffee Roasters") was founded in **2005** by two friends,
**David Blair** and **Darren Thorpe**, in a small Derbyshire industrial unit. It
has relocated twice and now roasts on the **historic Courteenhall farming estate,
Northamptonshire**. The storefront is the live WooCommerce store
[thebeanworks.co.uk](https://thebeanworks.co.uk) (the checklist's `beanworks.co.uk`
is dead).

## Address

- Courteenhall farming estate, Northamptonshire — United Kingdom (the roastery
  moved there in 2022)

## Sustainability

The site's "Our impact" section is unusually comprehensive:

- **Half of company vehicles fully electric**; the team commutes by bike/carshare
- Green coffee shipped by **sea** (rather than air)
- Sugarcane decaf decaffeinated **at source**
- Sustainable energy suppliers; recyclable / CO2-neutral packaging
- Coffee chaff collected by estate farmers for compost — a nice
  roastery-adjacent loop
- ~75% of roastery waste recycled (target: 100%)
- **One Tree Planted partner**: one tree planted (per their site) per
  order over £30

## Roasting & Equipment

- Started on a **2nd-hand Italian Trabattoni roaster**, roasting by **bean colour**
  with no software at all — a striking contrast with today's setup
- Now roasts on **Giesen roasters with Cropster profiling software**
- **Head of Coffee Felix Gale** spent three years in Central/South America
  working with producers at farms and washing stations
- Team also includes Rob Lawes (roastery ops), Fran Burns (barista trainer) and
  Will Harvey (training)

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Product lines: **Core Collection**, the experimental **Forager Series**, new
  releases, decaf, and subscriptions.
- The "roasted to order" positioning and the emphasis on producer relationships
  (Felix Gale's field background) hint at a sourcing philosophy of directness.

## Scraping Quirks

- **Subscription products** are excluded: product pages whose titles carry the
  `* -subscription` suffix are dropped, along with the gift-subscription
  product.
