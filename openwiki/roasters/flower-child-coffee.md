# Flower Child Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Flower Child Coffee is an Oakland, California limited-release coffee project
founded and roasted by Prestin Myles. The Shopify shop focuses on clean coffees,
varieties, regions and producers, with seasonal coffees presented as fully
developed light roasts. The active catalogue is separate from an archive of
historical offerings.

## Address

- Oakland, California, United States — full roastery address not published on
  site.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Flower Child says it works closely with producing partners, keeps compensation
  high despite market fluctuation and pays a higher premium to reinvest in the
  livelihood and future development of producers and the supporting supply
  chain. It does not publish FOB or farm-gate figures on the consulted page.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The About page states: roasts are every Monday, shipped on Tuesdays, and orders
  should be placed by Sunday for the next Monday roast.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The stated aim is to showcase each coffee's harvest, terroir and varietal with
  clear expression. The shop describes its selections as coffees from advanced
  farmers and a limited-release project.

## Scraping Quirks

- The scraper reads only Shopify's `/collections/active-coffee/products.json`;
  the archive collection is intentionally not included. It canonicalizes
  collection-prefixed URLs and excludes subscriptions, drip bags, mystery
  coffees, bundles, pods, cascara and gift cards.
