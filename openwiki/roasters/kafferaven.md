# Kafferäven — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Kafferäven Rosteri & Import is a specialty roaster in Gothenburg, Sweden, run by Per and Susanna with Malvina. The company says it began in 2013 with two goals: to buy, roast and share the best coffee it could find from producers with whom it has close relationships, and never to compromise quality. Its Shopify shop focuses on single-estate coffees, blends, espresso, filter coffees, subscriptions and producer stories.

## Address

- Lilla Waterloogatan 2, Slakthus B, 415 02 Göteborg, Sweden — published for Kafferäven Rosteri & Butik.

## Sustainability

- Kafferäven says its coffee comes from producers it is proud to call friends and publishes a sustainability section in its shop navigation. The consulted homepage did not expose a measurable packaging, energy or reforestation programme, so no such claim is added here.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- The company describes close, long-term producer relationships and highlights coffees produced by women as a shop category. It also publishes a dedicated transparency page, but no current price-paid-to-producer figure was visible in the consulted page output.

## Schedules & Shipping

- Sweden: free shipping over 400 SEK. EU shipping is advertised at 150 SEK. Kafferäven says it roasts at the beginning and middle of the week and sends orders at the end of each week; most coffee is roasted to order. It says coffee tastes best 2–8 weeks after roasting and may be shipped up to two weeks after roast so it can rest.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The shop’s “Fermentation Project” is a curated set made with James Hoffmann and Lucia Solis. It also sells producer-led bundles and subscriptions whose origins and contents vary while remaining with producers the company says it trusts.
