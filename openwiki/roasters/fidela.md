# Fidela — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Fidela Coffee Roasters is a speciality roaster based on the North Coast of
Northern Ireland, founded by husband-and-wife team **Frank and Rachel**. The
coffee journey began in 2016 with four of Frank's family farms in **Nariño,
Colombia**; in 2019 they moved back to Rachel's native North Coast of Ireland
and opened the roastery. Everything they roast is direct-traded, 100%
traceable green coffee imported directly from their Nariño farms, sold
through a Shopify storefront at [fidelacoffee.com](https://fidelacoffee.com)
alongside subscriptions and home-brew courses.

## Address

## Sustainability

- Set up a **coffee laboratory at Origin in Nariño** (2017) where their
  Q-grader, Jairo, cups every sample farmers bring during harvest and gives
  personal feedback — the project has grown from 4 farms to many more
  smallholders in the region.
- The lab doubles as a training hub: courses on coffee processes, cupping and
  brewing to empower younger generations to keep working the farms.
- **La Fidela Cafeteria** — a farmer-run speciality coffee shop opened in
  October 2019 in the heart of the coffee community in Colombia, letting
  farmers taste their own coffee and understand the price a cup of
  speciality coffee commands.
- Publishes an Impact Report (2024/25 edition on the site).

## Sourcing & Transparency

- **Direct trade, end to end**: green beans are imported directly from the
  family's farms in Nariño; Frank travels to the farms regularly through the
  harvest season and the family is present at every step of the supply chain.
- Began in 2016 working with four family members to improve farm-level
  processing; first direct export (to the Netherlands) in 2017.
- Farm visits, farm-level process improvement and regular producer feedback
  are the core of the model — "from plant to cup" traceability on every bag.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- Education in both directions: they train farmers in Colombia on processes,
  cupping and brewing, and host baristas, coffee enthusiasts and North Coast
  visitors at the roastery — roasting fresh coffee on site during visits.
- **The Fidela Tourism Experiences** and coffee courses (home brew, home
  espresso, latte art) turn the roastery into a destination.
- "From plant to cup" is the recurring theme — telling the farmers' story is
  treated as part of the product.

## Scraping Quirks

- **AI reads the bag, not the page**: Fidela prints the tasting notes on the
  bag label, so the scraper downloads the main product image and passes the
  bag photo to the AI extractor as the visual input instead of a full-page
  screenshot (which pulls in related products and banners that contaminate
  tasting notes).
- **Slug-based non-coffee exclusion**: subscriptions, gift cards, capsules/
  pods, cold-brew cans, easy-pour, equipment and merch slugs are filtered
  from the `/collections/coffee` products.json feed.
