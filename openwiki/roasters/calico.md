# Calico Coffee — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Calico is a London café and in-house micro roastery where "coffee, design, and
storytelling come together", founded by a team with architecture and design
backgrounds who built the space in-house. Founder **Junchao Huang** placed 3rd
at the **2023 UK Brewers Cup** before opening Calico in 2025 (per their brand
story page; Calico Coffee Ltd was incorporated in December 2023 and a separate
Calico Roastery Ltd is registered at the same address, per Companies House).
The Shopify storefront at [calicocoffee.co.uk](https://calicocoffee.co.uk) is
heavy on showpiece lots — Panama Hacienda La Esmeralda and Kotowa Geishas,
Hacienda Barbara Pacamara, Kenyan AA and natural Kamavindi/Thunguri lots,
Ethiopian anaerobic naturals — organised into "Bright & Clean", "Complex &
Fruity" and "Calico Exclusive" collections, plus "(Calico Lot)" house
selections. Roasted in London and shipped to 30+ countries.

## Address

- Unit C, 101 Lower Marsh, London SE1 7AB, United Kingdom (per Companies House
  for Calico Roastery Ltd; the café's own "Get Direction" map pin sits by
  Waterloo Station on Lower Marsh)

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Beyond the café, the founder team "built close relationships with coffee
  producers and origin partners, focusing on green bean sourcing and transparent
  supply chains" — from carefully selected bulk greens to unique micro lots and
  customised processing projects, working across **Panama, Kenya, Ethiopia and
  Yunnan**.
- Competition culture is treated as internal development: staff are encouraged
  to compete, with the team noting "competition is never the final destination".

## Roasting & Equipment

- **ROEST P3000** — a hot-air, convection-driven production roaster for
  small-batch production "of up to 3 kg", chosen for "precision, control, and
  consistency"; Calico states they are "proud to be working with ROEST".
- **Stronghold S2** alongside it, dedicated to precision micro-batch roasting
  with per-lot tailored profiles.
- Roasting philosophy: "not about imposing flavor, but about shaping how coffee
  expresses itself, without replacing its identity".

## Schedules & Shipping

- Micro-batch roasting cadence: **orders placed by Tuesday are shipped the
  following Monday**, with UK delivery typically a further **3–4 working
  days**; all items ship with the most recent roast date.
- **Free UK delivery on orders over £24** (site announcement bar); other
  shipping is calculated at checkout. International customers are responsible
  for import duties and customs where not collected at checkout.
- Coffee rest guidance: at least **10 days** after roast, best within **2 weeks
  to 3 months** depending on coffee and brew style.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The name and design concept come from **calico — the plain untreated cotton
  fabric** — read through Kenya Hara's book *White*: "all colors and no color
  at the same time"; the brand pursues "simplicity, purity, and substance" on
  a blank-canvas aesthetic.
- Early sketchbook line behind the vision: "Sip into a world of flavors, where
  every corner tells a tale through art and coffee."
- Café trivia service: if you can't remember which of their single origins you
  had in the shop, they'll identify the closest match from your visit date and
  order via Instagram DM.

## Scraping Quirks

- **Duplicate product in the shop feed**: the catalogue contains both
  `kenya-kamavindi-aa-natural` and a near-identical `kenya-kamavindi-aa-natural-copy`,
  which can surface as near-duplicate beans in the catalogue.
- Product URLs returned by the collection feed sometimes carry collection
  paths/variant query strings; the scraper normalises them to the fixed
  `/products/<handle>` form before fetching.
