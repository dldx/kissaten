# Delirante — Roaster Profile

## Overview

Delirante is an Argentinian specialty coffee roaster based in Bariloche,
Patagonia. Its WooCommerce storefront sells whole-bean and ground coffee,
drip coffee, subscriptions and equipment; the brand also operates cafés in
Bariloche and elsewhere, which are separate from the roastery address below.
The site says it has roasted since 2016 and holds the SCA Professional Roaster
certification.

## Address

- Roastery: Bariloche, Patagonia — Argentina; the reviewed site publishes an
  address for a Bariloche café/location but does not identify it as the
  roastery address.

## Sustainability

- Delirante says its Loring roaster can use up to 80% less gas per kilogram than
  a conventional roaster by recirculating air and incinerating smoke before it
  leaves the chimney.

## Sourcing & Transparency

- Product information gives origin, producer, altitude and processing for
  individual coffees. For example, the current house blend lists Brazil,
  Colombia and El Salvador, with 40% natural and 60% washed processing. No
  price-paid-to-producer or cost-breakdown figures were published on the
  reviewed pages.

## Roasting & Equipment

- Delirante roasts on a Loring fluid-bed/convection roaster. Its site describes
  weekly quality control using colour measurement and group cupping, and says
  the profiles are developed from instrumental data and sensory evaluation.

## Schedules & Shipping

- The shop says coffee is roasted periodically and dispatched every day, with
  the aim that it arrives in under 15 days from roasting. Shipping within
  Argentina is listed as ARS 12,500, free above ARS 70,000; delivery is usually
  24–48 hours in CABA/GBA and 3–4 business days in the interior.

## Philosophy & Quirks

- The name “Delirante” is explained as deriving from the Latin *delirare*,
  “to leave the furrow”; the stated aim is to take drinkers outside their
  usual track. The FAQ also says its coffee contains no added sugar and that it
  does not sell capsules.
